\section{Recruitment unlocks, wagon ownership, and eligibility}
\AchievementSetup{61418}{I Don't Want Your Help}{\textbf{Missable.}
On this route, keep all random recruitment locked until the adult
Killer Panther reunion in Section 6.2. Defer the wagon purchase and
the recruitment steps below. Hero and Henry cover the Reinhart route,

for the wagon after Kitty rejoins, then begin the species collection.}

Random recruitment begins in the second generation. Buy the wagon from
Oracleberry's northwest merchant at night for 300 G and speak with Monster
Gramps in the southwest basement. Carry the Hero on recruitment trips and
develop his level as stronger regions open. Only eligible species can join.
The travelling roster holds eight members, including three active positions.
Wagon access varies by dungeon and story event.

\section{Battle resolution, enemy groups, recruitment chances, and exceptions}
Win the encounter and check the post-battle joining offer. In a mixed encounter,
finish the desired species last among the recruitable enemies. Defeated
monsters can join through ordinary victories, and called Healers can provide
additional opportunities. Escaping enemies supply no defeated candidate.
Expel removes enemies from battle, so use damage to defeat a recruitment target.
Scripted bosses and the Evil Mountain interior have recruitment restrictions.
Use the Demon World field and Bonus Dungeon for the corresponding late recruits.

Rarity varies by species and additional copies can be harder to obtain.
Difficulty descriptions are planning aids. An unsuccessful battle can be
followed by another eligible encounter. Short runs with a recovery stop keep
the search manageable. Preserve room through the storage service.

\section{Guaranteed story companions and random monster recruitment}
The Killer Panther joins after Lenoire in childhood. During the second
generation, use Bianca's Ribbon in the cave west of Kabochi to restore his
recognition and rejoin him. These are two stages of the same story companion.
The other 40 catalog species use random recruitment. Named human companions
follow their own story events or Ruida's recruitment service.

\section{Intelligence, direct commands, and improving obedience}
Use an intelligence value above 20 as the practical training target for
dependable orders. Check Status after level gains and after each Intelligence
Seed. Bianca's Ribbon raises intelligence for an eligible wearer. The Dancing
Jewel's short growth schedule makes permanent intelligence improvements useful.
Keep essential healing assigned to a reliable member while a new recruit develops.

\section{Monster Gramps: storage, retrieval, capacity, and releases}
The daycare holds up to 50 monsters. Sign Up deposits a companion, Pick Up
retrieves one, Check displays its status, and Part releases it permanently.
Deposited monsters stop gaining travelling-party experience. Check the
depository for belongings transferred during storage and re-equip retrieved
companions before combat. Use releases only after checking the species, level,
learned abilities, and retained equipment.

\section{Repeated species, default names, and roster organization}
Keep separate records for repeated species using the displayed personal name
and level. The catalog's name lists identify possible assigned names.
Additional names do not indicate new species. Check both the travelling party
and daycare when organizing duplicates. A second healer can supply extra MP,
and a duplicate physical attacker needs a second equipment set. Reserve
storage for distinct roles and scarce companions before collecting extra copies.

For the achievement milestones, record distinct species as they join and
check the achievement list at the first recruit and the 5, 10, and 16 goals.
Retain one of each distinct species in the travelling party or daycare
until the required milestones have registered. Organize releases afterward.
Keep the Kitty-first route protected until its separate reunion unlock.
The adult panther event has its own alert in 6.2.

\subsection{Port Selmi, Kabochi, Ruraphin, and Salabona}
Port Selmi's fields offer Magician and Dragon Pup. Kabochi supplies the
panther reunion. Ruraphin and the Mountain Village approaches offer Puppet
Man. Around Salabona, Wyvern supplies healing and breath attacks. The volcano
offers Bomb Crag and Dancing Jewel. Continue the ring objectives while
searching these regions, using town recovery between outings.

\subsection{Telepador, mountain routes, and Granvania}
Telepador's desert offers Big Eye, and the Medal King island offers Curer and
King Slime. Around Ned's Inn and the Granvania mountain route, seek Mad
Dragon, Minidemon, Messala, and Metal Babble. Granvania's fields and trial
approach offer Orc King. The upper Demon Tower offers Leaonar. Orc King's
Revive at 11 and Healus at 8 are particularly useful development targets.

\subsection{El Heven, Zenithia routes, and the sea}
The later sea offers Neileus. El Heven and the central-land routes offer Golem,
Eliminator, and Centaurus. The Sky Tower offers King Healer. The Forest of
Illusions offers Eigerhorn and Farewell Crag. Search the northern continent
and Emblem Cave routes for Blizzard Hawk and Soldier Bull. Keep Return and
Outside available for recovery trips during training.

\subsection{Demon World and Bonus Dungeon}
Search the Demon World fields for Attackbot, Great Dragon, and Gigantes.
Lionex and Springer can be sought on late routes and in the Bonus Dungeon.
Hellbattler is a Bonus Dungeon recruitment objective. Search outside Evil
Mountain for eligible field encounters, and use the Bonus Dungeon for further
late recruitment after clear-state access opens.

\section{Level caps, training priorities, and late-game replacements}
Short-cap companions mature quickly, while level-99 species have long growth
schedules. The Dancing Jewel caps at 7, Metal Babble at 8, and Orc King at 30.
Train for a useful ability: Curer's Revive at 23 and Healusall at 30,
Wyvern's Healus at 18, King Healer's Barrier at 7 and Revive at 10,
Golem's Meditate at 20, and Great Dragon's Sparkling Breath at 30.
Compare present HP, agility, equipment, and MP before replacing a developed
companion. The next catalog keeps the maximum-level and learning fields separate.

\section{Rare recruits, metal companions, and realistic collection planning}
Attackbot, Metal Babble, Farewell Crag, and Hellbattler are extended-search
objectives. Great Dragon and King Healer can also require repeated visits.
Choose a fixed number of encounters per outing and recover afterward.
Metal Babble combines very high defense with low HP, so protect it while
training. The recruitable metal species is Metal Babble. Ordinary Metal Slimes
and Metal Kings supply experience during collection routes. Save after a valuable joining event,
retain a functional story team, and use daycare capacity to preserve rare recruits.
