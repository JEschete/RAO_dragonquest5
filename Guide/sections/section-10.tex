\chapter{Third Generation: The Demon World and Ending}
\label{written-finale}

The family is together again, and the search for Martha leads
through the boundary she has guarded for so many years. Carry
the three rings north, prepare the whole travelling roster,
and follow her voice into the Demon World.

The journey ahead brings the strongest purchasable equipment,
new monster companions, and a long climb through Evil Mountain.
Keep the party's recovery tools distributed among available
members and preserve enough resources for the final confrontations.

\section{Water Shrine revisited and the three-ring portal}

\subsection*{The northern silver door}

Return to \textbf{El Heven}, board the ship, and sail north
into the Water Shrine. Follow the main channel toward the
\textbf{northern branch} that was sealed during the earlier
town approach.

Carry the \textbf{Final Key} with an active member and open
the silver door. Continue into the goddess chamber with the
\textbf{Ring of Flames}, \textbf{Ring of Water}, and
\textbf{Ring of Life} available in the party's inventories.

\subsection*{The three goddess statues}

Approach each statue and use its ring through the owner's
Item command. Place the rings as viewed from the entrance:
\begin{description}
  \item[Left statue:] Ring of Flames.
  \item[Right statue:] Ring of Water.
  \item[Central statue:] Ring of Life.
\end{description}

When all three are placed, the passage opens beyond the
statues. Walk toward the northern portal to cross into
the Demon World.

\subsection*{Martha's gift at the arrival shrine}

As the party leaves the small arrival shrine, Martha speaks
again. She recognizes how much the Hero has grown and gives
him the \textbf{Sage's Stone} to support the journey.
Finish the conversation and collect the Stone before leaving.

Used as an item in battle, the Stone provides repeatable
\textbf{group healing without an MP cost}. Assign it to an
active member whose turn can handle recovery while the other
two attack or provide support. Stronger healing and revival
remain useful alongside its regular recovery action.

The \textbf{blue portal} returns the party to the ordinary
world when supplies, equipment, or recovery require a trip
back. Keep its location in mind while establishing a new
base in the eastern town.

\begin{routecheck}{The Demon World crossing}
\begin{description}
  \item[Water Shrine:] Final Key used at the northern silver door.
  \item[Goddess chamber:] Flames left, Water right, and Life center.
  \item[Arrival shrine:] Martha's conversation finished and
    Sage's Stone collected.
  \item[Party:] Stone assigned to an active recovery user and
    the return portal identified.
\end{description}
\end{routecheck}

\section{The Demon World overworld: routes, encounters, and recruitment}

\subsection*{The eastern bridges and rocky passages}

Leave the arrival shrine and travel \textbf{east across the bridges}.
Follow the passages between the mountain ridges until the town of
\textbf{Shahanna}, also called Jahanna, comes into view.

The field enemies make elemental protection and regular recovery
important immediately. Great Dragons have powerful breath attacks.
Bazuzus and Blizzard Hawks add breath or offensive magic, and enemy
Attackbots can strike twice in a round. Concentrate damage on a
dangerous member of the group and use the Sage's Stone when several
companions need recovery.

The wagon is available on the field. Rotate injured or depleted
members out for prepared reserves, and keep another recovery action
ready when a group can inflict more damage than the Stone restores.
Reach town before committing to a long training circuit.

\subsection*{Late recruitment choices}

The Demon World fields offer \textbf{Great Dragon},
\textbf{Attackbot}, and \textbf{Gigantes} as recruitment
prospects. Use ordinary field encounters for the search and assess
the new member's equipment, intelligence, and current abilities
before assigning it an essential role.

Update the wild-recruitment tally after each successful recruitment.
Count unique species across the journey and count duplicates of a
species once. Include the earlier field recruits when checking this
total. Save new recruitment progress during the next town visit.

\Achievement{61417}{Monster Collector}{10}{Recruit sixteen unique monsters}{
Check the running species tally during these field recruitment trips.
Confirm the unlock when sixteen unique wild monster species have been
recruited. This threshold may already have been reached on an earlier
route, so check the existing achievement status too.}

A Great Dragon begins with Flame Breath and learns
\textbf{Violent Flames at 10}, \textbf{Freezing Breath at 15},
\textbf{Burning Breath at 20}, and \textbf{Sparkling Breath at 30}.
Its breath attacks make useful group coverage as it develops.

Attackbot has a \textbf{level-30 cap} and a physical role.
Gigantes has a \textbf{level-7 cap} and also develops as a
physical attacker. Their equipment and immediate strength determine
how well they fit the current roster. Keep experienced healers
and support users ready while training a new recruit.

The established companions remain suitable for completing the game.
Choose recruitment as a preparation option, then continue once the
active roles and reserve recovery are strong enough for the mountain.

\AchievementSetup{61411}{Heroic Trainer}{Use manageable Demon World
field encounters to develop the wild-recruited roster for its own
Mildrath run. Train Golem or another physical attacker alongside a
King Healer or developed Slime Knight with the needed recovery spells.
Inspect their HP, MP, equipment, and healing after training, and save
that progress before the final climb. Prepare every intended active
monster and reserve for both consecutive forms.}

Shahanna's residents explain that Martha's sacred water protects
their home. Several inhabitants remember life under the Demon Lord
and describe the freedom her power has given them. Their gratitude
connects this last refuge with the mother the Hero is searching for.

Use the inn and saving service, arrange the roster with
\textbf{Monster Gramps}, and visit the \textbf{Depository}
before spending the carried gold. Open the magic-locked doorway
to the northeastern weapon shop with the available key.

The Hero's Dragon Staff and Royal Cloak, the Son's Zenithian set,
and the party's earlier healing items already provide a substantial
equipment foundation. Spend first on a vulnerable active member
or a reserve whose intended job needs stronger protection.

Return to the fork, go west, and take the northern stairs

Follow its passage left and take the stairs to Martha.

\subsection*{Martha and Papas}

Two \textbf{Dark Shamans} guard the Hero's mother.
Restore the active party before approaching and defeat them
with focused attacks and ordinary recovery.

Martha recognizes her son and welcomes the reunion she has
imagined through her years of captivity. She asks the family
to leave while she offers her life to seal Mildrath away.
His power interrupts the prayer and strikes her down.

As she makes her final plea, Papas's voice reaches her.
His spirit appears, and Martha joins him. They entrust the
continuing struggle to their children and promise to watch
over them before departing together.

Finish the scene.

\subsection*{The five-move sliding-room solution}

Eight rooms occupy a three-by-three grid. Initially the
\textbf{bottom-center space is empty}. Stepping on a
room's central tile moves that room into an adjacent empty
space. Follow the connecting paths between moves.

Starting from the initial arrangement, use this complete
left-side route:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Enter the \textbf{bottom-left room} and activate its
    central tile to move it \textbf{right}.
  \item Enter the \textbf{middle-left room} and move it
    \textbf{down}.
  \item Enter the \textbf{top-left room} and move it
    \textbf{down}.
  \item Enter the \textbf{top-center room} and move it
    \textbf{left}.
  \item Enter the \textbf{middle-center room} and move it
    \textbf{up} into the newly opened northern position.
\end{enumerate}

Use the northern door from the final position and follow
the passage toward the guardian chamber. If the rooms were
moved earlier, restore the initial bottom-center vacancy
and original room arrangement before following this sequence.

\section{Mildrath's first form: tactics and summoned enemies}

\subsection*{Establishing the three active roles}

Enter the dark chamber and speak to \textbf{Mildrath}.
His first form begins the final battle sequence.
The wagon is available, so keep the wider roster ready
to replace an injured fighter or exhausted support user.

For either restricted attempt, keep every active member and every
replacement within the prepared eligible pool through both forms.
Use the family recovery plan for a family run and the monster recovery
plan for a monster run, with the mandatory Hero inactive throughout
the latter attempt.

Start with \textbf{Barrier} for breath protection,
\textbf{Increase} for physical defense, and
\textbf{Bikill} on the principal attacker when the
party's HP allows the support turns.

A practical three-member group has a strong physical
attacker, a recovery user, and a flexible support or
second attacker. The Hero and Son, when eligible for the
prepared attempt, can alternate healing and physical work.
A well-equipped monster, when eligible
for the prepared attempt, can take the
damage role while another companion operates the Stone.

\subsection*{Handling summons and clearing effects}

Mildrath can summon \textbf{Attackbots and spellcasters}
beside him. Watch the added enemies' actions and concentrate
damage on a summon when it threatens recovery or prolongs
the fight. Resume pressure on Mildrath after controlling
the dangerous group.

His strong spells and cold breath call for regular group
healing. Use the \textbf{Sage's Stone} on appropriate
rounds, with stronger healing when the party needs more
HP than its usual recovery provides.

When \textbf{Freezing Rays remove support effects},
renew Barrier and the useful attack boosts. Decrease can
improve physical damage when it succeeds. Bring a fresh
support member forward if the first one has spent too
much MP to maintain the protection.

Treat Bounce as a deliberate spell-defense action within
the chosen healing plan. Item recovery remains useful
when arranging reflection and the party's spell actions.

\subsection*{Keeping resources for the transformation}

Preserve sufficient MP, revival supplies, and strong group
healing for the next fight. Use efficient physical damage
and repeatable item recovery while the current group is
stable, then spend stronger recovery when survival needs it.

After this form falls, Mildrath reveals his greater power
and transforms. The second confrontation follows directly.
Keep the party prepared for its opening attacks.

\section{Mildrath's second form: healing, defenses, and sustained damage}

\subsection*{Breath protection and stronger recovery}

Apply \textbf{Barrier} promptly in the new battle.
The stronger form uses devastating breath alongside
\textbf{Explodet} and physical pressure. Elemental and
spell protection on the equipment help the party survive
while the support effects are rebuilt.

Give group recovery a regular place in the turn plan.
The Stone's user can support ordinary damage, while
the Hero, Son, or another prepared healer, when eligible
for the selected attempt, handles a severely injured member.
Rotate in King Healer for
\textbf{HealUsAll} when the living roster needs a
complete HP refill.

Use that King Healer rotation only in an unrestricted or monster-only
run. In the family-only run, maintain the Son's healing and the wife
or Daughter's item recovery, with replacements drawn from the family.

An available reserve can replace a weakened active
member while the recovery role stays covered. Revive
a fallen companion when the surviving group can afford
the action and has enough protection for the next attacks.

\subsection*{Dispelling, reflection, and Meditation}

Mildrath can remove the party's magical enhancements.
After the clearing wave, restore Barrier first when
breath pressure threatens the group, then reapply Bikill
to the active fighters during safe turns.

Restore Increase after defense-lowering actions when
physical damage requires it. Counter Mildrath's defensive
boosts with an available reduction or the Zenithian Sword's
dispelling item action.

Watch for his \textbf{Bounce}. Maintain physical damage
while the reflection is active and keep offensive spells
away from a reflected target. Support spells directed
toward the party and carried recovery items remain part
of the chosen battle plan.

His \textbf{Meditation restores a substantial amount of HP}.
Keep protected, supported fighters dealing sustained
damage between recovery turns. Renew their equipment
and support assignments as members rotate through
the active positions.

\subsection*{Finishing the battle}

Use MP-restoring supplies on a caster before the reserve
becomes too low for essential healing or protection.
Let another member handle the item turn while the regular
recovery user continues supporting the party.

Keep enough living members ready to cover damage and
recovery through the final rounds. Steady attacks,
renewed protection, and the full roster's healing tools
bring the confrontation to its conclusion.

\section{Ending tour, credits, clear-state behavior, and postgame access}

\subsection*{Zenithia and the homeward tour}

After Mildrath is defeated, the family appears before
the \textbf{Master Dragon in Zenithia}. He thanks
them for restoring peace and offers to escort them home.
Visit the castle's residents, then meet him at the
entrance to begin the tour.

At each destination, finish the family conversation,
visit all the familiar residents, and leave when ready
for the next stop:
\begin{description}
  \item[El Heven:] The elders remember Martha and welcome
    the family she protected. The old couple reflect on
    Papas's first visit to their village.
  \item[Reinhart:] Henry and Maria celebrate with the twins.
    Speak to Maria about Joshua's final message, then visit
    Dale and the other castle residents.
  \item[Santa Rosa:] The village has been restored. Visit
    its familiar homes and residents before continuing.
  \item[Salabona:] Ludman's household welcomes the family.
    Complete the conversations suited to the chosen marriage.
  \item[Mountain Village:] Visit Duncan and finish the
    family's reunion there before the last flight home.
  \item[Granvania:] Speak to the waiting residents and
    follow Sancho into the throne room.
\end{description}

With \textbf{Bianca}, Duncan is delighted to see his
daughter and grandchildren together. In Salabona,
Andy and Flora offer their congratulations.

With \textbf{Flora}, Ludman celebrates his role as
the legendary warrior's grandfather. Speak to her
mother for the conversation about Flora's adoption
and her place in their family. In the Mountain Village,
Bianca looks forward to friendship with Flora.

The Granvania celebration brings the Hero, his wife,
and their children to the royal seats. The family
dance and the voices of Martha and Papas close the
journey as Zenithia rises and the credits begin.

\subsection*{The final screen and resumed save}

Let the complete credits finish and \textbf{wait for
the screen reading The End}. Reset after that screen
appears, return to the title menu, and load the save
used for the completed journey.

Ordinary adventure progress resumes from the saved
state before Mildrath's defeat, with access to the
Bonus Dungeon available. The post-treasure save preserves
the final equipment for the continuing adventure.

\subsection*{The Bonus Dungeon entrance}

After the completed ending, load that save and cast
\textbf{Return to Evil Mountain}. From the field
arrival point, walk \textbf{directly south into the
swamp} to reach the Bonus Dungeon entrance.

Recover and save before exploring its deeper rooms.
The full treasure route, new companions, and Estark
confrontation continue in the next section.
