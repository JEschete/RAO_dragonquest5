\section{Bestiary fields: names, HP, rewards, drops, behavior, and locations}
The catalog gives enemy HP, EXP, gold, and a possible item drop. Drops are random unless a scripted event explicitly gives an object. R marks a recruitable species. P marks the story Panther encounter. These flags describe recruitment eligibility, with encounter restrictions discussed below. A question mark leaves a conflicting statistic unspecified. Alternate spellings are normalized, including Balloon, Dark Shaman, Dead Emperor, Minidemon, Mechaban, and Springer. The encounter indexes locate the enemies without repeating their statistics. Exact encounter probabilities, resistance percentages, and drop odds are unconfirmed.

\section{Mimics, trapped chests, summoned enemies, and fixed encounters}
Man-Eater Chest has 90 HP, Mimic 400, Demon Jar 200, and Urnite 500. Each can drop a Small Medal. A monster in a chest or jar is a battle reward opportunity. Its item is separate from the route's guaranteed treasure. Demon Tower, Boble, Evil Mountain, and Bonus Dungeon contain such encounters. Carry instant-death safeguards and revival supplies before opening containers on long routes.

Summoning can add Healers in the Southern Tower, Curers at Demon Tower's switch guards, Golems in Boble, and dangerous reinforcements in the final battle. Kill a healer or reviver when it sustains the enemy formation. Slime fusion creates a King Slime encounter. Fixed fights also include Lenoire's statue and Candles, the slave guards, Reinhart's Dragon Pups, Port Selmi's bandits, tower sentries, Kandar's guards, Great Temple sentries, two Dark Shamans at Martha's plateau, and two Hellbattlers at the final treasure. Chapter~\ref{written-bosses} indexes their recovery demands.

\section{Recruitable flags and differences between enemy and companion data}
The R flag covers 40 randomly recruitable species. The reunion Panther is the additional story companion. Recruitment needs the wagon and the Oracleberry preparations. Select a route that permits recruitment, defeat the desired species through a suitable battle finish, and allow for repeated attempts. A species present as a fixed guardian or summoned reinforcement can have different eligibility from its normal field encounter. Evil Mountain's battles are unsuitable for planning recruit attempts, with the Bonus Dungeon providing Hellbattler, Lionex, and Springer opportunities.

Enemy HP, actions, drops, and rewards describe the opponent. Companion growth, learned skills, equipment, and maximum level have their own data. In particular, an enemy Attackbot can act twice without establishing two actions for an allied Attackbot. A hostile healer's current spell use does not establish a companion's learning level. The ordinary Killer Panther and reunion encounter also have different purposes. Use the companion development reference for skills and the verified eligibility sets in Chapter~\ref{written-equipment} for gear.

\section{Boss entries and variant encounters}
Boss Ghost, Zaile, Snow Queen, Imposter, Lava Savage, Kandar, Orc Lv. 20, Wyvern Lv. 35, Jami, Buorn, Gonz, Boble Gema, Ramada, Ivol, both Mildrath forms, and Estark have dedicated fight notes in Chapter~\ref{written-bosses}. Childhood Gema is scripted. The reunion Panther is resolved with the Ribbon. Kandar Henchmen recur at guarded treasures. Ordinary Orc and Wyvern statistics are separate from their tower variants. Boss Garm is an ordinary encounter despite its name. Keep the script's reward chest separate from a random battle drop.
