\chapter{First Generation: Childhood}
\label{written-childhood}

The childhood journey begins with Papas close at hand. Townspeople remember
him, sailors speak warmly of him, and his strength makes the roads feel
safe. The Hero's first independent adventures grow out of ordinary village
life: a missing herbalist, a sick friend, and Bianca's determination to help
an animal in trouble.

Take time to explore the houses and speak with the people along the way.
The early finds have immediate value, and the shops offer enough choices
to shape how comfortably the young party handles its first dungeons.
Free resting places make it easy to prepare a little at a time.

\section{Birth scene, the opening ship, and Vista Port}

\subsection*{A name and a voyage}

Choose the Hero's name when starting the adventure. The opening scene
shows Papas waiting anxiously for news of a birth. When he reaches Martha,
the conversation turns to their new son and the name she has chosen.
The scene closes as Martha becomes unwell and Papas calls to her.

The Hero then wakes in a ship's cabin with his father. Papas suggests
some fresh air, and the deck becomes the first place to explore freely.
The crew's conversations establish a little of their history with Papas.
The captain remembers an old kindness, the sailors know that father and
son are travelling together, and the cook has his own opinion about Papas'
cooking.

\subsection*{The cabin's Medical Herb}

Search the dresser in the cabin where the Hero wakes to obtain a
\textbf{Medical Herb}. Keep it for the first independent trip into a cave.
The barrels around the ship are empty, and the chests in the lower crew
quarters are locked during this voyage.

Walk around the deck and speak to the sailors, including the man at the
helm. Visit the captain's cabin and the lower rooms with the cook and
crew. After the conversations, the ship reaches Vista and the captain
asks the Hero to fetch Papas.

Return to the cabin, speak to Papas, and follow him onto the deck.
When he asks whether the drawers have been checked, confirm after collecting
the herb. Father and son disembark, and the ship departs.

\subsection*{Vista Port and the first battle}

At the port, Papas stops to speak with a friend and leaves the Hero free
to wander nearby. The woman upstairs remembers an earlier visit by Papas
and a small child. Explore the building and then leave through the port's
exit when ready to continue.

Three Slimes attack as the Hero steps outside. Choose Attack and let the
encounter play out. Papas arrives during the battle and quickly takes
control of the situation. He heals the Hero afterward and leads the way
to Santa Rosa.

Any encounters on that escorted journey are handled with Papas in the
party. His post-battle healing keeps the Hero ready for the next steps.
Save the Medical Herb for the time when the Hero is exploring alone.

\section{Santa Rosa: Sancho, Bianca, supplies, and the missing herbalist}

\subsection*{A homecoming}

Papas' arrival sets the village in motion. A guard runs ahead with the
news, old acquaintances greet him, and Sancho welcomes both travellers
into his house. Bianca and her mother are visiting while seeking medicine
for Duncan, who is ill at home in Alcapa.

Bianca takes the Hero upstairs and tries to read a book about the sky.
After her mother calls her away, search the room's dresser for a
\textbf{Leather Hat} and equip it on the Hero. This is the first useful
piece of free defensive equipment.

Downstairs, Papas announces that he has business elsewhere. Sancho offers
a place to rest, giving the Hero a convenient recovery point for his
first solo adventure.

\subsection*{Santa Rosa's household finds}

Collect these objects while looking around the village:
\begin{description}
  \item[Leather Hat:] The upstairs dresser in Sancho's house, in the room
    where Bianca reads to the Hero. Equip it immediately.
  \item[Medical Herb:] The dresser on the ground floor of Sancho's house.
  \item[Strength Seed:] The pot in Sancho's basement.
  \item[Medical Herb:] The dresser upstairs in the weapon shop.
\end{description}

The Strength Seed permanently improves strength. The Hero is a useful
recipient because he will be doing the attacking during the cave rescue.
Using it now gives that improvement time to contribute to the early fights.

Together with the ship's herb, the two village herbs provide a small
recovery supply. Sancho's free rest makes it practical to keep those items
for occasions when the Hero is several floors from safety.

\subsection*{Learning what has happened}

Speak with the villagers and visit Bianca's mother at the inn. The man
who went into the nearby cave to gather medicinal herbs has failed to
return. The household in the mountain north of Sancho's home also has
news of the missing herbalist.

Papas is occupied, and the Hero remains within Santa Rosa at this point.
The cave entrance lies on the west side of town, near the church. Rest
with Sancho, save at the church, and approach the entrance with the new
weapon equipped and the herbs available.

\section{Santa Rosa Cave: rescue route, treasure, and early training}

\subsection*{The first independent expedition}

The cave is close enough to home for the Hero to prepare in short trips.
Spend some time fighting near the entrance, then return to Sancho whenever
HP runs low. The gold contributes toward a better weapon, and the early
levels give the Hero a little more room to survive an unlucky attack.

Level 4 is a useful first target because the Hero learns Heal. Level 4 or
5, a purchased weapon, and the Leather Hat make a comfortable starting
point for the rescue. Keep the Medical Herbs available as a second form
of recovery, especially once the Hero is two floors below the village.

Slimes and the small creatures near the entrance provide gentle early
training. Deeper encounters include Drakees, Horned Rabbits, and
Whackamoles. The latter can land a critical hit worth roughly 10 HP at
this stage. Heal promptly when the Hero's remaining HP approaches that
amount, and check his condition after each battle.

\subsection*{The entrance floor and its Medical Herb}

From the entrance, follow the passage north. Take the first branch to
the west and open the chest for a \textbf{Medical Herb}. Return to the
main passage and continue north until the route divides around the water.

The eastern side has a boat beside an island with stairs. The rescue
route follows the western passage, where another staircase leads down
to the first basement. This is a convenient point to turn back for a
rest if the entrance encounters have already used much of the Hero's HP.

\subsection*{50 G and the Leather Shield}

On the first basement floor, take the passage toward the southeast of
the stairs and collect \textbf{50 G} from its chest. Return to the stairs
and follow the western route past the warning sign. The northward branch
leads to the chest containing a \textbf{Leather Shield}.

Equip the shield immediately. Its protection helps throughout the rest
of the cave and remains useful during the next journey. The chest's
location also makes the shield a worthwhile goal for a short supply trip
before attempting the deepest floor.

From the shield route, continue south through the floor. At the lower
junction, the western passage leads to the stairs down. The nearby eastern
branch ends locally. Descend once the Hero is healthy enough to continue.

\subsection*{Freeing the herbalist}

On the second basement floor, follow the passage east and north. Continue
past the first eastern side branch until the trapped man comes into view.
A boulder pins him to the ground. Walk into the rock from the upper side
to push it away, then speak to him.

\Achievement{40046}{The Lost Herbologist}{4}{Free the trapped herbologist}{Push
the boulder away from above and speak to the freed man to finish the rescue.}

The herbalist thanks the Hero and hurries back to deliver the medicine
needed by Duncan's household. His relief gives this first expedition a
satisfying conclusion, and there is still a useful chest nearby to collect
before climbing home.

Continue north from the rescue spot and open the chest for
\textbf{Wayfarer's Clothes}. Equip them on the Hero. Together with the
Leather Hat and Leather Shield, this gives him a practical set of defenses
assembled largely through exploration.

Return toward the place where the herbalist was trapped. A passage to
the east and southeast leads to a friendly Slime. Speak to it for its
searching advice, then retrace the route to the basement stairs.

\subsection*{Treasure check and the trip home}

Before leaving the cave, confirm these four collections:
\begin{routecheck}{Santa Rosa Cave collections}
\begin{description}
  \item[Medical Herb:] The entrance-floor chest on the first western
    branch of the northward passage.
  \item[50 G:] The first-basement chest reached southeast of the stairs.
  \item[Leather Shield:] The first-basement chest north of the route
    past the warning sign.
  \item[Wayfarer's Clothes:] The second-basement chest north of the
    herbalist's rescue spot.
\end{description}
\end{routecheck}

Climb back through the two staircases and return to the village. Rest
with Sancho. In the morning, Bianca and her mother have received the
medicine and are ready to return to Alcapa. Papas offers to escort them
and invites the Hero along.

Agree to join the journey. The party travels under Papas' protection,
and his strength makes the road encounters comfortable. The Hero can
arrive in Alcapa with his new armor equipped, a useful weapon, and the
remaining herbs ready for the next adventure.

\section{Alcapa / Alkapa: Bianca's home and the captive panther}

\subsection*{Duncan's household and the inn's finds}

Papas takes the travellers straight to the inn, where Duncan is resting.
While the adults attend to him, the Hero has time to explore. Bianca joins
him as he leaves the room, and the town becomes the first place the two
children can wander together.

Search the upstairs dressers before spending money. The dresser on the
second floor contains a \textbf{Defense Seed}, and the one on the upper
floor contains a \textbf{Wing of Wyvern}. Use the seed on a companion
whose defense needs help, then check the resulting value. Keep the wing
available as a travel item.

Speak to the people around the inn and shops. A woman describes the
weeping heard at Lenoire Castle after dark. Near the town entrance, two
children are tormenting a small animal. Bianca insists they release it,
and their condition is an adventure at the haunted castle.

Return to Papas after exploring. Duncan's wife persuades the visitors to
stay overnight. Bianca wakes the Hero during the night, ready to follow
through on the promise and rescue the animal. Papas is asleep, and the
two children leave together.

\subsection*{Training with a free resting place}

The inn provides free recovery during this part of the visit. After a
rest, Papas has caught Duncan's cold and remains in bed. Speak to Bianca
near Duncan's room to arrange the next nighttime outing. This gives the
children time to train and improve their equipment before tackling the
castle.

Fight close to Alcapa while learning what the party can handle. Forest
groups can be considerably rougher than the nearby open ground. Enemies
may inflict sleep, obstruct spells, or drain MP. Return to the inn when
recovery runs low, then make the next purchase with the earnings.

Around level 7 or 8 is a comfortable goal for the castle expedition.
Bianca's Surround and defense-lowering spell become useful preparations
for its stronger opponents. Keep her MP available for those actions and
let the herbs handle some of the ordinary injuries.

\section{Lenoire Castle: entry, separated companions, darkness, and the torch}

\subsection*{Reaching the haunted castle}

Leave Alcapa during the nighttime outing. Follow the northward route and
the bridge around the intervening terrain toward the castle. The western
route around the mountains also connects with the approach. Arrive with
both companions healed and their equipment assigned.

The main door is locked. Walk around the eastern side and climb the
exterior ladder to the upper entrance. The gate closes behind the children,
and the route begins by descending through the castle.

In the bedroom below, skeletons rise from their beds as the children
approach the stairs. When the disturbance clears, Bianca has disappeared.
Continue carefully with the Hero alone until she can be found.

\subsection*{The first chests and Bianca's grave}

On the next floor, open the northern chest for a \textbf{Wing of Wyvern}.
The other chest is empty. Take the stairs or the floor opening down,
then leave through the southern doorway onto the exterior terrace.

There are two graves here, and a moan points toward Bianca's whereabouts.
Search the left grave to release her. She is shaken and impatient to
rejoin the adventure, and the party once again has both children available.

Return through the doorway to the room with bookcases. Speak to the ghost
there, and a bookcase moves aside to reveal stairs. Bianca can read the
message asking for the graves to be returned. Descend, enter the nearby
bedroom, and speak to Sophia, the former queen.

Sophia explains that she and Erik need help reclaiming their resting
places. Her request gives the strange rooms and threatening inhabitants
a purpose: the children must reach the creature controlling the castle.

\subsection*{Finding Erik and lighting the dark room}

Leave Sophia's room and take the stairs into the darkened floor. Follow
the walls to the small lit doorway at the far side. The door leads into
another room and a series of stairs descending toward the kitchen.

At the lower room, a male ghost moves away as the children approach.
Search the kitchen pot for a \textbf{Medical Herb} before following him
up the nearby stairs. Speak to Erik on the balcony and agree to help.
He mentions a torch left in the kitchen.

Return downstairs and search the kitchen pot for the \textbf{Torch}.
Bring it back to the dark room and use it through the Item command.
The light reveals enough of the floor to find the stairs in the northeast
corner. Take those stairs to continue toward the upper passages.

\subsection*{The Walking Statue and the second Medical Herb}

\AchievementSetup{40116}{The Stalker}{\textbf{Missable.} Defeat the
Walking Statue during this visit, before defeating Boss Ghost. Restore both

A statue begins following the children along the corridor. Examining it
starts the optional \textbf{Walking Statue} battle, required for
\textbf{The Stalker}. Its physical blows
can be heavy at this stage, so heal before approaching it if the party
is already injured.

Have Bianca use Surround, then her defense-lowering spell if available.
The Hero can attack while she supports the fight and uses herbs as needed.
Keep enough HP to withstand a strong hit. The battle awards experience
and gold and can provide a \textbf{Defense Seed} as its item reward.

\Achievement{40116}{The Stalker}{3}{Defeat the Walking Statue in Lenoire Castle}{\textbf{Missable.}
Examine and defeat the following statue before the Boss Ghost confrontation.}

Continue south to the stairs. In the pot room above, Erik indicates
the container that leads onward. Search the other pots first, including
the southern pot containing the castle's second \textbf{Medical Herb}.
Search Erik's pot to reach the next part of the castle.

\subsection*{Opening the front door and using the castle inn}

From the new room, take the western doorway and the stairs leading down
toward the lower hall. Walk south and open the castle's front door from
inside. This creates a much shorter way back into the building.

The surrounding rooms reveal the castle's unpleasant hospitality.
Skeletons are waiting for their feast, and a nearby ghost hints at a
hidden treasure. Return upstairs and follow the northward route toward
the inn and the throne room.

The castle inn restores the party, then leaves the children outside
the castle when they wake. With the front door open, walk straight back
through the lower hall and retrace the short route upstairs. This is a
useful recovery opportunity before the final confrontation.

Continue north past the inn. Follow the passage through the western side,
then the southern stairs and the route east into the throne room. Speak
to the cloaked creature. Its trap drops the children into the dining
area, where \textbf{four Demon Candles} attack.

Clear the candle group, heal, and return to the throne room. The creature
retreats onto the balcony. Follow it when both companions are ready for
the first full boss battle.

\subsection{Ghost-boss preparation and battle strategy}

The \textbf{Boss Ghost} has roughly 200 HP. It uses physical attacks,
offensive magic, and Sap to lower a companion's defense. A prepared pair
of children can handle it through steady attacks, useful support, and
prompt recovery.

Open with Bianca's \textbf{Surround} to hinder ordinary physical attacks.
Use her defense-lowering spell as well, then let the Hero keep attacking.
Bianca can add damage when recovery and support are already under control.

The Hero learns \textbf{Upper} at level 9. If it is available, use it to
restore protection when Sap has left a companion vulnerable. Keep the
party's HP high enough to handle the next attack while assigning that
support action.

Medical Herbs let Bianca heal during a round when the Hero needs to
attack or strengthen a defense. The Hero's Heal spell supplies another
recovery option. Check both companions after a damaging spell, and give
the most threatened member priority.

Winning gives \textbf{300 experience} to each participating companion
and \textbf{180 G}. The Ghost promises to restore the graves if spared.
Accept its surrender, and Erik and Sophia lead the children back to
their resting places.

\Achievement{40117}{The Haunted Housekeeper}{5}{Defeat the Boss Ghost in Lenoire Castle}{Defeat
Boss Ghost on the balcony and finish the surrender and graves scene. Check

\subsection{Golden Orb, the panther's names, and Bianca's departure}

The king and queen thank the children and finally find peace together.
A \textbf{Golden Orb} appears between their graves. Collect it and keep
it safe with the party's important possessions.

Before leaving Lenoire, check these finds:
\begin{routecheck}{Lenoire Castle collections}
\begin{description}
  \item[Wing of Wyvern:] The northern chest after Bianca disappears.
  \item[Empty chest:] The other chest on that same floor.
  \item[Medical Herb:] The kitchen pot reached while following Erik.
  \item[Torch:] The kitchen search after Erik explains where it was left.
  \item[Medical Herb:] The southern pot in the room containing Erik's
    hidden pot passage.
  \item[Defense Seed:] The possible item reward from the optional
    Walking Statue battle.
  \item[Golden Orb:] The gift appearing by the king and queen's graves
    after the Boss Ghost is defeated.
\end{description}
\end{routecheck}

Return to Alcapa. News of the children's success has spread by morning,
and Bianca demands that the two boys honor their promise. The little
panther joins the party. Choose a name from the suggestions Bianca offers,
accepting the one that suits the new companion.

The panther's early defense needs attention. A \textbf{Wooden Hat} from
Alcapa costs 120 G and makes a useful purchase before leaving. Check its
equipment and distribute remaining herbs while the shops are nearby.

Speak to Papas at the inn once the shopping and farewells are complete.
Transfer useful equipment and items from Bianca to the Hero or panther
before agreeing to leave. As the party departs, she gives
\textbf{Bianca's Ribbon} to the Hero and places it with the panther.
Papas then leads the travellers home.

\section{Santa Rosa revisited: Bella and the route to the faerie realm}

\subsection*{The herbalist's hidden gift}

Sancho has a letter waiting for Papas, and the Hero wakes after another
rest at home. The panther is now available for the next independent
outing. Before investigating the village's new disturbances, visit
the herbalist's house in the mountain north of Sancho's home.

The man rescued from the cave thanks the Hero and offers a gift.
Search his dresser for the \textbf{Handwoven Cape}. Equip it on the
panther, whose defensive equipment has become an immediate concern.
This household search is easy to overlook among the new conversations,
so collect it before following the next invitation out of town.

\Achievement{40277}{Reward for the Rescue}{2}{Acquire the Handwoven Cape}{Search
the rescued herbalist's dresser in his mountain house and collect the cape
before following Bella into the faerie realm.}

\subsection*{The traveller and the village mischief}

Speak to the traveller near the church. He asks to see the Golden Orb,
examines it, and returns it. Finish the conversation and his request
concerning Papas, then continue exploring the village.

Residents complain about objects going missing or being moved. At the
inn, a faint figure stands by the bar. Speak to her. She is surprised
that the Hero can see her and asks him to meet her in Sancho's basement.

Rest and save if needed, then go downstairs at Sancho's house. The visitor
introduces herself as \textbf{Bella} and admits to the tricks around
town. She has been trying to attract someone's attention because her
own village needs help.

Agree to accompany her. A luminous staircase appears in the basement.
Follow it with the panther to reach the faerie realm, where winter has
continued well beyond its proper season.

\section{Faerie Village and the Dwarves Cave: Thieves' Key knowledge}

\subsection*{Lady Powan's request and the village search}

Bella escorts the Hero to \textbf{Lady Powan}. The Flute of Spring has
been stolen, and the faeries need it to bring the season around. Agree
to recover it. Bella accompanies the Hero and panther, bringing battle
magic and healing to the three-member party.

Explore the village before setting out. The church beneath Powan's
meeting area offers saving, and the people around the inn explain where
the thieves have gone. Their hideout is the Ice Mansion to the north,
whose entrance requires the unlocking knowledge held in the western cave.

Search the pots beside the inn for \textbf{Holy Water}. Inside, speak
to the skeleton for the account of the dwarves and their Thieves' Key.
The dwarf on the bed runs the general store, and the armor merchant
provides another opportunity to improve the young party's equipment.

The inn restores the party and returns the Hero to Sancho's house.
Use the basement staircase to rejoin the faerie journey afterward.
This gives a familiar route back to recovery while training or shopping.

\subsection*{Preparing the panther and entering the western cave}

Train near the village until the panther can withstand the local groups
comfortably. Its early command obedience can be unreliable, so let the
Hero handle essential recovery. Bella contributes support during battle.
The Hero's level 11 \textbf{Healmore} is a valuable target for the coming
Ice Mansion fights.

Travel west across the snowy country to the Dwarves' Cave. Babble and
other troublesome creatures make poison treatment useful on the approach.
Inside, watch for enemies with disruptive magic or dangerous explosive
actions and keep the active members healthy.

\subsection*{The first gold chests and Zaile's grandfather}

Follow the entrance passage south to the warning sign. Turn west, then
take the northward branch into the chest room. Collect \textbf{25 G}
and \textbf{100 G} before returning to the main passage.

Continue south and enter the small room branching to the east. A dwarf
and a friendly Slime explain the theft. \textbf{Zaile} took the flute
because he believed Lady Powan had wronged his grandfather. The grandfather
asks the Hero to recover the unlocking secret sealed deeper in the cave
and help bring the young thief home.

Leave the room and follow the southern route to the stairs. On the next
floor, the nearby downward stairs and northern hole lead to a small area
occupied by another treasure hunter. Return from that detour and use
the western opening to reach the stairs into the large branching floor.
Keep clear of the poisonous ground where practical.

\subsection*{The Agility Seed and the unlocking secret}

Explore the eastern side branch on the large lower floor for an

Using the seed also frees its inventory slot for the discoveries still
ahead.

Return to the corridor and take the next eastern branch below that
chest route. Its stairs lead to the lowest chamber. A large locked door
stands beside the arrival area. Follow the passage north and open the
chest containing the \textbf{Secrets of the Key}.

The southern stairs reconnect with another branch of the floor above.
Retrace the route toward the entrance, speaking to the grandfather again
if desired. The five ordinary treasure finds and the learned secret are:

Return to Faerie Village, rest, and save. The cave's money can support

\section{Ice Mansion: sliding-floor route, Zaile, and the final boss}

\subsection*{The outdoor Intelligence Seed}

Travel north from Faerie Village, then around the mountains toward the
west to find the Ice Mansion. Before entering the front door, follow
the ice around the western side to the northwest chest. It contains an
\textbf{Intelligence Seed}.

Check the panther's intelligence before choosing a recipient. A permanent
increase can help an early companion become more reliable. The unlocking
ability from the cave opens the mansion's entrance.

\subsection*{Understanding the sliding floors}

Movement on the ice carries the party along the selected direction.
Walls, rocks, and the ends of the open runs provide stopping points.
Let each slide finish before choosing the next direction.

The holes in the entrance floor drop the party into a recovery basement.
Its stairs return to another part of the main floor. A separate basement
contains three treasure chests, so include that staircase in the route
before climbing to the upper level.

After the initial fall, climb the recovery basement's stairs. From the
return position on the main floor, use this sequence to reach the stairs
to the treasure basement:
\begin{quote}
Down, down, left, left, up, up, left, down, down, down.
\end{quote}

Each repeated direction represents another move along the sliding route.
If a hole returns the party to the recovery basement, climb back to its
stairs and begin again from the same position.

\subsection*{The three basement chests}

In the treasure basement, the upper-left chest holds \textbf{5 G},
the upper-right chest is \textbf{empty}, and the lower-left chest holds
a \textbf{Wing of Wyvern}.

From the treasure stairs, move left twice, up, right, and up to reach
the upper chests. From the empty chest, move down twice and left to reach
the Wing of Wyvern. Work back to the stairs after collecting the finds.

Back on the main floor, use the route up, right, right, up, and right
from the treasure-stair position to reach the stairs toward the upper
level. On that level, the route to Zaile is:
\begin{quote}
Left, up, left, up, left, left, down, right, down, left, down, right, up.
\end{quote}

Zaile stands near two chests. Heal the party before speaking to him.
The upcoming battles follow one another, so approach with enough MP and
items for both opponents.

\subsection*{Zaile and the Snow Queen}

Zaile has roughly \textbf{160 HP}. He can recover his own HP and drain
MP, but steady attacks from the Hero and panther make this the shorter
of the two fights. Bella's support can help reduce physical pressure.
Keep the party healthy as Zaile approaches defeat so that the next battle
begins in good condition.

The victory gives \textbf{105 experience} and \textbf{50 G}. Zaile
can also leave \textbf{Magic Water} as an item reward. The conversation
reveals that he has been misled about Lady Powan. The \textbf{Snow Queen}
appears, abandons her disguise, and attacks the party herself.

The Snow Queen has \textbf{600 HP} and presents a much longer fight.
Her physical attacks, cold magic, and breath require the party to maintain
recovery throughout the battle. She can also heal herself and gather
strength for a dangerous attack.

Let the panther contribute physical damage while the Hero divides his
turns between attacks and necessary recovery. \textbf{Healmore}, learned
at level 11, makes an injured companion much easier to restore. Bella's
healing and support help, and the Hero's herbs provide another answer
when the party needs several recovery actions close together.

\textbf{Upper} improves protection from physical blows. Use it when
the party has room for a support turn, especially for a vulnerable member.
Cold breath damages the group, so check all three companions after it
and recover the most threatened member first. When the Queen gathers
strength, prepare for the heavier blow with healing or a defensive action.

A defense-lowering effect can improve the Hero's and panther's physical
damage when it takes hold. Keep the recovery plan working while pressing
the attack. The Queen's 600 HP means the party needs enough resources
to sustain that rhythm through several rounds.

Winning gives \textbf{650 experience} and \textbf{200 G}, with a
possible \textbf{Wing of Wyvern} item reward. Speak to Zaile afterward.
He understands the deception and leaves to return to his grandfather.

\subsection*{The reward chests and the return route}

Open both chests near the battle site. The left contains a
\textbf{Boomerang}, and the right contains the \textbf{Flute of Spring}.
Both belong in the party's inventory before leaving.

\Achievement{61362}{The Sound of Birds}{5}{Retrieve the Flute of Spring}{After
defeating Zaile and the Snow Queen, open the right reward chest and collect
the Flute of Spring before returning to Lady Powan.}

If the Hero has been using a Copper Sword, the found Boomerang supplies
a useful group-clearing weapon. If he already owns one, keep the extra
safe until deciding how to use or sell the spare.

Check the mansion's collections:
\begin{routecheck}{Ice Mansion collections}
\begin{description}
  \item[Intelligence Seed:] The northwest chest outside the main door.
  \item[5 G:] The upper-left chest in the treasure basement.
  \item[Empty chest:] The upper-right chest in that basement.
  \item[Wing of Wyvern:] The lower-left treasure-basement chest.
  \item[Magic Water:] The possible item reward from Zaile.
  \item[Wing of Wyvern:] The possible item reward from the Snow Queen.
  \item[Boomerang and Flute of Spring:] The two chests beyond the bosses.
\end{description}
\end{routecheck}

Heal before retracing the ice route. Ordinary encounters can still occur
during the journey out, and the walk back to Faerie Village needs enough
recovery to keep the party safe.

\section{Flute of Spring, returning home, and childhood loose ends}

\subsection*{Bringing spring back}

Return to Lady Powan with the Flute of Spring. Complete any remaining
village shopping or searches before speaking to her, including the
Holy Water beside the inn if it is still waiting there.

Powan thanks the Hero and promises help if he ever needs it. Bella says
farewell, and the flute's music brings spring to the faerie realm.
Blossoms replace the long winter as the Hero and panther return to
Sancho's basement.

\subsection*{Bella's farewell and Papas at the church}

Upstairs, Sancho explains that the King of Reinhart has summoned Papas.
The Hero's father wanted to bring him along and has been looking for him.
Visit the inn for Bella's message in the guest register, then go to the
church.

Papas is there offering a prayer. Speak to him and record the adventure
with the priest. He will wait at the village entrance while the Hero
finishes his preparations.

Before meeting him, equip the new Boomerang if useful and check the
panther's Stone Fang and protective gear. Make sure the Handwoven Cape
has been collected from the herbalist's dresser and that important
possessions such as Bianca's Ribbon remain with the party.

The village's small errands have given the Hero a sturdy beginning:
free equipment, a rescued herbalist, a new companion, and experience
handling several quite different dungeons. Restock herbs if needed,
then meet Papas at the entrance for the journey to Reinhart.

\section{Reinhart Castle: Henry's disappearance and the kidnapping}

\subsection*{An audience with the king}

Papas leads the travellers east through the passage connecting the two
regions. The guard recognizes the royal summons and allows them through.
At Reinhart, the castle guards bring father and son directly to the king.

The king asks to speak privately with Papas, leaving the Hero time to
explore. The corridors are full of opinions about the royal family.
Prince Henry has a reputation for mischief, his younger brother Dale
has little enthusiasm for the throne, and the queen has strong ideas
about which child should inherit it.

Visit the princes' rooms and the kitchen. The cook explains that Henry
lost his mother while very young and has found little sympathy in the
new household. These conversations give the prince's pranks a little
context before the Hero is asked to become his companion.

\subsection*{The hidden Acorn of Magic}

Find the palace chapel in the eastern part of the lower castle.
The priest can record the adventure. Go into the room above the altar
and search the left dresser for an \textbf{Acorn of Magic}.

The Acorn permanently raises maximum MP. The Hero is a useful recipient
because his healing will support the next independent journey. Using it
now also leaves another inventory slot available.

The chest below Henry's room is \textbf{empty}. Another room contains
three chests behind a locked door. That treasury remains inaccessible
during this childhood visit, so collect the Acorn and continue exploring
the rooms that are open.

\subsection*{Henry's badge and the hidden stairs}

Return to the throne room once the conversations are complete. The king
explains that Papas is to protect Henry and asks the Hero to befriend him.
Papas waits outside the prince's room while the children talk.

Henry offers to make the Hero his personal goon and sends him to retrieve
a badge from the chest in the adjacent room. Search it, then return.
The chest is empty and Henry has vanished. Tell Papas and accompany him
back to the room, where Henry has reappeared in his usual place.

Repeat the badge errand when Henry insists. After his next disappearance,
search the stool or square where he was sitting. A concealed staircase
leads into the room below.

Henry is annoyed that his hiding place has been discovered, but the
conversation is interrupted by two men arriving through the side door.
They seize the prince and carry him toward a boat. Follow the scene,
then go upstairs and tell Papas.

Papas immediately sets off after the kidnappers. Follow him out of the
castle. He runs ahead in town, leaving the Hero and panther to learn
where the search has gone. A girl near the drawbridge mentions the large
cave to the northeast.

\section{Thieves Cave: Henry's rescue, Gema, and the scripted transition}

\subsection*{The northeast approach}

Follow the route around the mountains north and east of Reinhart, then
turn toward the swamp and the cave beyond it. Use the clear ground around
the swamp when approaching the entrance.

Slime Knights and other local enemies can hit hard, while Dancing Needles
can drain MP. Keep the Hero ready to heal and check both companions after
a difficult group. A return to town is sensible if the approach has
already exhausted the intended cave supplies.

Enter the first room and continue north. Papas is visible fighting
monsters on another part of the cavern. The route to him follows the
eastern passages around the intervening rooms.

\subsection*{180 G, the Antidote Herb, and the kidnappers}

Take the eastern path and descend the first downward stairs. Go north
through the doorway and open the chest for \textbf{180 G}. Return
to the junction and take the upper stairs east across the small bridge.

Follow the southern branch to the chest containing an
\textbf{Antidote Herb}. From there, go north into the kidnappers' room.
Their conversation connects the abduction with the queen and reveals
that Henry has been brought here to be sold.

Leave through the same doorway. Circle around the room's northern and
eastern sides, then follow the southern branch to the next chest.
It contains a \textbf{Strength Seed}. Use it on the Hero or panther
if that companion is the intended recipient.

Take the nearby western stairs and continue through the large door.
The prison can be seen along another branch, but the route to Papas
follows the western side and the southern doorway. Work back around
the cavern to reach the doorway beside his fight.

\subsection*{Papas rejoins the party}

Approach Papas and finish the conversation. He clears the monsters,
expresses pride in the Hero's courage, and joins the party for the rescue.
His attacks and post-battle healing make the following passages much
more comfortable.

This is also a useful opportunity for a little additional training.
Keep the Hero and panther healthy during each encounter and let Papas
handle much of the opposition. Any time spent here can strengthen the
young companions before the story advances.

The Hero learns Outside at level 14, but a magical force blocks its
use in this cave. Keep the walking route to the entrance in mind when
choosing whether to explore farther.

\subsection*{The two boat-access chests}

Step on the floor symbol to open the northern door, then go through
to the docked boat. Board it and sail west, then south to reach the
chest that was visible from an earlier passage. It contains a
\textbf{Monster Lure}.

Sail back north and follow the water east. Stop at the northern chest
for \textbf{Elven Medicine}. Keep this valuable recovery object safe
with the party's supplies.

Before entering the prison approach, check the cave's five chests:
\begin{routecheck}{Thieves Cave collections}

  \item[180 G:] The room north of the first downward staircase on the
    eastern route.
  \item[Antidote Herb:] The chest south of the upper stair-and-bridge route,
    near the kidnappers' room.
  \item[Strength Seed:] The southern chest reached by circling the

  \item[Monster Lure:] The chest reached by sailing west and south from

  \item[Elven Medicine:] The northern water-route chest reached before

\end{description}
\end{routecheck}

Finish the treasure searches and any desired training now. The childhood
collection checklist in the next subsection can be checked before moving
the boat east and north through the opening toward Henry's cell.

\subsection*{The rescue and the retreat}

At the prison, Papas forces open Henry's cell. The prince's first reaction
is bitter, and Papas reminds him that his father cares for him. As the
party prepares to leave, three monsters arrive.

Papas stays to hold them off and tells the Hero to take Henry to safety.
Board the boat and retrace the water route toward the entrance. Continue
through the earlier passages with Henry. The kidnappers' room is now quiet,
and the retreat leads to a new encounter at the exit.

\subsection*{The encounter with Gema}

\textbf{Gema} blocks the way out. This encounter advances through a
scripted defeat. Let the battle play out, keeping rare recovery items
for occasions where they can affect the result.

Papas reaches the children and defeats Gema's two followers. Gema then
threatens the Hero, forcing Papas to stop defending himself. Before his
death, Papas tells his son that Martha is still alive and asks him to
find her.

Gema destroys the Golden Orb and takes the Hero and Henry away.
The panther is left behind. Papas' last request remains with the Hero
as the childhood journey ends.
