\chapter{Dungeon Atlas, Puzzles, and Treasure Routes}
\label{written-dungeon-atlas}

\section{Atlas conventions: floors, entrances, exits, switches, and treasure}
Floor descriptions distinguish entrance level, upper rooms, basements, exterior ledges, and isolated return pockets. Screen connections in mazes can lead to different rooms from the apparent bearing. A \textbf{fixed-find set} includes placed chests, pots, dressers, ground searches, and event objects within the profile. Trapped containers and possible battle drops have their own entry. Doors require the listed key, learned unlocking secret, switch, or event. Use the corresponding walkthrough for the full route and battle plan.

\section{First-generation maps}
\subsection{Santa Rosa Cave, including the adult revisit}
\label{atlas-santa-rosa}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Entrance and two childhood basement levels, deeper boat-access basements in adulthood. Push the rescue rock clear. Adult upper-floor walking changes the corresponding obstruction below. Poisonous enemies and stronger lower groups require treatment and MP reserves. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Childhood Medical Herb, 50 G, Leather Shield, Wayfarer's Clothes. Adult 850 G, Agility Seed, Breastplate, Lamp of Darkness, Strength Seed pot, Leather Loincloth dresser, Zenithian Sword, Papas' Letter. The village Intelligence Seed belongs to the town search. \textbf{Encounters:} Lower-cave Hork recruitment and Metal Slimes. \textbf{Route:} 4.3 and 5.4--5.4.1 in Chapters~\ref{written-childhood} and~\ref{written-escape}.

\subsection{Lenoire / Linoire Castle}
\label{atlas-linoire}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Exterior climb to upper entrance, descending bedrooms and dark rooms, kitchen basement and throne approach. Front door opens from inside, grave search restores Bianca, kitchen Torch lights the dark room. Hidden pot passage and trapdoor redirect the climb. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Wing of Wyvern, two Medical Herbs, Torch, Golden Orb after boss. One upper chest is empty. \textbf{Encounters:} Optional Walking Statue can yield Defense Seed, Boss Ghost guards the event reward. \textbf{Route:} 4.5--4.5.2 in Chapter~\ref{written-childhood}.

\subsection{Dwarves Cave}
\label{atlas-dwarves}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Entrance branch, intermediate drop and stairs, large lower branching floor, lowest locked treasure room. Poison ground and explosive enemies call for recovery. Learn Secrets of the Key in the lowest north chest, then open the adjacent large door. \textbf{Fixed finds:} 25 G, 100 G, Agility Seed, Secrets of the Key, 120 G, Acorn of Life. \textbf{Route:} 4.7 in Chapter~\ref{written-childhood}.

\subsection{Ice Mansion}
\label{atlas-ice}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Outdoor chest, sliding entrance floor, separate recovery and treasure basements, upper boss floor. Learned unlocking secret opens entrance. Holes fall to recovery basement, wall stops determine slide direction. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Outdoor Intelligence Seed, basement 5 G and Wing of Wyvern, upper Boomerang and Flute of Spring. One treasure-basement chest is empty. \textbf{Encounters:} Zaile then Snow Queen, possible Magic Water and Wing battle rewards respectively. \textbf{Route:} Full slide sequences in 4.8, Chapter~\ref{written-childhood}.

\subsection{Thieves Cave / Reinhart Cave}
\label{atlas-thieves}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Upper bridges and kidnappers' rooms, lower passages and boat channels, prison landing. Papas opens Henry's cell. Finish collection before rescue and scripted exit confrontation. Poison enemies make Antidote useful. \textbf{Fixed finds:} 180 G, Antidote Herb, Strength Seed, Monster Lure, Elven Medicine. Last two require boat detours. \textbf{Encounters:} Kidnappers and the story-controlled Gema confrontation. \textbf{Route:} 4.11--4.12 in Chapter~\ref{written-childhood}.

\section{Reinhart, regional side quests, and marriage maps}
\subsection{Reinhart Castle basement}
\label{atlas-reinhart}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Boat entry beneath drawbridge, upper passage and lower prison, courtyard and castle treasury. Search pedestal control, eastern descent bypasses locked door, marked tile opens shortcut. Dale gives Reinhart Key upstairs. Treasury floor damages walkers. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Basement Steel Fang and Shell Hat. Connected key circuit adds Reinhart Key, 1,200 G, Acorn of Life, Half Plate Armor, storage-pot Small Medal. \textbf{Encounters:} Two fixed Dragon Pup guards. \textbf{Route:} 5.6 in Chapter~\ref{written-escape}.

\subsection{Southern Tower}
\label{atlas-southern}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Five floors, split fourth-floor branches and exterior drop. Maria's prayer opens entrance. Use northeastern first stairs, collect northeastern fourth-floor jar detour, cross top gap on far-left walkway line. Falls return to lower connections. \textbf{Fixed finds:} 650 G, Scale Shield, Acorn of Magic, Magic Water jar, Mirror of Ra. \textbf{Encounters:} Metal Slimes and knight-summoned Healers. \textbf{Route:} 5.7--5.8 in Chapter~\ref{written-escape}.

\subsection{Ruraphin South Cave}
\label{atlas-ruraphin}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Northern entrance corridor and lower southern-exit passage. Resolve soldier's Henry invitation, take southeastern chest branch, then western route to exit stairs. No key puzzle on the passage. \textbf{Fixed finds:} One Small Medal chest. Rumor Shrine well medal is outside this map. \textbf{Route:} 6.4 in Chapter~\ref{written-marriage}.

\subsection{West Kabochi Cave}
\label{atlas-kabochi}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Upper fork and campfire search, lower panther chamber. Follow the descending route with Bianca's Ribbon in an available inventory, use it during the panther encounter. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Small Medal near campfire, Stone Axe, Iron Helm, Papas' Sword at the panther's position. \textbf{Encounters:} One Man-Eater Chest with possible medal reward, story panther reunion. \textbf{Route:} 6.2--6.2.2 in Chapter~\ref{written-marriage}.

\subsection{Volcano Cave}
\label{atlas-volcano}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Entrance bridges, staff-and-helm floor, lower seed-and-gold floor, separate recovery basement and ring chamber. Stepguard covers walkable lava. Healing tile restores active HP and MP, rotate reserves through it. \textbf{Fixed finds:} 450 G, Staff of Antimagic, Iron Helm, Defense Seed, 1,500 G, Ring of Flames. \textbf{Encounters:} One Man-Eater Chest, three Lava Savages at ring, Dancing Jewel and summoned Curer prospects. \textbf{Route:} 6.6--6.6.1 in Chapter~\ref{written-marriage}.

\subsection{Waterfall Cave}
\label{atlas-waterfall}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Entrance descent, bridge rooms, drop-linked exterior ledge, deep eastern treasure basement and hidden ring room. Bianca opens river gate before entry. Pale shallow water is walkable, holes connect ledge detours to return stairs. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Robe of Serenity, Elven Medicine, underwater 1,200 G, Ring of Water behind falls. \textbf{Puzzle cue:} First drop and southern door reach robe, eastern stair spur reaches medicine, beach opening reaches ring. \textbf{Route:} 6.7--6.8 in Chapter~\ref{written-marriage}.

\section{Mountain crossing, royal trials, and rescue maps}
\subsection{Mountain Cave, west side}
\label{atlas-mountain-west}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Exterior ascent ledges, underground pot and rest rooms, upper cave to Chizod. Marsh and poisonous approaches favor Stepguard. Branch before resting for ring and medals. \textbf{Fixed finds:} 550 G, Ring of Prayer, three Small Medals, Hero's 5-point strength event from overnight stay. Flora's courier gives Water Flying Clothes on this route. \textbf{Encounters:} One Demon Jar in pot room. \textbf{Route:} 7.4 in Chapter~\ref{written-granvania}.

\subsection{Mountain Cave, east side}
\label{atlas-mountain-east}
\textbf{Floors and access:} High Chizod-side descent, exterior drops, third-floor return branch, lower stair spurs, upper return loop and final ledge. Drops and stairs serve different treasure branches. Re-enter behind lower medal ledge and climb to main third floor for 270 G. Walk east through apparent wall on upper Mimic floor for hidden staff. \textbf{Fixed finds:} 1,500 G, three Small Medals, cursed Rogue Armor, Leaf of the World Tree, 270 G, Bomb Stone, 1,600 G, Staff of Punishment, Wing of Wyvern, Stone of Life, Agility Seed. \textbf{Encounters:} Three Mimics, two Man-Eater Chests, Metal Babble and caster recruits. \textbf{Route:} 7.6 in Chapter~\ref{written-granvania}.

\subsection{Cave of Trials}
\label{atlas-trials}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Four-door entrance, reservoir basement, plate room, lowest pillar trial. Enter and exit far-right door, then second from left. Boulder below latch stops reservoir current, left bird plate opens stairs, search broken western pillar and step on new tile. Escape magic is restricted on the initial return. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Small Medal, Swordedge Armor, Symbol of Royalty. \textbf{Encounters:} Kandar and Shield Hippo ambush. \textbf{Route:} 7.8 in Chapter~\ref{written-granvania}.

\subsection{Demon Tower}
\label{atlas-demon-tower}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Basement and multiple entrance wings, teleporter rooms, spear floor, exterior treasure loop, flame floor and upper rock room, switch chambers and guardians. Start right entrance. Left spring supplies leader HP recovery. Spears reveal with movement, drop boulders from above to block dragon-head jets, operate both corridor switches for bridge. Wagon stays outside. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Wizard's Robe, Strength Seed, three Small Medals, Armband of Sacrifice, 880 G, Magic Water, Zombie Mail, Leaf of the World Tree, Wing of Wyvern. \textbf{Encounters:} One Demon Jar, two Mimics, Bomb Crag rocks, fixed teleporter guards, Level 20 Orc, Level 35 Wyvern, Jami. \textbf{Route:} 7.10--7.12, including 7.11.1--7.11.2, Chapter~\ref{written-granvania}.

\section{Third-generation travel, Zenithia, and temple maps}
\subsection{Water Shrine: El Heven route and ring portal}
\label{atlas-water-shrine}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Ship channels and dry treasure landings on one water level. Southern exit reaches El Heven lake. Northern silver door requires Final Key. Shallow channels are navigable, deeper-looking blocked water defines walls. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Cursed Satan Helm and one Small Medal. Portal chamber uses Flames left, Water right, Life center. Sage's Stone gift is at Demon World arrival shrine. \textbf{Route:} 8.6 and 10.1 in Chapters~\ref{written-reunion} and~\ref{written-finale}.

\subsection{Emblem Cave / Cave of Seals}
\label{atlas-emblem}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Entrance gate and successive seal basements, southern treasure branches, deepest four-face altar. Final Key opens gate, wagon stays outside. Stone tiles cover exposed monster faces and stop local encounters, all four deepest faces must be covered. Preserve pushing room behind each tile. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Small Medal, Elven Amulet, Demon Hammer, Royal Cloak. \textbf{Encounters:} Moon Face Chance, Eaters, Sauruslords. \textbf{Route:} 9.10 in Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}.

\subsection{Sky Tower}
\label{atlas-sky}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Seven-floor climb with separate eastern and western lower branches and exterior upper walkways. Carpet to clear northern plains, walk across approach, wagon outside. Eastern second-floor detour gets Leaf, western climb gets medal and staff. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Leaf of the World Tree, Small Medal, Magma Staff. \textbf{Encounters:} King Healer, Centaurus, Soldier Bull recruitment and dangerous healer groups. \textbf{Route:} 9.1 in Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}.

\subsection{Torokko Mines / Trolley Cave}
\label{atlas-torokko}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Track basements, second-floor isolated treasure pocket reached from third, final submerged connection to Zenithia. Use Magma Staff outside rock barrier south of El Heven. Set both first-floor gap switches, use left cart to set the later right-cart route, release Pusan's loop lever. Satan Helm enemies can curse, wagon stays outside. \textbf{Fixed finds:} 950 G, Small Medal, Acorn of Life, 750 G, Magic Water, Staff of Benediction. \textbf{Route:} 9.2--9.4 in Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}.

\subsection{Forest of Illusions}
\label{atlas-forest}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Connected woodland screens, dwarf's home, sword trail, campfire and southern gold hollow. Child required in active party. From entrance clearing go east, south, north for sword trail. Face space right of campfire and Talk, then follow child and guide. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Small Medal pot, Elven Medicine dresser, Faerie Sword, 1,500 G. \textbf{Encounters:} Eigerhorns and Farewell Crags. \textbf{Route:} 9.5 in Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}.

\subsection{Tower of Boble}
\label{atlas-boble}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Roof entry into seventh floor, split fifth-floor nun room, statue-level third-floor paths, first-floor entrance lever, branching basements and statue interior. Castle to island, Hook at roof ring. Pull entrance lever before boss circuits. Final Eye placement uses dragon's perspective, Right Eye in left screen socket and Left Eye in right. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Two Small Medals, 2,700 G, Devil's Armor, Shield of Darkness, Elven Medicine, Dragon's Left Eye, Dragon's Right Eye, Dragon Staff, Dragon Orb. \textbf{Encounters:} One Mimic, Gonz and Gema, paralysis breath and reflection. \textbf{Route:} 9.11--9.11.2 in Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}.

\subsection{Great Temple}
\label{atlas-great-temple}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Summit side room and assembly hall, hidden stage stair, captivity basements, upper walkways and lower ladders, priest's carpeted chamber. Dragon-only summit approach, humans active on approach, wagon supplies field recovery. Search Ramada's former standing tile for stair, Final Key opens deeper route, escape magic restricted. Ivol restores access to wider battle roster. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Zenithian Armor, Demon Claw, Slave Clothes, cursed Shield of Ruin, Ring of Life. \textbf{Encounters:} Snakehand armor guard, two Dragon Warriors, Ramada, Ivol. \textbf{Route:} 9.13--9.13.2 in Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}.

\section{Final and postgame dungeon maps}
\subsection{Evil Mountain}
\label{atlas-evil-mountain}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Entrance ascent to summit, separate fourth-floor robe branch, post-reunion arrow rooms, Pitcher-cleared lava room, falling-floor pocket, sliding-room grid and final chambers. Stepguard for damage tiles. Arrow-stair loop aligns eastward Pitcher route. Use Pitcher inside lava room. Drop southwest of arrival stairs. From initial grid with bottom-center vacancy, move rooms $7\rightarrow8$, $4\rightarrow7$, $1\rightarrow4$, $2\rightarrow1$, $5\rightarrow2$, numbering positions left to right, top to bottom. \textbf{Fixed finds:} Robe of Darkness, Ogre Shield, two Small Medals, Echoing Hat, Sun Crown, Sacred Pitcher, cursed Multi-Edge Sword, King Metal Helm, 3,300 G, Orihalcon Fang after guardians. \textbf{Encounters:} Two Urnite jars, one Mimic, two Dark Shamans, Fang guardians, Mildrath's two forms. \textbf{Route:} 10.4--10.9 in Chapter~\ref{written-finale}.

\subsection{Bonus Dungeon}
\label{atlas-bonus}
\textbf{Floors and access:} Five basements, first-floor ledge and Final Key gate, cracked-floor treasure pocket, second and third maze basements, fourth swamp corridor, fifth lava walkway to Estark. Enter swamp directly south of Evil Mountain after clear-state reload. First maze onward sequence is west, north, northwest, north, east, south, east, south. From third-floor central block go west, south, south for armor, retrace north, north, east. Onward route is south then east. \textbf{Nine fixed finds:} Restoration Orb, Small Medal, Shield of Radiance, Drum of War, Hell Sabre, cursed Sword of Decimation, King Metal Armor, Elven Medicine, Iron Ball of Decimation. \textbf{Other containers:} Two Mimics, three empty chests east of central, northern, and southern block rooms. \textbf{Encounters:} Hellbattler, Lionex, Springer recruitment, Estark. \textbf{Route:} 11.1--11.5 in Chapter~\ref{written-postgame}.
