\section{Strategy format: access, party, equipment, patterns, and recovery}
Choose the active trio before a dungeon that excludes the wagon. Refill HP and MP before speaking to a boss or opening a guarded chest. Put recovery items on more than one survivor and give each reusable tool to an accessible user. The table indexes bosses and scripted fights, with detailed routes in the linked walkthrough chapters. HP is per enemy. A question mark marks an unsafe estimate or a stage-dependent value. Chapter~\ref{written-bestiary} supplies EXP, gold, and possible drops. Scripted victories, recruitment resolutions, and defeats have their own outcomes.

\section{First-generation battles}
Party access changes between the Hero and Bianca's Lenoire visit, the Hero and Panther's faerie quest with Bella, and Papas's eastern rescue route. Chapter~\ref{written-childhood} contains the routes and early purchase choices. Stock herbs before each boss sequence and keep the Hero able to recover the party.

\subsection{Lenoire ghost boss}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-childhood}, Lenoire Castle. The Hero and Bianca fight the Walking Statue, four Demon Candles, and Boss Ghost at different points. Restore HP between the fixed fights. Boss Ghost uses Sap and fire magic. Have Bianca apply available defense-lowering or accuracy-disrupting support, then contribute spells or attacks. The Hero attacks while healing becomes his priority whenever another hostile action could defeat an ally. Reapply defensive support if Sap makes physical hits dangerous. Keep herbs on both characters so one user's MP or turn order does not decide the fight.

\subsection{Zaile and the Ice Mansion boss}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-childhood}, Ice Mansion. Zaile has 160 HP and can heal or drain MP. Use efficient physical damage and save resources for Snow Queen's 600 HP fight immediately afterward. The Hero and Panther supply the dependable attack and recovery plan, with Bella providing temporary battle support. Snow Queen uses cold damage, physical attacks, and healing. A deep breath signals a stronger attack, so recover or defend vulnerable allies before the next blow. Bring herbs to cover MP drain and repeated group damage. The Flute of Spring is the quest objective after victory.

\subsection{Gema and the childhood scripted confrontation}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-childhood}, eastern cave rescue. This Gema encounter advances through defeat and Papas's automatic intervention. A large stock of rare recovery items cannot change its required story outcome. Keep the battle's scripted nature in mind when deciding what to spend. Gonz and Jami appear in Papas's confrontation as story participants. Their later battles have their own statistics and player-controlled strategies.

\section{Second-generation battles}
The Hero and Henry begin adulthood with limited equipment. The wagon and recruited companions later expand the traveling roster, while many dungeon fights still depend on the chosen active trio. Chapters~\ref{written-escape}, \ref{written-marriage}, and \ref{written-granvania} contain the corresponding approach and recovery points.

\subsection{False Queen}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-escape}, Reinhart and the Southern Tower. Carry the Mirror of Ra back from the tower and restore the party before revealing the Imposter. The Hero, Henry, and a prepared companion can handle her 350 HP. Fire breath damages the group and summoned helpers can interfere with magic or healing. Remove a helper when its support prolongs the fight, then return concentrated damage to the queen. Henry's available support and a healing companion reduce the Hero's recovery workload. Check Henry's deposited equipment after he leaves.

\subsection{Killer Panther reunion: resolution without defeating it}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-marriage}, Kabochi and the western cave. Put Bianca's Ribbon in the Hero's inventory before entering the encounter. Have the Hero use it on the Panther and allow the recognition event to resolve. Other active members can defend while the item is used. The reunion restores the childhood companion to the roster and makes Papas' Sword available. The ordinary field Panther's stats and drop belong to a separate catalog entry.

\subsection{Lava Men}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-marriage}, Volcano. Prepare the Hero and two developed companions before reaching the Ring of Flames. All three Lava Savages have 400 HP and can damage the full active party with fire. Heal at the dungeon's recovery point, then enter with enough MP for repeated healing. Concentrate attacks on one Savage until it falls, reducing the number of possible breath attacks each round. A healing Slime Knight and a developed Wyvern are useful role choices. Keep the Hero free to heal when the other recovery user cannot keep pace. Spread damage across all three only when the group attack still produces a timely first defeat.

\subsection{Kandar and the Cave of Trials confrontation}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-granvania}, Cave of Trials. The wife stays in Granvania for this route, so prepare companions for the Hero's active trio. Kandar has 600 HP and the Shield Hippo 88. Kandar can heal, while the Hippo's guarding can reduce physical damage. Focus Kandar and use available defense support if healing pressure rises. Keep enough resources for the walk back because Outside remains sealed until the entrance portion. The Symbol of Royalty is retained for coronation.

\subsection{Demon Tower gatekeepers and Jami}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-granvania}, Demon Tower. Flying Shoes reach the approach shrine. Leaonar, Hawk Men, Orc King, Balloon, Mamoo, jars, and Mimics add resource demands before the summit. Clear summoned Curers when they sustain the switch guards. Avoid arriving at the summit with the same low HP left by the flame-statue traps.

Orc Lv. 20 has 950 HP, hits physically, and reduces defense. Use Increase and concentrated damage. Wyvern Lv. 35 has 800 HP and uses breath and Snowblast. Refill after each gatekeeper. Jami begins behind a story barrier that makes attacks ineffective. Defend and maintain survival until the wife removes it, then switch to attack support and healing. Preserve enough MP for the exposed phase. Exact HP is left unspecified for this staged fight. The stone transformation follows the completed fight.

\section{Third-generation battles}
Develop the children before the harder fights. The Son's Barrier at level 23 and the Daughter's Bikill at 16 and Decrease at 22 provide useful preparation targets. Use the Hero's recovery spells and item tools alongside their roles. Companion alternatives depend on the approach's party restrictions.

\subsection{Buorn / Bohen}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}, Salabona's seal shrine and tower. Inspect the sealed pot and complete Ludman's tower event, with the active trio prepared before the confrontation. Hero, Son, and Daughter are a practical team when their support spells are learned. Buorn combines heavy physical attacks, breath, lightning, and defensive boosts. Start with Barrier and Increase, then boost the physical attackers. Counter his raised defense through the Daughter's available defense reduction. Keep recovery ahead of the next damaging turn, especially while support spells are being established. Claim the Final Key after victory and revisit barred treasure routes. Exact HP and a guaranteed reserve-party rotation are unconfirmed.

\subsection{Gonz and Gema at the Tower of Boble}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}, Tower of Boble. Open the tower's main door during the first descent so town recovery is convenient between attempts. Use a carefully supplied active trio and carry the Grappling Hook. Gonz has 1,700 HP and relies on physical attacks. Increase and boosted fighters shorten the fight while a healer handles damage. Take the Left Eye from his chest.

Gema demands stronger recovery and paralysis safeguards. Barrier reduces breath pressure and Increase helps physical survival. Keep Numboff available through the Son or another qualified caster, with Full Moon Herbs on more than one character. An Elven Amulet helps reduce status risk. Total-party paralysis can end an otherwise healthy attempt. Avoid assigning every cure to one vulnerable user. The Right Eye follows victory. Put the Right Eye in the statue's left screen socket and the Left Eye in the right screen socket. The inner room's Dragon Staff and Dragon Orb complete the route.

\subsection{Great Temple guardians and high priest}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia}, Great Temple. Use humans for the approach. The Snakehand guarding the Zenithian Armor has 131 HP. Two Dragon Warriors at the crowd have 123 HP each. Patrolling Snakehands add optional fixed fights below. Equip the recovered armor on the Son before the deeper battles.

Ramada's stage encounter combines physical attacks with possible Blizzard. Use Increase and keep healing available. Check the party for curses after the false-Martha dialogue and recover before descending. Defensive debuffs are unreliable against this fight, so concentrate on dependable physical damage and support. Use the 1,800--2,000 HP range for planning.

Ivol summons the traveling roster before combat, permitting swaps. His dangerous sequence includes Explodet, cold breath, Bounce, and dispelling rays. Maintain Barrier, healing, and attack support, then rebuild the vital effects after dispelling. Keep physical offense available while Bounce is active. Rotate a healthy healer into the trio before another strong group attack can defeat it. Use wagon members as a recovery reserve and leave a survivor able to revive a fallen healer. The Ring of Life follows victory. Retrieve it and complete the exit and family reunion before assembling the next party at Ruida's.

\section{Final-dungeon encounters and both Mildrath forms}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-finale}, Evil Mountain. Two Dark Shamans guard Martha's plateau, with 170 HP each. Two Hellbattlers guard the Orihalcon Fang, with 450 HP each. Restore the active party before those fixed encounters and again before entering Mildrath's room. These guardians are ordinary enemy fights within a story route.

First form uses powerful magic, cold breath, dispelling, and summoned Evil Clowns or Attackbots. Use the Sage's Stone, a separate spell healer, Barrier, and physical attack support. Remove reinforcements when their actions destabilize recovery. Enemy Attackbots can act twice. Keep enough MP and consumables for the immediately following form.

Second form adds stronger group damage and Meditate recovery, with Bounce and dispelling still disrupting plans. Reapply Barrier after it is removed and restore attack boosts once survival is stable. Sage's Stone healing may need a strong spell heal in the same recovery cycle. Rotate healthy reserve members before a nearly defeated character becomes the sole remaining healer. Sustained damage must overcome the boss's self-recovery. Check for reflected magic before choosing offensive spells. Exact final-form HP is left unspecified. Keep a leaf and MP recovery on independent users, and treat Farewell as an emergency recovery action that sacrifices its caster. The carried Restoration Orb's exact activation and consumption remain uncertain.

\section{Estark / Esterk and repeat postgame challenges}
\textbf{Lookup:} Chapter~\ref{written-postgame}, Bonus Dungeon. Wait for The End, load the completed game's pre-boss save, and use the swamp entrance south of Evil Mountain. Carry the collected Drum of War and distribute the Sage's Stone, revival supplies, and MP recovery among active users. The Bonus Dungeon's ordinary enemies and two Mimics can consume resources before Estark.

Estark has 9,000 HP and two actions per round. His dangerous combinations include Explodet and powerful fire or cold breath. He also uses Bounce, dispelling, and sleep pressure. Start with Barrier and the Drum. Restore them after dispelling while preserving a healing turn. Status-protective equipment reduces the risk of the only healer losing an action. Plan recovery for the full two-action turn, with 200 HP a useful caution threshold and stronger recovery whenever equipment or recent damage makes that unsafe. Use the Sage's Stone alongside group healing when one tool cannot keep pace. Rotation and available revival matter more than committing a weakened trio for another attack.

Victory gives 5,000 EXP and 510 G, with a possible Small Medal. Estark reports the turn count. Leaving and returning permits repeat attempts. The count is a personal performance measure, with no verified extra prize or recruitment reward attached. Sleep susceptibility, exact elemental weaknesses, and multiplicative stacking of Drum and Bikill are unconfirmed, so the core plan uses dependable physical damage and recovery.
