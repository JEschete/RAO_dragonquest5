\section{Healing, status cures, revival, and MP-restoration consumables}
Medical Herbs cover early single-target recovery. Antidote Herbs cure poison and Full Moon Herbs cure paralysis. A Leaf of the World Tree revives one ally, while Sap of the World Tree restores party HP. Keep a leaf on someone who can survive the same attack that defeats the healer. Elven Medicine restores one character's MP fully, so reserve it for a depleted healer in a long dungeon or boss fight. Magic Water supplies a smaller refill. The Ring of Prayer can refill MP repeatedly until it breaks. Its breakage makes a second recovery supply valuable.

\section{Seeds, acorns, permanent growth, and allocation planning}
Acorns increase maximum HP or MP. Seeds increase strength, agility, defense, or intelligence. Allow for small, variable gains within the 1--5-point span, with exact per-item bounds uncertain. Choose recipients by role. HP helps fragile active healers, MP helps sustained spellcasting, and strength helps regular physical attackers. Agility helps a healer act before incoming damage. Intelligence helps a monster approach reliable command control. Favor companions you intend to retain.

\section{Battle items, reusable effects, limited uses, and breakage}
The Use command costs a turn. Assign each reusable tool to an active character whose ordinary action can be spared. Consumables, breakable tools, and equipped effects have different handling, even when their results resemble spells.

\subsection{Sage's Stone, Drum of War, and Sands of Time}
The Sage's Stone supplies free party healing in battle. Receive it on entering the Demon World and move it to a dependable support user. The Bonus Dungeon's Drum of War boosts the active party's physical offense. Refresh the boost after a dispelling attack. Do not budget a second multiplicative bonus from adding Bikill to an already boosted attacker. The Sands of Time come from Shahanna and restart a battle, making them useful when an opening round leaves the party unable to stabilize.

\subsection{Rings, stones, waters, bells, and situational tools}
The Ring of Flames and Ring of Water supply reusable elemental attacks. The Ring of Life restores its wearer's HP while walking and during battle. Keep all three for the portal. Angel's Bell is a reusable battle confusion cure. A Bomb Stone is an expendable attack. A Stone of Life belongs in the inventory of the character it protects. Holy Water is field encounter control. Fighter's Hair boosts one attacker. The Armband of Sacrifice activates when its equipped wearer dies and breaks in the process, with ordinary enemies as its intended targets.

\section{Keys and access progression}
Key access accumulates across the adventure. Revisit locked rooms when a new key arrives, using the route and treasure checklists in Chapters~\ref{written-escape}, \ref{written-reunion}, and \ref{written-zenithia}.

\subsection{Thieves' Key knowledge, Reinhart Key, Magic Key, and Final Key}
Learn Thieves' Key knowledge in the Dwarves' Cave during the faerie quest. Henry supplies the Reinhart Key for his castle. El Heven supplies the Magic Key for red-framed doors. Defeating Buorn supplies the Final Key for barred cells and the remaining locked access. The Final Key opens the Medal King's barred treasure and prison revisits. Check Reinhart, Granvania, shrines, and El Heven's locked rooms as their access becomes available.

\section{Childhood and faerie-realm objects}
Chapter~\ref{written-childhood} places these objects in their original routes. Preserve Bianca's Ribbon during the later party changes so the adult reunion can be resolved.

\subsection{Torch, Golden Orb, Flute of Spring, and Bianca's Ribbon}
The Torch opens Lenoire's dark route. Lenoire's ghost quest gives the Golden Orb, which is lost in the childhood confrontation. The Flute of Spring ends the Ice Mansion crisis when returned to the Faerie Queen. Bianca's Ribbon later lets the Hero identify himself to the Killer Panther. Its intelligence benefit also gives it an equipment role.

\section{Wedding, royal, and rescue objects}
Chapters~\ref{written-marriage} and \ref{written-granvania} contain the corresponding collection routes and story handovers.

\subsection{Papas' Letter, Mirror of Ra, wedding rings, and Silk Veil}
Papas' Letter accompanies the Zenithian Sword in adult Santa Rosa. The Mirror of Ra reveals the impostor queen. Retrieve the Ring of Flames from the Volcano and the Ring of Water from Waterfall Cave before the marriage decision. Collect the Silk Veil in Mountain Village for the ceremony. Keep the elemental rings after the wedding for their battle use and later portal placement.

\subsection{Symbol of Royalty, Flying Shoes, and Ring of Life}
The Symbol of Royalty completes the Cave of Trials objective. Search the minister's room after the Granvania abduction for Flying Shoes, then use them to reach the Demon Tower approach. The Ring of Life follows the Great Temple rescue and completes the three-ring portal set.

\section{Zenithia and Demon World objects}
Chapter~\ref{written-zenithia} contains the forest, palace, Orb exchange, Boble, and Great Temple routes. Chapter~\ref{written-finale} contains the portal and Sacred Pitcher route.

\subsection{Magma Staff, faerie horn, orbs, Grappling Hook, and Dragon Eyes}
Use the Sky Tower's Magma Staff to clear the Torokko entrance. Bring a child to the forest, obtain the Faerie Horn, and sound it at the lake flowers. Take the Glowing Orb from the palace into the childhood visit and exchange it for the Golden Orb. Return the Golden Orb to Zenithia. The Grappling Hook permits marked descents in Boble. Gonz gives the Left Eye and Gema the Right Eye. Place the Right Eye in the left screen socket and the Left Eye in the right screen socket.

\subsection{Dragon Orb, Zenithian Bells, and Sacred Pitcher}
The inner Boble chamber holds the Dragon Orb. Bring it to Pusan, then receive Zenithian Bells for Master Dragon travel. Evil Mountain's family event provides the Sacred Pitcher. Its lava use permits the descent toward the final battles.

\section{Maps, wagon, vehicles, and non-inventory unlocks}
The Wayfarer's Map is a reusable orientation tool. The 300 G wagon enables the expanded traveling roster in areas that admit it. The wedding ship opens sea routes. El Heven's Magic Carpet crosses suitable open terrain and water, with mountains and blocked landing areas limiting its route. Restored Zenithia travels to Boble for the hook approach. Zenithian Bells provide later airborne travel. These unlocks have route-specific boarding, landing, and party-access rules.

\section{Where to obtain, retain, use, or revisit each key object}
Follow four collection circuits. Childhood covers Torch, Golden Orb, Flute, and Ribbon. Early adulthood covers Letter, Reinhart Key, Mirror, and both wedding rings. The royal and reunion routes cover Symbol, Shoes, Map, Carpet, and Magic Key. Zenithia through Evil Mountain covers Horn, replacement Orb exchange, Hook, Eyes, Dragon Orb, Bells, Final Key, Ring of Life, Sage's Stone, Sands, and Pitcher. Keep reusable tools on accessible users, retain the three portal rings, and spend medals only after choosing the next reward. Granvania's concealed room contains the Ring of Prayer, Naughty Underwear, and Hat of Happiness as three separate finds.
