\chapter{Second Generation: The Road to Granvania}
\label{written-granvania}

\AchievementSetup{61381}{Special Delivery}{\textbf{Missable.} With

Keep the protected pre-choice church save until both bride achievements

The ship makes the journey feel much larger. The towns already visited
remain useful for recovery and supplies, while the southern desert and
islands offer new companions, valuable equipment, and another lead in
the search for the legendary warrior. Beyond them, the eastern mountains
hold the route to Granvania.

Prepare the travelling roster before sailing. Equip the bride and the
companions who will handle the next encounters, keep healing and revival
available, and leave some inventory space for the discoveries along
the mountain road.

\section{The first sea journey and optional coastal stops}

\subsection*{Leaving Port Selmi}

At the docks, confirm the Hero's identity to the man beside Ludman's
ship. Visit the tavern for the reports of a southern desert kingdom
with a shrine to the legendary warrior, then board when ready.

Steer with the directional pad and use the Wayfarer's Map to identify
the desert south of Port Selmi. Reefs define the area currently open
to the ship. The inner sea has several possible landings, and the
route toward Telepador follows the accessible southern coastline.

Sea encounters have their own opponents and can consume recovery
resources before the party reaches land. Keep the active members
healthy and choose a convenient town or inn stop if supplies run low.
A developing companion can travel in the wagon while a dependable
front line handles the voyage.

\subsection*{The desert oasis}

Land by the southern desert and look for the small pool surrounded
by trees toward its eastern side. Enter the \textbf{Desert Oasis}
and speak to the old man. He points toward \textbf{Telepador} to
the west and the Medal King's island off the southeastern coast.

Search the oasis pot for a \textbf{Small Medal}. The oasis gives a
useful landmark for the desert approach, and that medal contributes
to the equipment choices at the nearby island.

\subsection*{Choosing the next stop}

The Medal King's island and Telepador can be visited in either order.
A visit to the island first lets the party examine the exchange
equipment before spending more gold on defensive upgrades. Telepador
provides the story lead needed for the eastern journey.

If earlier town errands remain, use the ship and available Return
destinations to finish them while the party still has convenient access
to the western region. Keep the purchased map, important rings, Bianca's
Ribbon, and collected Zenithian equipment safe with the travelling party.

\section{Medal King Castle, islands, shrines, and travel planning}

\subsection*{The island and its companions}

Sail to the small island southeast of the desert and enter the castle
near its center. The surrounding encounters include useful recruitment
prospects such as \textbf{Curer} and \textbf{King Slime}.

Curer begins with Healall and develops further recovery and support.
King Slime brings its own growth and revival options. Either can be
worth pursuing when the roster needs another specialist. Keep the
active party prepared for the island's heavier groups while training
or attempting recruitment.

The castle has a depository, allowing the party to withdraw stored
medals and rearrange possessions. Its locked treasure area contains
three chests reserved for later key access.

\section{Telepador: desert approach and the Zenithian Helmet test}

\subsection*{The desert castle and its searches}

From the oasis, travel west across the desert to Telepador. Visit
during the day to enter the castle and meet its queen. The town's
services give the party a useful chance to recover before the longer
eastern crossing.

Collect the accessible finds as the party explores:
\begin{description}
  \item[Wing of Wyvern:] A pot in the basement of the weapon shop.
  \item[Defense Seed:] The left pot in the castle kitchen.
  \item[Small Medal:] Search the well in the lower garden area.
  \item[Demon Jar:] The other kitchen pot begins an optional encounter.
    Prepare the active party before searching it.
\end{description}

The kitchen's trapped pot can use dangerous magic. Keep available
revival and recovery if pursuing the battle, and collect the Defense
Seed separately from that optional encounter.

\section{Mountain Cave, west side: ascent, treasure, and resources}

\subsection*{Ned's Inn at the foot of the route}

Sail east along the accessible coast until reaching the small landing
and building at \textbf{Ned's Inn}. Rest and record the adventure.
The mountain road is long enough that both the active party and reserves
deserve a full recovery before entry.

\textbf{Mad Dragon} can be recruited around the approach. Train new
companions through manageable outings near the inn if the present
lineup needs strengthening. Carry accessible healing and poison
treatment, and keep revival available for the more dangerous encounters
farther along the mountain.

Travel north across the marsh toward the mountain entrance. Use
Stepguard when an available companion knows it, and check HP after
any damaging ground crossed during the approach.

\subsection*{550 G, the first medal, and the Ring of Prayer}

Follow the climbing route until two northward paths are available.
The eastern branch and its ledges lead to a chest containing
\textbf{550 G}. Return to the main route and continue on the western
side toward the next junction.

\AchievementSetup{61421}{Small Medal Collector}{Record each fixed medal
chest on this mountain route. Keep these chest collections separate from
medals obtained through hidden searches or trapped-container battle rewards.}

Take the southern branch at that junction for a
\textbf{Small Medal} chest. Return and use the northward route,
working along the eastern ledges to the \textbf{Ring of Prayer}.
The ring provides a useful MP-restoration option, with its possible
breakage worth accounting for when relying on repeated uses.

\subsection*{The underground rest and strength increase}

The next stairs lead to an old woman's resting place. Accept the

a strange sound, and the woman explains that she has sharpened the

\section{Chizod / Chizot: the village and the mountain crossing}

\subsection*{A necessary overnight rest}

The party emerges into \textbf{Chizod}, the village high in the
mountains. As the Hero moves through it, his wife falls behind and
becomes unwell. Speak to her and follow the scene as the townspeople
help bring her to the inn.

After a night's rest she feels better and is ready to continue.
Search the inn's barrel for a \textbf{Monster Lure}, then take
time to explore the shops and the remaining houses.

Below the shopping building, collect a \textbf{Fighter's Hair}
from the pot and a \textbf{Small Medal} from the barrel.
The Fighter's Hair \textbf{doubles one selected companion's attack}
when used in battle. Keep it with an available user for a suitable
longer encounter.

\section{Mountain Cave, east side: descent and hidden treasure}

\AchievementSetup{61383}{The Mimics of Mountain Pass}{Check that the

\subsection*{The first cash and trapped chest}

Descend the first two stair connections from the Chizod side.
Near the pool, take the northeastern branch to two chests. The left
holds \textbf{1,500 G}, and the right is a \textbf{Mimic}.
Choose the optional battle, required for \textbf{The Mimics of Mountain
Pass}, only with a healthy party and useful
recovery available.

Mimics can use dangerous instant-death magic, including Defeat.
Their defeat can provide a \textbf{Small Medal}. Keep track of
fixed medal chests separately from these battle rewards while
collecting the mountain's treasure.

Return to the main stairs and take the southwestern branch for a
fixed \textbf{Small Medal}. Continue along the southeastern route,
speaking to the traveller if desired, then leave through the exterior
opening and drop to the ledge below.

\subsection*{Rogue Armor and the middle-floor branches}

Enter the next opening, work west and south through its passage,
and take the next exterior drop. The ledge chest contains
\textbf{Rogue Armor}, also called Roaming Armor. It is cursed
and affects the wearer's controllability, so keep it unequipped
while arranging the ordinary travelling loadout.

Return through the ledge's opening to the main chamber. Its northern
side branch leads
to a \textbf{Leaf of the World Tree}. Return to the route and work
east for a paired set containing a \textbf{Mimic} and a fixed
\textbf{Small Medal}.

Continue south and take the western exterior doorway to another
\textbf{Small Medal} chest on the lower ledge. Re-enter through
the opening behind that ledge, follow the passage north, and climb
back to the main third-floor chamber. Take its eastern passage to
the chest containing \textbf{270 G}.
Follow the exterior connection around the cliff to the next cave opening.

\subsection*{The Bomb Stone and news of Sancho}

Continue through the lower passages to the junction with stairs and
a nearby drop. Use the western or blue-tiled stair branch for a
chest containing a \textbf{Bomb Stone}. Return to the junction
after collecting it.

The southern exterior branch has a bard who remembers a traveller
named \textbf{Sancho} on his way to Granvania. His account gives
the Hero a welcome connection with the household left behind in
childhood.

\subsection*{1,600 G and the hidden Staff of Punishment}

Take the eastern stair route and climb through the connected rooms

On that same upper floor, walk \textbf{east through the apparent

\textbf{Staff of Punishment}, providing a Tornado effect when

\section{Granvania / Granbania: Sancho, Ojiron, family, and castle searches}

\subsection*{The castle town and Sancho's house}

Granvania's town lies within the castle walls. Enter the outer grounds,
then visit the small house on the eastern side before the formal
audience. Important possessions carried by the Hero's wife can be
transferred to an available companion before the next scene.

Search the house's pot for an \textbf{Acorn of Life} and the dresser
for \textbf{Boxer Shorts}. Speak to the man living there. It is
\textbf{Sancho}, who recognizes the young master he last knew as
a child and is overwhelmed to see him alive.

After learning of the marriage, Sancho leads the couple to
\textbf{Ojiron}, Papas' younger brother and the acting king.
Ojiron recognizes his nephew. During the audience, the Hero's wife
becomes unwell and is taken to the royal rooms.

The examination explains her recent exhaustion: she is pregnant.
She remains in Granvania to rest, and the active party needs another
member for the expedition ahead. Retrieve any necessary supplies,
then prepare a suitable companion from the travelling roster.

\subsection*{Royal-room and terrace finds}

Once the scene has finished, explore the available rooms:
\begin{description}
  \item[Stone of Life:] One of the dressers in the royal sleeping rooms.
  \item[Lace Bisuche:] Another dresser in the same group of rooms.
  \item[Defense Seed:] The dresser in the adjoining royal room.
  \item[Small Medal:] Search the far northeast corner of the terrace
    reached from those rooms.
\end{description}

Keep the Stone of Life with an available companion and inspect the
free clothing's eligible users. The terrace medal is a ground search,
so examine the corner before returning downstairs.

\subsection*{Town-floor searches and the concealed treasure room}

The item shop has an \textbf{Intelligence Seed} in its barrel.
Reach the shop's side or rear area to perform the search. West of
the church, examine the row of pots for a \textbf{Strength Seed}
and another \textbf{Small Medal}.

East of the church, approach the large staircase. Walk into the
wall beside and below the steps to find a concealed room. Its
three chests contain a \textbf{Ring of Prayer},
\textbf{Naughty Underwear}, and \textbf{Hat of Happiness}.
Collect all three before returning to the public hall.

\Achievement{61385}{Hidden Treasure - Granvania}{5}{Find the hidden treasure in Granvania}{Enter
the concealed room beside and below the staircase east of the church.
Open all three chests and collect the Ring of Prayer, Naughty Underwear,
and Hat of Happiness before leaving the room.}

The Hat's walking MP recovery can help a compatible caster.
Compare the clothing with the wearer's current equipment and keep
the additional Ring of Prayer safe as a recovery resource.

The soldier rooms on the next floor also contain a
\textbf{Small Medal} in the eastern bathing-room dresser.
Complete these searches as access to that floor opens. The remaining
locked treasure rooms belong to a later key visit.

\subsection*{Ojiron's request}

\section{Cave of Trials: puzzles, the Symbol of Royalty, and Kandar}

\subsection*{The four-door entrance}

The cave opens with four doors and a room that repeats through
different connections. Use the following sequence from the entrance:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Enter the \textbf{far-right door}, then leave through that
    same doorway.
  \item Enter the \textbf{second door from the left}, then leave
    through that same doorway.
  \item Return south toward the entrance area to reach the newly
    accessible descending stairs.
\end{enumerate}

On the floor below, take the northward side route to the chest
containing a \textbf{Small Medal}. Return to the main path and
continue toward the reservoir door and nearby boulder.

\subsection*{The reservoir and Swordedge Armor}

Opening the reservoir releases a current that sweeps the Hero
toward the lower hole. Push the boulder upward into the same vertical
line as the door's latch, a few squares directly below it.

Stand at the latch and open the door. The water pushes the Hero
against the boulder, which holds him in place until the flow drains.
The path to the chest is then accessible.

Collect the \textbf{Swordedge Armor}, also called Blade Armor.
It provides useful defense and can return part of an attack's damage
to the opponent. Check the eligible wearer and current body piece,
then continue through the doorway behind the chest.

\subsection*{The floor plate and broken pillar}

Descend to the room with two bird-marked plates. Step on the
\textbf{left plate} to reveal the onward route and stairs.

On the lowest trial floor, approach the \textbf{broken pillar}
on the western side of the row. Search it and examine its protrusion.
A new marked tile appears. Step on that tile to form the path
across the water, then follow it north to the
\textbf{Symbol of Royalty}.

Collect the symbol and prepare to walk back. Magical escape is
restricted while leaving this part of the trial, and an ambush waits
on the return route.

\subsection*{Kandar and the Shield Hippo}

\textbf{Kandar} and a \textbf{Shield Hippo} confront the party
and demand the symbol. Kandar has about \textbf{600 HP}, while
the accompanying Hippo has roughly \textbf{90 HP} and can block
some physical attacks.

Recover the party before committing to damage. Upper or Increase
helps with physical pressure, and a useful defense reduction can
support the attackers. Kandar can heal himself, so maintain damage
once the party is in a stable position.

The smaller guard can be removed early with suitable attacks or
magic when its guarding slows physical progress. Then concentrate
on Kandar, keeping healing available for a heavy hit.

After victory, retrace the reservoir route and the entrance rooms.
Outside becomes available near the entrance area. Return to
Granvania with the symbol and recover before the court events.

  \item[Reservoir chamber:] Swordedge Armor behind the drained door.
  \item[Final trial room:] Symbol of Royalty beyond the pillar puzzle.

\section{Coronation, the twins, and the missing queen}

\subsection*{Returning with the symbol}

If the party returns at night, the castle guards direct the Hero
to Sancho's house. Speak to Sancho and accept the rest, then visit
the castle in the morning.

Speak to Ojiron and the chancellor to report the completed trial.
Ojiron confirms that the Hero will become king. The preparations
are interrupted when a maid announces that his wife is in labor.

Follow the scene upstairs, speak to her, then return downstairs
when the attendants ask. Speak to Ojiron, Sancho, and the guards
as the court waits. The maid eventually brings news of the birth.

Return upstairs to meet the \textbf{twins}, a boy and a girl.
Choose their names and confirm them. The couple's conversation
closes the family scene as the new mother rests.

\subsection*{The ceremony and nighttime search}

The next day, return to the throne room. Complete the coronation
and the greeting of Granvania's people. The celebration fills
the castle through the evening.

\Achievement{61386}{A Kingdom of Your Own}{5}{Be crowned king}{Complete
the coronation in the throne room and the greeting of Granvania's people
after returning with the Symbol of Royalty.}

When control returns during the night, visit the royal bedroom.
The queen is missing. Search \textbf{under the bed} to find
the attendant and the twins in hiding. She explains that the
queen sensed danger and had the children protected before the
intruders took her away.

Sancho arrives, and the following council discusses the kidnapping.
The chancellor has disappeared as well. Complete the conversations
and begin checking the rooms opened by the new circumstances.

\section{Flying Shoes, the Northern Shrine, and the Demon Tower approach}

\subsection*{The chancellor's dresser}

Visit the chancellor's now-accessible room northeast of the
council area. Search the dresser for the \textbf{Flying Shoes}.
Speak to Doris for her account of seeing him fly away.

The shoes lead to the northern region, but the party benefits
from a full preparation before following. Choose three dependable
members, equip them, and give each suitable recovery items. The
Demon Tower restricts wagon access, so place the important battle
supplies with those entering it.

Keep the Zenithian Sword and Shield, Bianca's Ribbon, the wedding
rings, and other important possessions safe through the journey.
Reassign needed items before a companion leaves the active party,
and record a prepared adventure before undertaking the tower.

\subsection*{The shrine's recovery and saving services}

Use the Flying Shoes outdoors to arrive near the
\textbf{Northern Shrine}. Its inn and upstairs church provide
recovery and saving. The residents report monsters travelling
north with a human among them.

Rest and record the adventure. Check the active members' HP,
MP, equipment, revival, and status-treatment options. A party
around the low thirties with reliable healing has a comfortable
foundation for the tower, although its hazards still deserve care.

Travel north from the shrine toward the Demon Tower once the
chosen three are ready.

\section{Demon Tower: moving floors, treasure, and gatekeeper battles}

\subsection*{The three entrances and first teleporter}

The tower has a large central entrance and smaller side doors.
The left entrance leads to a spring that restores the leading
companion's HP. Reorder the party when using it for another member,
and check the remaining MP before relying on further healing spells.
The nearby basement has a treasure hunter waiting for his brother.

Begin the main ascent through the \textbf{right-hand entrance}.
Climb the stairs and approach the marked teleporter. A fixed
\textbf{Leaonar} encounter blocks the way. Defeat it, recover
as needed, and step on the tile to reach the next treasure area.

\subsection*{The early robe, seed, and medal}

The first teleported room has two chests containing a
\textbf{Wizard's Robe} and a \textbf{Strength Seed}. The
\textbf{western pot} contains a \textbf{Small Medal}, while
the \textbf{eastern pot} is a \textbf{Demon Jar}.

Assign the robe to a compatible companion if its spell protection
improves the current loadout. Use or retain the seed according to
the active attackers. Treat the trapped pot as a separate optional
battle and keep revival available if opening it.

Defeat the \textbf{two Hawk Men} guarding the onward teleporters,
then use either tile. A \textbf{Leaonar and Orc King} encounter
waits near the next stairs. Continue upward after winning by taking
the nearby ascent. The northern stair branch reconnects with the
main entrance.

\subsection*{The spear room and eastern treasure loop}

The next room appears open until metal spears rise from the floor
as the party moves. Work around the revealed barriers to its chest,
which contains an \textbf{Armband of Sacrifice}. This is another
situational possession to keep available for an appropriate plan.

Take the \textbf{eastern stairs} for the treasure loop. Follow
the route east and north, climb the connected stairs, and use the
exterior doorway and descent to reach the three-chest room.
Collect \textbf{880 G} and \textbf{Magic Water}. The third
container is an optional \textbf{Mimic}.

Return to the spear room afterward. Its other staircase continues
the main climb. This loop is worth completing before committing
to the more hazardous upper passages.

\subsection*{Zombie Mail and the flame approach}

Take the \textbf{western stairs} from the spear room and work
around the floor opening to the next ascent. At the upper doorway,
step outside for the chest containing \textbf{Zombie Mail}, then
return and continue upward.

A small room on the next approach is exposed to dangerous dragon-head
flames. Use the \textbf{exterior door and bridge} to reach the
other side of this level and the safe stairs upward.

On the larger flame floor, stay behind the barriers and existing
cover while moving toward the stairs to the rock room. Stepguard
helps with damaging floor patches. The dragon-head jets require
physical cover along their firing lines.

\subsection*{Dropping rocks to block the flames}

In the upper rock room, first take the western and southern route
to the two chests. They contain a \textbf{Leaf of the World Tree}
and a \textbf{Small Medal}.

Return to the loose boulders and push several into the central
opening. Some apparent rocks are \textbf{Bomb Crags}, so keep
the party recovered while working through this area.

Descend to the flame floor again. Move the dropped rocks west and
south into the lines directly in front of the dragon heads. Use
each boulder to block a jet before crossing its path. Drop additional
rocks from above if another firing line still needs cover.

Once the route is protected, reach the stairs on the far side
and continue into the next upper section.

\subsection*{The bridge switches and isolated room}

Climb to the level with the exterior bridge. The crossing is
incomplete until its corridor controls have been operated.
Continue upstairs to \textbf{Corridor Switch Number One}.
Defeat the guarding encounter when it appears and operate the switch.

Use the nearby teleporter to reach the isolated room. Its
\textbf{left chest} holds a \textbf{Wing of Wyvern}, the
\textbf{right chest} is another \textbf{Mimic}, and the
\textbf{pot} contains the tower's third fixed \textbf{Small Medal}.
Operate \textbf{Corridor Switch Number Two} before taking the
teleporter back.

Return downstairs and cross the newly connected exterior bridge.
The chancellor lies on the route ahead. Speak to him for his
confession about the agreement with the monsters, then continue
toward the guarded chambers.

Restore the three active members before each confrontation.
The tower's three main battles come in sequence, and the recovery
plan needs to keep working across all of them.

\subsection{Orc and Wyvern gatekeepers}

The first guardian is the \textbf{Level 20 Orc}, with about
\textbf{950 HP}. Its physical attacks and defense reduction
can make a weakened companion vulnerable. Use Upper or Increase
when protection needs restoring, and apply suitable support before
settling into steady focused attacks.

Keep healing available as the Orc's HP is worn down. Victory
awards \textbf{350 experience} and \textbf{300 G}, and a
Fur Coat can appear as the battle-item reward. Recover before
following the newly opened route west.

The next guardian is the \textbf{Level 35 Wyvern}, with about
\textbf{800 HP}. Its offensive spells, breath, and healing
create a different kind of pressure. Watch the condition of all
three companions after an attack that reaches the group.

A successful defense reduction supports the physical attackers.
Use equipment protection and an appropriate recovery action to
handle the spells and breath, then maintain focused damage while
the guardian occasionally restores its own HP.

Victory awards \textbf{450 experience}, with a possible
\textbf{Wing of Wyvern} item reward. Heal before returning
toward the middle connection and descending to the queen's chamber.

\subsection{Jami, the final confrontation, and the generation transition}

Speak to the Hero's wife, who is being held with
\textbf{Jami}, also called Jahmi or Centarsus. She explains
that the kidnapping was intended to lure the king away from
Granvania. Finish the conversation and prepare for the battle.

Jami begins protected by a barrier that heavily reduces the
party's attacks. Use \textbf{Parry} and necessary healing
during that opening stage. Keep rare consumables available for
the later part of the encounter.

After Jami boasts of his protection, the queen intervenes with
a burst of light that breaks the barrier. Resume ordinary damage
and useful support once the opening has appeared.

His offensive magic and cold breath can injure several companions.
Maintain a dependable recovery member while the others attack.
Restore the most threatened ally promptly, and use the second
healer or accessible herbs when one recovery action is insufficient.

A successful defense reduction can speed the physical damage.
\textbf{Fighter's Hair} strengthens an appropriate attacker
when its item action fits the round. Protective equipment and
repeated recovery from Mystic Armor, if already purchased, can
help keep that attacker working.

Continue the attack-and-recovery rhythm until Jami falls.
The victory provides \textbf{3,500 experience}. Jami recognizes
the queen's light as a sign of the legendary warrior's bloodline,
then uses his final spell. The Hero and queen are turned to stone
before the rescue can bring them home.

\Achievement{61387}{Guardian of the Tower}{10}{Defeat Jahmi}{Defeat
Jami, also called Jahmi, after the queen breaks his barrier, then finish
the concluding scene and generation transition.}

The treasure hunters remove the statues, and the following scenes
separate the parents while Granvania searches for its missing
royal couple. This closes the second-generation route. The twins
remain in Granvania's care as the years begin to pass.
