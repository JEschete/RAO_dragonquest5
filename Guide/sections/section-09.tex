\chapter{Third Generation: Restoring Zenithia}
\label{written-zenithia}

The central continent's ruined tower preserves a route toward the
castle that once stood above the world. Follow its surviving stairs,
then the railway beneath the mountains, to discover why Zenithia
fell. The family search continues through the faerie realm and the
strongholds responsible for the years the Hero has lost.

Keep the children developing alongside reliable companions. This
journey includes several places where the wagon remains outside,
making the equipment and recovery items carried by the three active
members especially important.

\section{Sky Tower: floor route, treasure, and the Magma Staff}

\subsection*{The central-continent approach}

Use the Magic Carpet from open ground and travel to the central
continent. Land on the clear plains north of the poisonous marsh,
then walk toward the tower to the north.

Recover before entering. The wagon stays outside, so choose three
members who can sustain the whole climb. The Hero and Son provide
a useful foundation when their healing is ready. The Daughter gains
valuable experience here if her protection and the party's recovery
can support the encounters. A trained monster can fill either
supporting position as needed.

Give the active members healing supplies and keep a revival option
available. Enemy groups can include King Healers, whose recovery
extends a fight, so concentrate damage on a healer when its actions
are keeping the opposing group alive. The tower also provides
recruitment opportunities for companions such as King Healer,
Centaurus, and Soldier Bull.

A recruited \textbf{King Healer already knows HealUsAll}.
It gains Barrier at level 7 and Revive at level 10, making
it a valuable recovery reserve as its MP and equipment develop.
Budget its larger healing spells for the encounters that need them.

\subsection*{The eastern stairs and the Leaf}

Enter the main first-floor room and take the eastern passage toward
the stairs in its northeastern corner. Climb to the eastern part
of the second floor.

Enter the first doorway along this branch and open the chest for
a \textbf{Leaf of the World Tree}. Assign it to a member who
can use it if another companion falls. Continue up the nearby stairs
to hear the resident's account of the treasure in the tower.

Return down this branch to the main first-floor room. The remaining
treasures are reached through the other ascent.

\subsection*{The western ascent and the Small Medal}

Cross the first floor toward the northwestern staircase. Climb
through the separate second-floor section, follow its southern
exit onto the exterior walkway, and take the next stairs upward.
Continue through the third-floor room and its exterior connection
to the broad fourth-floor walkway.

Open the chest on this walkway for a \textbf{Small Medal}.
Continue east to the room above the resident visited on the
eastern branch. Inside, choose the northern of the two stair routes
and follow it through the fourth and fifth floors to the sixth.

\subsection*{The upper walkways and the Magma Staff}

Leave the sixth-floor room for the exterior path and go west.
The winged resident and treasure enclosure are visible farther
up the tower. Follow the doorway and stairs leading toward them.
The other downward branch is a short dead end that can be left
for a curiosity visit.

Climb to the seventh-floor approach and continue onto the upper
walkway. Open the chest for the \textbf{Magma Staff}, then
speak to the winged resident.

He explains that the tower once reached Zenithian Castle. The
castle now lies beneath the lake, and the staff can clear the
rock barrier hiding the route toward it. Keep the staff with
the travelling party and leave the tower with Outside or the
return path when the searches are complete.

\begin{routecheck}{Sky Tower collections}
\begin{description}
  \item[Eastern second-floor branch:] Leaf of the World Tree.
  \item[Fourth-floor exterior walkway:] Small Medal.
  \item[Upper treasure enclosure:] Magma Staff and the resident's
    directions toward the submerged castle.
\end{description}
\end{routecheck}

\section{Opening the mountain barrier and entering Torokko Mines}

Return to El Heven's region and approach the rock-blocked cave
\textbf{south of the town}. Use the \textbf{Magma Staff}
from its owner's Item menu while standing outside the obstruction.
The rocks crumble and uncover the mine entrance.

Recover and save before descending. The mines also leave the
wagon outside, so arrange the three active members and transfer
their recovery items while still on the field. Check their MP
after the tower journey and provide enough healing for a longer
series of track puzzles.

Enter the passage, take the first stairs downward, and approach
the mine carts. Torokko Mines, also called Trolley Cave, uses
levers to change junctions and carts to cross routes that walking
cannot reach. Inspect the connected track before boarding each
cart and collect the nearby chests before leaving a landing.

\section{Torokko Mines: trolley switches, loops, Pusan, and the lake}

\subsection*{The first rides and three western chests}

On the first basement floor, walk south from the entrance stairs
and board the first cart. Ride it north to the next landing.
Before taking the second cart, walk north through the narrow path
and east to the lever. Change the junction, then return to the
cart and ride to the western part of the floor.

Walk west past the next cart to the chest containing
\textbf{950 G}. Return along the path and walk south past
the cart. Take the steps west to the paired chests containing
a \textbf{Small Medal} and an \textbf{Acorn of Life}.

From this treasure platform, move toward the cart on the right.
Change the nearby junction and board the cart to jump the broken
track. At the next landing, change \textbf{both switches},
including the second one farther north, before boarding again.
The resulting ride crosses the gap and reaches the stairs to
the second basement floor.

\subsection*{The second basement and the two-cart puzzle}

Before travelling onward, follow the lower path around the room
to the northern chest for \textbf{750 G}. Return to the
arrival area, then explore east for the \textbf{Magic Water}
chest.

Set the nearby junction so the onward track points northeast.
Use the lower steps or ladder to reach the other lever and set
its track west. Return to the cart and take the ride across
the water and broken track.

Two carts are available at the next landing. Take the
\textbf{left cart} first, cross the gap, and change the lever
on the far side so the right-hand track points north. Drop
through the nearby hole and follow the stairs back to the
two-cart landing.

Board the \textbf{right cart}. Its longer route reaches the
third basement floor. A chest passes beside the ride, providing
a useful landmark for the next detour.

\subsection*{The Staff of Benediction detour}

From the third-floor landing, go west, climb the steps or ladder,
and follow the stairs back up to the isolated second-floor
treasure platform. Open the chest passed during the cart ride
for the \textbf{Staff of Benediction}.

Used as an item in battle, the staff casts \textbf{Healmore}
without spending the holder's MP. Give it to an active member
whose turn can support the party's healing. The staff is useful
for conserving resources through the remaining journeys.

Return down the same stairs to the third basement floor.
Satan Helm enemies in the mines can inflict a curse, so use
CurseOff when an available member has learned it, or arrange
church treatment during the next recovery stop.

\subsection*{Pusan's looping cart}

Follow the path south to the man circling endlessly in a cart.
Use the lever inside the loop. The rusted mechanism takes an
effort to move, and the cart finally leaves its circular track
and crashes beside the wall.

Speak to the passenger. He introduces himself as \textbf{Pusan}
and explains that he has been trapped here for twenty years.
He was travelling toward Zenithia and accompanies the party
as a guide for the remaining route.

Board his crashed cart and ride north across the gap. At the
next landing, climb the northern steps to the onward cart.
The closer cart returns toward the earlier stairs. Take the
onward ride, then board the final cart in the next chamber.
It carries the party through the water to \textbf{Zenithian Castle}.

\begin{routecheck}{Torokko Mines collections}
\begin{description}
  \item[First basement:] 950 G, Small Medal, and Acorn of Life.
  \item[Second basement main route:] 750 G and Magic Water.
  \item[Return stair from the third basement:] Staff of Benediction.
  \item[Looping track:] Rescue and speak to Pusan before taking
    the final carts toward the castle.
\end{description}
\end{routecheck}

\section{The sunken Zenithian Castle and the missing Golden Orb}

\subsection*{The hidden stair behind the throne}

Most of the castle lies below the water. Walk around the remaining
dry approach, climb to the large double doors, and continue
through the next doors and stairs to the throne room.

Pusan recalls a concealed stair behind the throne. Search there
to reveal it, then descend the long ladder to the castle's
orb chamber.

The \textbf{Silver Orb} remains on the western pedestal.
The eastern pedestal is empty, and Pusan realizes that the
missing \textbf{Golden Orb} caused the castle to fall. Speak
to him again so he can trace the lingering power of the lost orb.

\subsection*{The orb's journey through the Hero's childhood}

The vision follows the orb into Lenoire Castle and back through
the Hero's childhood. It recalls the treasure found with Bianca,
the meeting with a mysterious adult in Santa Rosa, and Gema's
destruction of the orb after Papas's death.

Pusan remembers that the faeries created the orbs. He suggests
finding their hidden village and asking for help, then waits
in the castle while the Hero investigates.

\Achievement{61392}{The Sunken City}{5}{Find out why there's a palace under the water}{
Finish the Golden Orb vision and Pusan's explanation of Zenithia's
fall in the orb chamber. Complete that scene and check the unlock
before leaving the castle.}

Zenithia is now a Return destination. Use Outside to leave
through the mine entrance, recover in a convenient town, and
prepare the family for the forest journey.

\section{Forest of Illusions: navigating the maze with a child}

\subsection*{The forest approach and the dwarf's home}

Bring \textbf{at least one of the children in the active party}.
The hidden guide can be seen by a child, and the journey is much
easier with a reliable fighter and healer supporting that child.
The woodland encounters include hard-hitting Eigerhorns and
Farewell Crags. Recover before entering and concentrate damage
on a dangerous opponent when its special abilities threaten
the party.

Return to Salabona and use the carpet east across the marsh.
Follow the river northeast to the sandy landing between the
forests. Land, walk south across the two bridges, and enter
the differently colored patch of woodland.

From the forest entrance, take the northern path to the small
home in the hillside. Search the \textbf{jar for a Small Medal}
and the \textbf{dresser for Elven Medicine}. The dwarf explains
that people who can see faeries can find the hidden village.
His companion remembers seeing them during her own childhood.

\subsection*{The Faerie Sword and gold detours}

Return south to the clearing by the entrance. Change screens
\textbf{east, south, then north} to reach the treasure trail
with the \textbf{Faerie Sword} chest. Check the Daughter's
weapon and the sword's eligible users before assigning it.

Go north through the fork and take its northern exit toward
the campfire clearing. Before approaching the fire, take the
southern exit to the small hollow with the \textbf{1,500 G}
chest. Return north to the campfire.

The forest's exits have unusual connections, so use the dwarf's
home, sword trail, fork, and campfire as landmarks when revisiting
a screen. The gold hollow is reached by leaving the campfire
clearing to the south.

\subsection*{The invisible guide}

Approach the fire with the child present. The children notice
someone standing beside it. Face the space immediately to the
\textbf{right of the fire} and use Talk to begin the encounter.

Ask to visit the faerie village, then follow the child and the
guide through the maze. Their path leads to a cave entrance.
Enter it to reach the village the Hero helped during childhood.

  \item[Treasure trail:] Faerie Sword.

\subsection*{The misty lake and the lily pad}

Travel to the \textbf{lake north of the Sky Tower}. Enter
the lake area and board its small boat. Sail north to the
\textbf{lily pad}, then use the Horn from its owner's Item menu.

The music clears the mist and reveals the palace. Continue
north and enter. A church is available on the right, and the
residents' conversations connect the Hero's El Heven ancestry
with the Son's legendary role.

\subsection*{The Queen's orb and two basement chests}

Speak to the Faerie Queen. Her ancestors made the original
Gold and Silver Orbs, and she creates a matching
\textbf{Glowing Orb}. It has the appearance needed for the
Hero's next task, and she believes he can reach the moment
when the original orb was still intact.

Enter the western room and speak to the guard so he opens the
way onward. Pass the stairs leading upstairs and take the
next stairs down to the basement. Open both chests for a
\textbf{Staff of Thunder} and \textbf{Princess Robe}.

Compare the free robe with the Daughter's current protection
and assign it to an eligible wearer before the next difficult
journey. Its defensive value helps stretch the equipment budget.

Return to the upper stairs. On the second floor, the eastern
painting commemorates the legendary warrior. The western
room holds the painting needed for the journey through time.

\section{The childhood return and the Golden Orb exchange}

\subsection*{The painting and Santa Rosa}

Transfer the \textbf{Glowing Orb to the Hero's own inventory}
before speaking to the woman beside the western painting.
Finish her explanation, then stand before the painting and
examine it. The Hero arrives in the Santa Rosa of his childhood.

Explore the village and speak to its residents, including the
northwestern guard. Sancho welcomes a visitor who knows Papas.
Go upstairs in the house to speak to the Hero's father again.
The conversation recalls the warning heard during childhood
and the family the Hero has spent these years trying to recover.

Complete the village conversations and find the young Hero
near the church with his panther companion. Speak to him and
accept his offer to show the \textbf{Golden Orb}.

\subsection*{Exchanging the two orbs}

Choose the exchange when prompted. The adult Hero keeps the
powered Golden Orb and returns the Queen's Glowing Orb to
his younger self. The encounter fulfils the strange meeting
remembered from the first-generation visit.

After the exchange, leave through the town exit to return

\section{Restoring Zenithia and unlocking castle travel}

\subsection*{The restored orb chamber}

Return to the throne room and use the hidden stair behind the
throne. Descend to Pusan, speak to him, and hand over the
\textbf{Golden Orb}. He restores it to its pedestal and
asks the party to follow him to the control tile.

Zenithia rises from the lake. The water drains, its residents
return, and the castle becomes a new travelling base. Explore
the recovered rooms before beginning the next field journey.

\subsection*{The Medal, fireplace, Hook, and healing pool}

Leave the throne room through the southern door and enter
the door on the right. Speak to the resident and climb to
the second floor. Search the dresser in the grandson's room
for a \textbf{Small Medal}.

Walk through the \textbf{fireplace on the northern side}
of that room to reach the grandfather's concealed room.
He explains that the Master Dragon's sealed power is in
Boble Tower. Search his dresser for the \textbf{Grappling Hook}.

Visit the room west of the fireplace room and search its
water pool for \textbf{Sap of the World Tree}. It restores
the party's HP, and the party can carry one at a time.
After using it, revisit the pool for another supply.

The library east of the throne-room entrance adds another
conversation about Pusan. Return to the throne room and
finish his dialogue after the searches.

\subsection*{Flying the castle}

Use the hidden stair and step onto the control tile to
pilot Zenithia. It crosses mountains and other terrain
that limited the carpet. The castle drifts while airborne,
so steer toward a clear landing and complete the landing
before stepping out.

\textbf{X opens the world map} during castle travel.
Use the moving castle to reach the mountain-enclosed island
south of Salabona, where Boble Tower stands. The Hook supplies
the tower's rooftop entry route.

\section{Salabona's sealed jar, Buorn / Bohen, and the Final Key}

\subsection*{Ludman's request and the red jar}

Return to Salabona and speak to Ludman upstairs in his mansion.
He is worried about the ancient jar in the island shrine
\textbf{northwest of Salabona, west of the Mountain Village}.
He asks the Hero to check its color.

Reach the shrine by ship or carpet, enter with the Magic Key,
and descend to the jar. Examine it to confirm the red glow,
then return to Salabona. The guard directs the party to
Ludman in the \textbf{Tower of Viewing beside the town}.

Recover, save, and deposit spare gold before climbing to him.
His final conversation starts the confrontation. The Hero and
both twins form the watchtower battle group, so prepare all
three for the encounter while arranging the party's equipment.

\begin{routecheck}{Buorn preparation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Develop the Hero toward the mid-thirties and give the
    children enough HP to survive a strong group attack.
  \item The Son learns \textbf{Barrier at level 23}. Its breath
    protection is especially useful here, alongside Increase and healing.
  \item The Daughter learns \textbf{Bikill at level 16} and
    \textbf{Decrease at level 22} to support physical damage.
  \item Equip appropriate fire protection and give an active
    member the Staff of Benediction for additional healing.
  \item Bring Sap of the World Tree for a group-healing emergency,
    plus a revival option and enough MP for sustained recovery.
  \item Keep the Zenithian Sword with the Son. Its battle item
    effect can remove the opponent's defensive magic.
\end{itemize}
\end{routecheck}

\subsection*{The watchtower confrontation}

Speak to Ludman at the top. He explains that his ancestor
Rudolph defeated the demon long ago and sealed it in the jar.
The weakening seal has released \textbf{Buorn}, also called
Bohen, who is approaching the town to settle that old grievance.

Ludman leaves to make preparations. Remain at the tower as the
approaching shape grows above the horizon. The demon's arrival
begins a long battle, with about \textbf{4,500 HP} to overcome.

\subsection*{Barrier, defense changes, and sustained damage}

Start with the Son's \textbf{Barrier} and the Daughter's
\textbf{Bikill on the Hero}. The Hero can attack when the
party's HP allows it. On the next safe turns, apply
\textbf{Increase} to the party and \textbf{Decrease} to Buorn.

Buorn uses powerful physical attacks, raging fire, and thunder.
Barrier reduces the breath damage, while the other attacks still
require a healthy reserve of HP. Restore a threatened member
before continuing the attack plan. The Hero and Son can share
spell healing, and the staff adds a recovery action to its holder.

When Buorn lowers the party's defense, restore Increase as
needed. When he raises his own defense, use Decrease or the
\textbf{Zenithian Sword as a battle item} to remove that
protection. A Son occupied with urgent healing can leave the
immediate defense response to the Daughter.

Use safe turns to put Bikill on the Son and add his physical
attacks. Reserve sufficient MP for protection and recovery
through the whole fight. Additional attack spells can be used
when that reserve is secure.

Victory awards \textbf{9,500 experience}.

\Achievement{61394}{Rude Awakening}{5}{Defeat Buorn}{
Defeat Buorn in the watchtower battle and check the unlock after
victory. Collect the Final Key from the resulting chest.}

Open the chest that appears for the \textbf{Final Key}, then return to
Ludman and finish the household conversations. Rest and save
before using the key for the next treasure circuit.

\section{Final Key cleanup and the Royal Cloak side route}

\subsection*{Oracleberry's prisoner and the Medal King treasury}

Return to Oracleberry and take the northern stairs down to
the prison. Open the occupied middle cell with the Final Key
and speak to the prisoner. Search the stool or its adjacent
square immediately to the left for a \textbf{Small Medal}.

At \textbf{Medal King Castle}, open the barred treasure room.
Its three chests contain \textbf{two Small Medals} and a
\textbf{Golden Tiara}. Compare the Daughter's present helmet
with the Tiara. Keep the Hat of Happiness available when its
MP recovery is useful during a longer journey.

\subsection*{Emblem Cave and the upper seals}

The \textbf{Royal Cloak} side route is in Emblem Cave,
also called Cave of Seals, \textbf{northwest of Santa Rosa
and east of Lenoire Castle}. Reach it by carpet or a
convenient ship landing. Carry the Final Key with an active
member and enter with a fully recovered three-person party.
The wagon remains outside.

\AchievementSetup{61395}{Monsters, Begone!}{Keep the entrance seal
covered, seal the exposed face on each of the two upper floors, and
cover all four faces in the final chamber. Complete every seal for
this achievement during the Royal Cloak route. Check their positions
before leaving.}

The cave's monster faces govern its random encounters.
Cover an exposed face with a stone tile to seal the local
enemies. Keep a tile already covering a face in position.
The final chamber requires all four of its faces to be covered.

Open the entrance gate and follow the path north to the
stairs. On the next level, follow the western and southern
path to the loose tile. Push it back onto the exposed face
for the all-monsters achievement, then continue to the
next downward stairs.

Moon Faces can cast Chance, producing effects that include
severe damage or drained MP. The Eaters and Sauruslords also
make this a dangerous place for an underprepared group.
Use Outside for a recovery trip when the party's remaining
resources become too low for the deeper rooms.

\subsection*{The treasure floor's four southern branches}

On the larger treasure floor, follow the path south and west
toward the four southern branches. The westernmost branch
contains this floor's exposed seal. Move the available tile
onto it for the all-monsters achievement before searching.

The next branch to the east holds a \textbf{Small Medal}.
The third southern branch holds an \textbf{Elven Amulet}.
Collect both, then take the \textbf{easternmost branch}
and its stairs down to the deepest floor. The other upper
stair branch is a short dead end.

\subsection*{The Demon Hammer and four final tiles}

From the deepest floor's arrival area, go north and follow
the eastern side path to the \textbf{Demon Hammer} chest.
Return to the main junction and follow the northern route
toward the altar and four-face chamber.

Move each tile to a free face, keeping space behind the tile
for the next push and leaving the main passage available:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Use the tile on the approach immediately south of
    the four-face chamber to cover the first face.
  \item Return toward the arrival junction and explore the
    room on its western branch. Push its northern tile up
    the passage into the seal chamber.
  \item Follow the southern branch from that western area
    for the next tile. Push it north along the same approach
    and cover a third face.
  \item Go west from the four-face chamber, then north to
    the remaining tile. Bring it back to cover the last face.
\end{enumerate}

Once all four faces are sealed, the monsters vanish and
the \textbf{Royal Cloak} appears at the altar.

\section{Tower of Boble: Grappling Hook, dragon statue, and floor route}

\subsection*{The island landing and rooftop entrance}

Pilot Zenithia to the mountain-enclosed island
\textbf{south of Salabona, west of Telepador's continent}.
Land on the small desert patch and walk toward Boble Tower.
Bring the \textbf{Grappling Hook with an active member}.

Choose a fully recovered three-member party with strong
protection, reliable healing, and paralysis recovery. A Hero
around the high thirties or early forties has a comfortable
foundation for this long journey. The Son's Barrier, Numboff,
and healing make him particularly useful. Give multiple active
members \textbf{Full Moon Herbs} so an available companion
can treat paralysis.

The tower's dragons have dangerous physical and breath attacks.
Keep the Royal Cloak on the Hero, inspect the others' elemental
protection, and bring the Staff of Benediction. The wagon remains
outside throughout the route.

Climb the exterior ladders to the roof. Stand by the golden
ring above the roof opening and use the \textbf{Grappling Hook}
to descend into the seventh floor.

\subsection*{The first Medal and the injured nun}

Take the stairs down through the sixth floor and into the
separate fifth-floor room. Open the chest for the first
\textbf{Small Medal}, then leave through its door into the
main fifth-floor area.

Circle the central enclosure containing the nun and descend
to the fourth floor. Use the central stairs back up to reach
her. She warns that two creatures have stolen the Dragon Eyes.
Return down to the fourth floor and take its northeastern door.

\subsection*{The gold, armor, and entrance lever}

Follow the passage to the third-floor exterior area overlooking
the dragon statue. Continue through the northeastern door to
the two stair branches.

Take the \textbf{eastern stairs} first and open the chest

\subsection{Gonz, Gema, and the two Dragon Eyes}

\subsubsection*{Gonz and the Left Eye}

Approach \textbf{Gonz} after restoring the party's HP.
He has about \textbf{1,700 HP} and fights with physical attacks.
Use Increase when his blows require more protection, then
support the fighters with Bikill and maintain steady healing.

Victory awards \textbf{3,500 experience} and \textbf{650 G}.
Open the chest behind him for the \textbf{Dragon's Left Eye}.
Use the opened front door for a recovery and saving trip
before the second confrontation when the party needs it.

\subsubsection*{Gema's chamber and paralysis preparation}

Return to the western treasure branch. Follow the northern
passage beyond the paired-chest area and take the onward
stairs into Gema's separate chamber.

Recover before speaking to \textbf{Gema}. The confrontation
returns the Hero to the enemy who ordered Papas's death.
Gema recognizes him and begins the battle after their exchange.

Apply \textbf{Barrier} for the flames, \textbf{Increase}
for physical protection, and \textbf{Bikill} for the main
attacker. Maintain a dedicated recovery action as his stronger
spells and breath attacks threaten the party.

\textbf{Numbing Breath is the central danger}. Use Numboff
or Full Moon Herbs promptly while another member remains
able to act. The Elven Amulet offers its wearer a useful
chance of resisting paralysis. A fully paralyzed three-member
party loses this battle, so spread the recovery supplies
across the active members.

Gema can cast \textbf{Bounce}. Use physical attacks while
his reflection is active and keep support spells directed
toward the party. The healing staff helps conserve MP during
the longer fight. Restore fallen members when the surviving
companions can sustain the recovery turn.

After victory, Gema praises the High Priest before dying.
Open his chest for the \textbf{Dragon's Right Eye}.
Recover and save through the front-door shortcut before
returning to the statue if needed.

\section{Great Temple: entry, captivity rooms, and party restrictions}

\subsection*{The cloud-ringed mountain and human party}

\subsection{Temple guardians, the high priest, and the wife's rescue}

\subsubsection*{Ramada and the hidden stair}

\subsubsection*{Ivol and the restored wagon roster}

\Achievement{61403}{Can I Have Your Autograph?}{4}{Use Ivol's Book in the battle with Ivol}{

Use three active members at a time and rotate the wider
roster when needed. A useful opening is the Hero attacking,

\textbf{Bounce}, and a freezing wave that removes the

protection. Use physical attacks while his Bounce is active.

user forward to renew breath protection. Reapply Bikill to
the active attackers when healing leaves a safe opportunity.
Restore Increase if physical damage calls for it.

Use the Son's Healus when available or rotate in a
\textbf{King Healer for HealUsAll}. The latter restores

and wagon. Replace an injured or exhausted fighter with
a prepared reserve while maintaining the recovery role.
The staff and carried Sap provide additional healing options.

Keep the roster's recovery resources in view through the
whole battle. The available members let the party recover
from a fallen active fighter and continue the attack plan.

\subsection{Ring of Life and preparations for the Demon World}

Speak to Ojiron about the intended journey. His concern
for the family accompanies the decision to continue the
search for Martha.

Visit \textbf{Ruida's tavern and rebuild the human roster}.
The reunion removes the travelling humans from the active
party, so select the Son, Daughter, wife, or other intended

Retrieve the \textbf{Ring of Flames}, \textbf{Ring of Water},
and \textbf{Ring of Life} from their present owners or
the depository. Keep all three with the travelling party,

while walking and after battle rounds. Use its gradual recovery
alongside the party's healing spells and carried supplies.

Recover, restock the party's healing and revival items,
deposit spare gold, and record the adventure. The household
has been reunited, and the next voyage continues the
search beyond the boundary Martha is guarding.
