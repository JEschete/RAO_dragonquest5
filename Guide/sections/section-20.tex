\section{Medal collection, exchange rules, rewards, and planning}

The Medal King takes carried Small Medals into an account. Each exchange
spends its listed cost. The island southeast of Telepador is reachable
by ship after marriage. See 7.2 for the visit and exchange.

Reserve 21 for Mystic Armor when recurring recovery is the priority.
Check equipment eligibility before exchanging. Keep medals, purchased
rewards, and remaining balance as separate collection entries.

\section{Version-specific Mimic encounters}

\textbf{Optional early-DeJap-patch repeat method:} save at a church before
opening a known Mimic or Man-Eater Chest. After a surviving party wins,
leave the room, re-enter, and examine the same chest. Some early patches
allow another battle. A critical-hit finish can permanently clear the
trap. Record only medals actually awarded. Compatibility with v2.01F
is unconfirmed. See 22.9 for the compact trial procedure.
