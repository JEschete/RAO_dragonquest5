\chapter{Third Generation: Reunion and El Heven}
\label{written-reunion}

The Hero's return brings a family together after years of searching.
The children who were left in Granvania as infants have grown up with
Sancho's stories and the hope of finding their parents. They now have
their own part in the adventure, alongside the companions who travelled
with the Hero before the tower.

Begin with the familiar services in Granvania. The new human companions,
the Son's equipment, and the ship prepared by Ojiron give the party
several useful choices for the next journey.

\section{The years in stone, rescue, and the return to Granvania}

\subsection*{The statue in the garden}

After the tower, the treasure hunters sell the Hero's statue at an
auction. A household places it outside as a good-luck possession.
The scenes that follow show the family's child growing while the Hero
remains unable to move.

Monsters eventually take the child away, leaving the household
despondent. Seasons continue to pass around the abandoned statue.
Eight years after the tower events, Sancho arrives with two children
who recognize what they have found.

\subsection*{The twins break the curse}

The Daughter uses the \textbf{Staff of Stoross} to restore the
Hero. Sancho introduces the two children as his Son and Daughter.
They have searched for him and want his help finding their mother.

\AchievementSetup{61401}{A Stronger Curse}{Keep the Staff of Stoross
available for the search for the queen. At the Great Temple, use it
on your wife's statue while her curse remains, before defeating Ivol.
Make the attempt before Ramada for a safe point in that visit.}

Complete the reunion conversations. The Daughter casts Return,
bringing the family back to Granvania. The kingdom celebrates
the missing king's return, and the Hero rests before the next
morning's meeting with Ojiron.

The queen is still missing. The renewed search now includes the
children and the information Granvania gathered while looking for
the royal couple.

\section{The twins, Sancho, Pippin, and rebuilding the active party}

\subsection*{The family joins the journey}

Leave the royal room and continue toward Ojiron. The twins meet
the Hero on the way and insist on accompanying him. Finish their
conversation to add both children to the travelling roster.

\AchievementSetup{61410}{A Family Matter}{Begin developing the Hero,
Son, and Daughter now, then include your wife after her rescue. For
the later family-only Mildrath attempt, store all monsters and leave
Sancho and Pippin at the tavern. Use three active family members with
any human reserve also drawn from the family. Develop the Son's
healing and assign recovery items to the wife or Daughter. Prepare
HP, MP, equipment, and supplies for both consecutive forms.}

\AchievementSetup{61411}{Heroic Trainer}{Keep developing wild-recruited
monsters alongside the children for a separate Mildrath attempt later.
Build a durable physical role with Golem or Slime Knight and a healing
role with King Healer or a developed Slime Knight. Inspect actual
healing spells, HP, MP, and protection as they grow. Before that
attempt, store every human except the mandatory Hero and keep him
inactive. Use only wild-recruited monsters in the active positions
through both forms. The Hero must perform no attack, healing, item,
or substitute action. Rotate only within the eligible monster pool
and check the achievement unlock before reloading a protected normal
church save for another attempt.}

Speak to \textbf{Ojiron}. Granvania's search has located Martha's
birthplace, \textbf{El Heven}, on the northeastern continent.
He marks the region on the map. Sancho explains that a ship is ready
and that the tavern now offers services for arranging both human
and monster companions.

\subsection*{Ruida and the expanded roster}

Visit \textbf{Ruida} at the second-floor tavern to organize human
companions. Speak to \textbf{Sancho} for his offer to travel,
and meet \textbf{Pippin}, also called Pipin or Nello, in the
room behind the inn. Return to the tavern to add available members
to the travelling party.

Monster Gramps is also present. Arrange the roster and inspect
the returning monsters' levels, spells, and equipment. The services
make Granvania a convenient base for preparing several different
lineups.

\subsection*{Giving the children a comfortable start}

Inspect the twins' current HP, MP, spells, and equipment. The trained
monsters provide useful partners while the children develop. Equipment
already purchased for eligible companions can be reassigned as the
new lineup takes shape.

The Son learns \textbf{Increase at level 8} and
\textbf{Healmore at level 12}, giving him useful support and

the existing inventory and storage before planning another purchase.

\section{Reinhart revisited: Henry, Del, and family-related rewards}

\subsection*{Henry and Maria's household}

Return to Reinhart with the children available to accompany the
visit. The guards recognize the Hero, and Dale welcomes him back.
Upstairs, Henry and Maria are relieved to see their old friend again.

Their son \textbf{Del}, also called Collins, has inherited a
familiar enthusiasm for pranks. Speak to Henry and the boy, then
let him take the twins on a tour. When the children return without
their guide, go to Henry's old childhood room.

Search the stool or square that concealed the stairs during the
first-generation visit. Descend to find the boy in his hiding place.
A servant returns him to the family. Go back upstairs and speak
to him for the \textbf{Hat of the Wind}.

\Achievement{61388}{Just Like His Father}{4}{Acquire the Hat of Wind}{

Give it to an available traveller and check the relevant destination
before using it during a field journey.

\subsection*{The guarded Demon Armor chest}

On the western side of Reinhart's castle grounds, a man guards
a chest. Speak to him or approach the treasure to fight a
\textbf{Kandar Henchman}, with about \textbf{500 HP}.

\AchievementSetup{61419}{Kandar's Henchmen}{Record this Reinhart
grounds victory toward the three guards. The other two guard the

Prepare the active members before triggering the encounter. He can
use Sleep, so keep another recovery or item action available if
an important companion loses a turn. The Hero and a reliable
attacker can maintain focused damage while the third member handles
support and injuries.

Victory gives \textbf{750 experience} and \textbf{150 G}.
Open the chest for \textbf{Demon Armor}. The armor is cursed.
Keep it unequipped until choosing an intentional use and accounting
for the curse-removal service.

\section{El Heven: family history, the Magic Key, and the Magic Carpet}

\subsection*{The old couple and the four elders}

El Heven is built above a network of lower paths. Climb the entrance
stairs and explore the raised town, including the shops east of the
inn. A resident directs the Hero toward the four elders.

Climb the long ladder to the old couple's room and search the pots
for a \textbf{Defense Seed} and \textbf{Small Medal}.
Their conversations recall Martha and the arrival of Papas. Continue
toward the elders' chamber and speak to all four women.

They welcome Martha's son and explain the village's role in guarding
the boundaries between the worlds. Its people's old power has weakened
with time, but Martha possessed an unusually strong gift. They believe
she was taken so that her power could be used to force open the way
to the Demon World.

The elders' warning gives the search urgency. Rescue Martha and the
missing queen while pursuing the means to reach that realm. Finish
the village conversations, including the man with a cane who offers
the hidden key and carpet if the party can find them.

the earlier towns convenient for recovery and purchases.

The important travel unlocks here are the \textbf{Magic Key} and
\textbf{Magic Carpet}. Their treasure circuit adds gold, a strong
agility accessory, and useful extra equipment while the party develops.

The \textbf{Sky Tower} is reached from the central continent's
open plains. Land north of its surrounding marsh and approach
on foot with the chosen active members prepared. The wagon stays
outside, so equip all three active companions for an independent
dungeon journey and assign their healing items before entering.

The tower holds the \textbf{Magma Staff}. Its power clears the
rocks blocking the cave south of El Heven. That passage's railway
continues the route toward the submerged Zenithian Castle.

\subsection*{Choosing when to investigate Salabona's warning}

Ludman has a new concern about the old jar shrine north of Salabona.
The Magic Key provides the relevant shrine access. His request leads
toward a demanding confrontation, so prepare the active party before
reporting the changed jar and visiting the viewing tower.

More training, protection, and the children's developing support
spells make that confrontation easier to sustain. The Sky Tower
route provides another journey on which the party can develop first.
Prison-bar treasure, including the Medal King's locked area, remains
reserved for the Final Key obtained through that separate challenge.
