\chapter{Complete SNES Monster Companion Catalog}
\label{written-companions}

\section{Companion profile fields}
Each profile gives a usable recruitment region, growth ceiling, starting
abilities, every later learning level, and a compact role. ``Starts'' means
available when recruited. ``None'' means the species has no starting spell
or special skill. The cap field is independent of the final learning level.
Firebal and Firevolt also appear as Fireball and Firebolt. Freeze Ray also
appears as Freezing Rays. These names identify the same respective abilities.

\section{Individual monster profiles}

\subsection{Attackbot}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Demon World fields, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none and learns none.
\textbf{Role:} Durable physical attacker with weapon-based damage. Equip and
develop its attack and defenses. Its companion profile has no learned double-action skill.

\subsection{Big Eye}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Telepador desert and earlier southern-tower encounters, level 20.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Healmore and Cold Breath. Learns 5 Build Up,
8 Dazzling Light, 10 Sweet Breath, 15 Healall.
\textbf{Role:} Early recovery and group breath support, with a short growth schedule.

\subsection{Blizzard Hawk}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Northern continent and Emblem Cave routes, level 25.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Defeat, Cold Breath, Freezing Breath.
Learns 8 Strange Dance 3, 15 Healus, 23 Sparkling Breath.
\textbf{Role:} Cold group damage with secondary party healing.

\subsection{Bomb Baby}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Oracleberry fields, level 15.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Sacrifice. Learns 5 Body Attack.
\textbf{Role:} Sacrificial damage and recoil attacks. Keep recovery available.

\subsection{Bomb Crag}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Volcano south of Salabona, level 20.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Sacrifice and Charge Up. Learns 10 Body Attack,
19 Farewell.
\textbf{Role:} Emergency sacrifice or party restoration, with a durable stone body.

\subsection{Brownie}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Fields east of the nunnery and around Oracleberry, level 99.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none and learns none.
\textbf{Role:} Physical specialist. Weapon upgrades and obedience determine usefulness.

\subsection{Centaurus}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Central-land Sky Tower approach and surrounding routes, level 50.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Healmore. Learns 5 Burning Breath, 9 Repel,
15 Boom, 20 Bikill, 23 Healall, 27 Barrier.
\textbf{Role:} Physical and support hybrid with healing and late breath protection.

\subsection{Curer}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Medal King island and called healing-monster encounters, level 50.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Healall. Learns 3 Healmore, 8 Upper,
10 Increase, 12 Vivify, 18 Healus, 23 Revive, 30 Healusall.
\textbf{Role:} Long-term recovery specialist. Train revival and party healing first.

\subsection{Dancing Jewel}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Salabona volcano, level 7.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Decrease, Confuse, Stopspell, Strange Dance 1.
Learns 4 Absorb, 5 Beat, 6 Sleepmore, 7 Aerocross.
\textbf{Role:} Resistant control caster. Intelligence improvements support dependable commands.

\subsection{Dancing Needle}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Reinhart fields, level 25.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Strange Dance 1. Learns 5 Strange Dance 2,
10 Sleep Attack, 15 Strange Dance 3, 20 Numbing Attack, 25 Body Attack.
\textbf{Role:} MP disruption and attack-based status control.

\subsection{Dragon Pup}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Port Selmi fields and Southern Tower encounters, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Fire Breath. Learns 5 Sweet Breath,
15 Flame Breath, 28 Violent Flames.
\textbf{Role:} MP-free fire damage and sleep support. Protect it during early training.

\subsection{Drakee}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Oracleberry and western first-continent fields, level 99.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none. Learns 5 Surround, 10 Sleep,
15 Day-Night, 17 Strange Dance 1, 20 Robmagic, 22 Dazzling Light, 38 Bedragon.
\textbf{Role:} Early control and later field utility, with a long growth schedule.

\subsection{Eigerhorn}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Forest of Illusions, ceiling unsettled between 40 and 50.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Firebane, Tornado, Snowblast. Learns 7 War Cry,
9 Charge Up, 11 Firevolt, 13 Eerie Light, 15 Blizzard.
\textbf{Role:} Broad group magic with strong physical potential. Its complete
spell development through 15 is a practical training target.

\subsection{Eliminator}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Later northern-continent routes, including west of Alcapa, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none. Learns 7 Massacre, 20 Sap.
\textbf{Role:} Heavy physical damage and single-target defense reduction.
Massacre carries friendly-fire risk.

\subsection{Farewell Crag}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Forest of Illusions and late Salabona-region fields, level 20.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Farewell. Learns 6 Body Attack, 8 Bounce,
10 Dazzling Light, 14 Vivify, 20 Thunder.
\textbf{Role:} Emergency party restoration, with revival and disruption options.

\subsection{Gigantes}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Demon World fields outside Evil Mountain, level 7.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none and learns none.
\textbf{Role:} High-power physical combat with a very short development schedule.

\subsection{Golem}
\textbf{Region and cap:} El Heven and central-land Sky Tower routes, level 50.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none. Learns 8 Build Up, 11 Charge Up,
20 Meditate, 45 Thunder.
\textbf{Role:} Durable physical anchor with MP-free self-recovery at 20.

\subsection{Great Dragon}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Demon World fields, level 60.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Flame Breath. Learns 10 Violent Flames,
15 Freezing Breath, 20 Burning Breath, 30 Sparkling Breath, 50 Infernal Flames.
\textbf{Role:} Resilient physical attacker and strong MP-free group damage.

\subsection{Healer}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Southern Tower, including called Healers, level 99.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Heal. Learns 5 Repel, 7 Healmore,
10 Antidote, 15 Numboff, 20 Healall, 25 Healus, 30 Vivify, 36 Healusall.
\textbf{Role:} Early recovery recruit with a complete long-term healing progression.

\subsection{Hellbattler}
\textbf{Region:} Bonus Dungeon. Its maximum-level ceiling remains unsettled.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Explodet and Violent Flames. Learns 5 Firevolt,
7 Bounce, 8 Revive, 11 Blazemost, 13 Sparkling Breath, 15 Infernal Flames.
\textbf{Role:} Late magical and breath attacker with reliable revival access at 8.
Plan its listed ability development through 15.

\subsection{Hork}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Santa Rosa Cave's deeper boat-access floors, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Lick and Poison Attack. Learns 10 Cold Breath,
15 Tempting Dance, 20 Poison Breath, 25 Deadly Poison.
\textbf{Role:} High-HP physical and status support companion.

\subsection{Killer Panther: childhood and adult story recruitment}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Alcapa after Lenoire, then the cave west of Kabochi, level 99.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none. Learns 17 Charge Up, 21 War Cry,
33 Freeze Ray, 40 Thunder.
\textbf{Role:} Fast physical damage and later enemy dispelling. Use Bianca's
Ribbon in the Kabochi cave battle for the adult reunion.

\subsection{King Healer}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Sky Tower, level 20.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Healusall and Robmagic. Learns 5 Absorb,
7 Barrier, 10 Revive, 14 Farewell, 17 Chance, 20 Bedragon.
\textbf{Role:} Full-party healing reserve with revival and breath protection.
Healusall costs 36 MP, so monitor reserves closely.

\subsection{King Slime}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Medal King island and Telepador-area encounters, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Vivify. Learns 5 Increase, 10 Barrier,
12 Blazemore, 15 Revive, 18 Freeze Ray, 20 Violent Flames, 27 Blazemost.
\textbf{Role:} Defense, breath protection, revival, and later group damage.

\subsection{Kukkle}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Fields south of the nunnery and Southern Tower approach, level 20.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Decrease. Learns 5 Surround, 10 Sleep,
13 Confuse, 15 Expel, 17 Increase, 18 Curseoff, 19 Stopspell, 20 Bikill.
\textbf{Role:} Early group control with eventual attack and defense support.

\subsection{Leaonar}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Upper Demon Tower floors north of the Northern Church, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none. Learns 5 Surround, 9 Build Up,
12 Decrease, 15 Flame Breath.
\textbf{Role:} Physical damage with group disruption and a developing breath attack.

\subsection{Lionex}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Demon World field routes and Bonus Dungeon, level 23.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Aerocross. Learns 8 Eerie Light,
14 Zap, 20 Lightning.
\textbf{Role:} Strong wind and lightning magic with physical equipment potential.

\subsection{Mad Dragon}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Ned's Inn and southeastern-continent fields, level 40.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none. Learns 6 Sweet Breath, 7 Tempting Dance,
8 Flame Breath, 10 Burning Breath, 13 Deadly Poison, 15 Violent Flames.
\textbf{Role:} Physical and breath damage with several MP-free control options.

\subsection{Magician}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Port Selmi fields, level 99.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Stopspell and Blaze. Learns 5 Firebal,
12 Firebane, 15 Robmagic, 19 Blazemore, 24 Absorb, 28 Firevolt.
\textbf{Role:} Early fire caster with silence and MP-management options.

\subsection{Messala}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Granvania's southern mountain cave, level 50.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Stopspell and Eerie Light. Learns 6 Beat,
11 Sleepmore, 14 Blazemore, 20 Defeat, 22 Curseoff, 27 Freezing Breath,
30 Blazemost.
\textbf{Role:} Status magic, single-target fire, and later cold breath damage.

\subsection{Metal Babble}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Granvania mountain cave and other Metal Babble encounters, level 8.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Firebal and Bang. Learns 3 Upper, 4 Firebane,
5 Boom, 6 Firevolt, 7 Farewell, 8 Explodet.
\textbf{Role:} Very high defense and broad magical protection, with low HP.
HP growth items and careful training help survival against exceptional physical hits.

\subsection{Minidemon}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Granvania's southern mountain cave and Ned's Inn routes, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Blazemore. Learns 4 Lick, 10 Confuse,
13 Sweet Breath, 17 Boom, 20 Day-Night, 23 Chance, 25 Blazemost, 28 Explodet.
\textbf{Role:} Fire and explosion caster with MP-free disruption.

\subsection{Neileus}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Later sea encounters, especially southern waters, level 50.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Snowblast and Tornado. Learns 6 Bounce,
10 Return, 12 Sleep, 17 Vivify, 20 Blizzard, 23 Aerocross, 40 Thunder.
\textbf{Role:} Ice and wind magic, travel, and reserve revival.

\subsection{Orc King}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Granvania fields and trial-route encounters, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Vivify. Learns 6 Beat, 8 Healus,
9 Stepguard, 11 Revive, 15 Defeat, 20 Blizzard.
\textbf{Role:} Early access to reliable revival and party healing, with physical support.

\subsection{Puppet Man}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Ruraphin and Mountain Village approaches, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Strange Dance 2. Learns 6 Bounce,
10 Strange Dance 3, 15 Stopspell, 18 Absorb, 20 Repel, 23 Tempting Dance,
25 Tornado, 30 Aerocross.
\textbf{Role:} MP disruption and spell support, followed by strong wind magic.

\subsection{Slime}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Oracleberry fields and Santa Rosa Cave, level 99.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with none. Learns 7 Expel, 10 Increase,
15 Decrease, 18 Outside, 20 Confuse, 25 Stepguard, 30 Vivify, 45 Eerie Light,
77 Meditate, 99 Infernal Flames.
\textbf{Role:} Inexpensive early support and travel utility, with distant high-level rewards.

\subsection{Slime Knight}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Reinhart fields and nearby dungeon encounters, level 99.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Heal and Robmagic. Learns 5 Healmore,
9 Antidote, 12 Bang, 18 Numboff, 20 Healall, 23 Boom.
\textbf{Role:} Durable sword-and-armor attacker with single-target recovery.

\subsection{Soldier Bull}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Emblem Cave and northern mining-route encounters, level 30.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Charge Up. Learns 5 Upper, 10 Bikill,
15 Decrease.
\textbf{Role:} Physical attacker with a compact attack and defense support list.

\subsection{Springer}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Boble island and Bonus Dungeon, level 50.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Decrease and Upper. Learns 7 Tornado,
15 Aerocross.
\textbf{Role:} Armored physical and wind-magic hybrid.

\subsection{Wyvern}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Salabona fields and volcano encounters, level 60.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with Healmore. Learns 5 Cold Breath, 10 Sleep,
12 Outside, 15 Healall, 18 Healus, 22 Freezing Breath, 24 Return,
28 Sleepmore, 31 Confuse.
\textbf{Role:} Versatile healing, cold breath, and field-travel reserve.

\subsection{Yeti}
\textbf{Region and cap:} Reinhart fields and basement encounters, level 40.
\textbf{Abilities:} Starts with War Cry. Learns 5 Cold Breath, 8 Sweet Breath,
12 Build Up, 15 Snowblast, 20 Lick, 22 Freezing Breath, 30 Blizzard.
\textbf{Role:} Early cold and control support, followed by stronger group damage.

\section{Equipment groups, resistance comparisons, and party roles}
Use these practical equipment families when distributing purchases and spare
gear. The Equip menu determines the compatible pieces for each individual.
Species protection, armor protection, defense, HP, and agility all contribute
to survival. Compare the expected attack type before assigning a front-line slot.

\toprule
\GuideTableHeading{Family} & \GuideTableHeading{Companion examples} & \GuideTableHeading{Equipment and role comparison} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Sword and armor & Slime Knight, Attackbot, Golem, Springer, Soldier Bull, Lionex & Strong physical weapons and compatible protective armor support sustained front-line damage. \\
Heavy physical & Brownie, Eliminator, Gigantes, Orc King, Eigerhorn, Hellbattler & Compare individual weapon and armor compatibility. Prioritize HP and reliable damage or support. \\
Fang and claw & Panther, Dragon Pup, Mad Dragon, Great Dragon & Fang and claw weapons support physical damage. Breath tiers provide independent group attacks. \\
Small and flying & Slime, Drakee, Wyvern, Blizzard Hawk, Kukkle & Check light weapons and clothing individually. Recovery, breath damage, and utility can determine equipment priorities. \\
Recovery casters & Healer, Curer, King Healer, King Slime & Protective equipment and agility help healing arrive safely. Preserve MP for the chosen recovery tier. \\
Magic specialists & Magician, Minidemon, Neileus, Messala, Dancing Jewel & Compatible robes, hats, and reusable casting items reduce spell expenditure and support survival. \\
Control and bulky bodies & Big Eye, Yeti, Hork, Puppet Man, Dancing Needle, Centaurus, Leaonar & Compare individual weapon and armor access. Assign control turns alongside a reliable damage dealer. \\
Sacrificial bodies & Bomb Baby, Bomb Crag, Farewell Crag & Survival equipment preserves the emergency action. Recoil and sacrifice require a recovery plan. \\
Metal body & Metal Babble & High natural defense and broad magic protection accompany low HP. Critical physical hits remain a survival concern. \\
