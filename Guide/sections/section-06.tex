The western voyage brings the Hero to a busy port and a countryside

Several valuable free finds lie along the route, and the town shops
offer enough choice to improve the party gradually as those discoveries
and rewards arrive.

\section{Port Selmi: the tavern confrontation and Kabochi request}

\subsection*{The harbor and its useful services}

The voyage from Vista ends at Port Selmi. From the landing, walk north
to the lighthouse. A local warning gives its resident an alarming
reputation, but the building can be explored safely. Climb to the
room at the top of the ladder and search the dresser for a
\textbf{Small Medal}.

Monster Gramps has a branch south of the lighthouse. Use it to adjust
the travelling roster if Henry's departure left a gap in the active
party on a recruitment route. For \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help},
keep Hero alone until the ribbon reunion. The church's basement provides a depository, and the inn gives
the party a convenient place to recover.

\subsection*{Flower searches and the inn's treasures}

A resident recommends searching the flowers around the church.
Search the \textbf{eastern flower patch} for another
\textbf{Small Medal} and the \textbf{western flower patch} for
\textbf{100 G}. Stand on the relevant square and use Search.

At the inn, the upstairs western room has a pot containing
\textbf{50 G}. The stage leads north into the dancers' dressing room,
where a dresser contains a \textbf{Silk Apron}. Check these rooms
while exploring, and leave the locked chests for their later access.

These five finds are worth collecting before the next countryside trip.
The two cash searches contribute to supplies, and the clothing may
be useful to an eligible companion or worth keeping in the depository.

\subsection*{The two bandits and the village's request}

On \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, fight this encounter with the human
Hero alone. Restore his HP and MP, equip the best available armor, and
carry Medical Herbs. Use manageable local battles and inn recovery to
prepare if his current equipment or level leaves him vulnerable.

Restore the active party before entering the inn's eastern tavern area.
Two bandits are harassing a peasant who is trying to protect his village's
money. Speak to the man and confront the bandits to begin a battle with
\textbf{two Bandit Wolves}.

They are ordinary enemies for a party that has completed Reinhart.
Use the Hero's attacks and suitable group damage, keeping recovery
available if the party arrived injured. A \textbf{Copper Sword}
can appear as a battle-item reward.

After the victory, accept the peasant's request. A large creature is
destroying the fields of \textbf{Kabochi}, a village to the south.
He offers \textbf{3,000 G} for resolving the problem and gives
\textbf{1,500 G} immediately. He will meet the party at the village.

\subsection*{Purchase guide: Port Selmi}

western cave contains a Stone Axe and Iron Helm, and its story reward
provides a powerful weapon for the Hero. Buy enough equipment to reach
those finds comfortably, with the greatest attention on companions
whose present defenses or weapons are weak.

\section{Kabochi and the West Kabochi Cave}

\subsection*{The damaged fields and village searches}

Follow the southern road into the mountain country around Kabochi.
Arriving at night brings a glimpse of a large animal in the fields.
It runs away when approached. Stay at the inn, then speak to the
villagers in daylight to hear what the crop losses have meant for
their households.

Search the village thoroughly before setting out:
\begin{description}
  \item[Armband of Sacrifice:] Push aside the plate or slab on the
    ground near the water, descend the hidden stairs, and open the chest.
    An equipped wearer who falls in battle triggers its explosion against
    ordinary enemies. The effect does not work on bosses, so choose its
    use as a deliberate part of a regular-encounter plan.
  \item[Strength Seed:] The pot in the house across the small bridge,
    near the boy and his grandmother.
  \item[Leather Hat:] A barrel in the storage building or barn.
  \item[Magic Water:] A pot upstairs in the elder's house.
  \item[Small Medal:] Another upstairs pot in the same house.
  \item[Small Medal:] Search the upper-left corner of the underground
    general store and church beneath the grassy mound.
\end{description}

The elder's house is recognizable by the horses outside. Speak to the
man who hired the party and the elder. Their discussion reveals how
anxiously the village has committed its savings to the task. The elder
says the creature appears to come from the west.

\subsection*{The skeleton's medal and the Stone Axe}

Leave Kabochi and follow the mountains west to the cave. From the
entrance, take the eastern passage and turn south at the junction.
The remains of a campfire and a skeleton provide a landmark for a
ground search.

Stand \textbf{one square north of the skeleton} and use Search for
a \textbf{Small Medal}. Take the western branch nearby to the chest
containing a \textbf{Stone Axe}, then return and use the southern
stairs to descend.

The Stone Axe provides 20 attack for an eligible physical companion.
Assign it where useful and keep the active members recovered while
exploring the next floor's bridges and drops.

\subsection*{The fallen traveller and free Iron Helm}

From the arrival stairs, work west across the bridges, then north and
east toward the traveller. Speaking to him startles him into falling
to the floor below. Follow the drop and speak to him until he explains
the dangerous creature deeper in the cave.

Search the southern chest on that lower floor for an \textbf{Iron Helm}.
Equip a suitable companion, then use the northern stairs to return to
the bridge level. Review the floor connections before choosing the
route into the final chamber.

\subsection*{The optional Man-Eater Chest}

A chest on the western side of the bridge floor is a
\textbf{Man-Eater Chest}. Opening it starts an optional battle.
It has about \textbf{90 HP} and can use dangerous magic, including
Beat's instant-death effect.

Approach with a healthy active party and available revival if pursuing
the encounter. Concentrate suitable attacks on the chest and handle
urgent recovery promptly. A \textbf{Small Medal} can appear as its
battle-item reward.

Continue around the southern and eastern route to the onward stairs.
The final chamber contains the beast and the sword it has been guarding.

\subsection{Bianca's Ribbon and reunion with the Killer Panther}

Approach the \textbf{Killer Panther}. The encounter has familiar behavior:
the animal watches the Hero, reacts uncertainly, and seems to be trying
to remember something.

Choose the Hero's \textbf{Item} command and use
\textbf{Bianca's Ribbon}. Its scent restores the recognition, and
the battle ends with an affectionate reunion. Speak to the panther
afterward to bring the childhood companion back into the travelling party.

\subsection{Papas' Sword, the village reward, and available recruits}

Search the sword the panther was guarding to collect
\textbf{Papas' Sword}. The crest identifies it as his father's weapon.
Its \textbf{40 attack} makes a valuable focused weapon for the Hero.
Keep the Boomerang or Bladed Boomerang available for ordinary formations
when that attack pattern remains useful.

The cave's fixed finds are the skeleton's Small Medal, Stone Axe,
Iron Helm, and Papas' Sword. The optional Man-Eater Chest has its own
possible medal reward. \textbf{Big Eye} is also a recruitment prospect
here, providing another developing companion for the roster.

Return to Kabochi and speak to the elder. The villagers are frightened
to see the party accompanied by the same creature and suspect that
the job was arranged from the beginning. Collect the remaining
\textbf{1,500 G} reward, completing the agreed \textbf{3,000 G}
payment, then leave with the panther.

\section{Ruraphin: Benett, the Lunar Herb, and learning Return}

\subsection*{A town of stairs, tunnels, and smoke}

Travel west from Port Selmi toward Ruraphin. Its connected walkways
and tunnels take a little exploration to understand. The inn is a
convenient starting point, and the large building north of it leads
to the tavern, church, and depository.

Speak to the residents while visiting both day and night. They describe
\textbf{Benett}, also called Bennet, and the smoke from his experiments.
His work concerns a spell that can transport a traveller back to places
already visited.

\subsection*{The seven town finds}

Collect the objects around the town as the route opens:
\begin{description}
  \item[Small Medal:] A pot upstairs in the inn.
  \item[Wing of Wyvern:] The barrel behind the bar, reached by going
    upstairs and descending the other staircase.
  \item[Acorn of Magic:] The barrel upstairs in the tavern during
    the nighttime visit.
  \item[Agility Seed:] A pot in the small sunken dwelling or pit area.
  \item[Small Medal:] Another pot in that same sunken area.
  \item[Medical Herb:] A pot in Benett's house.
  \item[Magic Water:] A barrel in Benett's house.
\end{description}

The Acorn can help the Hero's MP reserve, especially once the travel
spell is available. Give permanent stat increases to companions whose
roles make good use of them, and keep the Magic Water for a demanding
expedition.

\subsection*{Finding Benett and the glowing herb}

From the inn, follow the western route around the large northern building
and use the connecting stairs and tunnel to reach the remaining houses.
The weapon-and-armor shop is on the southeastern side of that area.
The other house, with smoke rising from it, belongs to Benett.

Speak to him and answer no when he asks whether the Hero has come to
complain about the smoke. Agree to help and follow him upstairs.
He shows the party a map of the \textbf{Rura Moon Herb}, the ingredient
needed to complete the travel spell.

The herb shines at night on the peninsula west of town. Leave Ruraphin,
climb the western steps, cross the bridge, and descend on the other
side. Continue south toward the peninsula's tip. Use the Lamp of Darkness
if daylight has returned, then stand on the glowing patch and Search.
Collect the \textbf{Rura Moon Herb}.

\subsection*{Learning Return and buying the map}

Bring the herb to Benett upstairs. Follow him back to the experiment
and finish the conversation after the burst of magic. The Hero learns
\textbf{Return}, costing \textbf{8 MP} per use.

Cast it once when Benett asks for the test. An available destination
such as Oracleberry demonstrates the result and makes the next errand
convenient. Return now changes the rhythm of the journey by putting
visited towns within a short spell trip.

\section{Salabona: Ludman, Flora, and the wedding prerequisites}

\subsection*{The puppy and the town searches}

A friendly dog runs up as the Hero enters Salabona. Its owner catches
up and thanks him, surprised by the puppy's enthusiasm. This is
\textbf{Flora}, the young woman at the center of the town's current
excitement.

Explore before gathering at the mansion:
\begin{description}
  \item[Iron Shield:] The dresser in the inn. Assign it to an eligible
    member who needs the protection.
  \item[Small Medal:] The dresser in Andy's house south of the inn
    and entrance area.
  \item[Intelligence Seed:] The dresser in Ludman's small guesthouse
    on the southwestern side of town, surrounded by water.
  \item[Silver Barrette:] The dresser in Flora's upstairs room in
    Ludman's mansion, accessible after the first meeting.
\end{description}

The free \textbf{Iron Shield} is particularly useful for the next
dungeon. Its protection helps an eligible wearer face physical damage
and elemental attacks. Review the active party's shields before buying
another expensive defensive piece.

\subsection*{Ludman's offer}

Enter the mansion and speak to the waiting suitors, including
\textbf{Andy}, who has known Flora since childhood. When Ludman
calls everyone in, listen to his request.

He wants a prospective husband to recover two magic rings, the
\textbf{Ring of Flames} and \textbf{Ring of Water}. The successful
traveller will also receive his family's heirloom shield. Flora objects
to the danger involved, but Ludman continues with the arrangements.

Speak to the family upstairs after the meeting and collect the Silver
Barrette. Ludman points toward a lava-filled cave southeast of Salabona
for the first ring. The two gift chests in the mansion remain locked
until their later handover.

\section{Volcano Cave: route, hazards, treasure, and the Ring of Flames}

\subsection*{The approach and 450 G}

\subsection*{The trapped chest, staff, and Iron Helm}

From the lower-floor arrival, follow the northern and eastern route.

\textbf{Man-Eater Chest}. Its dangerous spell and possible Small Medal
reward deserve the same preparation as the earlier trapped chest.

The isolated chest across the lava contains a
\textbf{Staff of Antimagic}. Use Stepguard before crossing when the

and heal before taking another encounter. The walkable lava patches
remove HP as the party moves.

spell-using enemies and the party changes ahead.

lava route to the chest containing \textbf{1,500 G}.

The central northern route leads to two stair choices. The lower or
southeastern branch reaches a small \textbf{healing chamber}. Step
on the center tile to restore the active party's HP and MP.

Rotate reserves into the active lineup and use the tile again to
recover them too. This makes the chamber a valuable base while
finishing the treasure or preparing for the guardians. The other
stair route continues toward the ring.

The volcano also has useful recruitment prospects. A Beast Master
can call a \textbf{Curer}, and \textbf{Dancing Jewel} can appear

\subsection{Lava Men battle and sustainable healing}

Use the remaining upward or northern branch from the lower junction
to descend toward the ring chamber. Approach the \textbf{Ring of Flames}
only after assigning the intended active members and recovery supplies.
Examining it begins the battle with \textbf{three Lava Savages}, also
called Lava Men, with about \textbf{400 HP each}.

The group puts sustained pressure on the whole party through fire
breath and ordinary attacks. Focus physical damage on one Savage at
a time. Each defeated guardian reduces the number of dangerous actions
the party has to absorb in later rounds.

Use cold attacks where suitable. Wyvern and Yeti learn
\textbf{Cold Breath at level 5}, and Yeti gains
\textbf{Snowblast at level 15}. Check those abilities before assigning
the attacking member. The guardians' fire
resistance makes physical and cold actions practical offensive choices.

Increase or Upper helps with physical blows. Protection from fire
breath and equipment resistance address the other pressure. Keep the
healing plan ready even when a support action has been successful.

The Hero and a Slime Knight can share single-target healing. A suitable
group-healing companion provides another answer when breath has injured
all three active members. Rotate an exhausted or badly injured member
out when an available reserve is better prepared to continue.

If several members are vulnerable, spend the round restoring the group
and protecting the most threatened companion. Resume focused damage
when the party has enough HP to handle the next sequence of attacks.
The healing chamber has supplied a full reserve roster for that purpose.

Winning awards \textbf{1,050 experience} and \textbf{750 G}.

Outside. Restore the party and return to Ludman during the day.
