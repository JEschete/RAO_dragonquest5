\section{Oracleberry casino access, coins, stakes, and prize exchange}

Oracleberry's casino is available from early adulthood and remains open
at night. Buy coins at the entrance for \textbf{20 G each}. Reserve the
\textbf{300 G wagon purchase}, equipment, inn fees, and revival money
before funding wagers. Coins buy the six prizes in 21.5. See 5.3.2.

\section{Ordinary-play budgeting versus optional emulator-assisted methods}

\begin{achievementsetup}{Ordinary achievement attempts}
Use ordinary game saves, the casino's normal controls, and earned coins
for the achievement route. Keep emulator-state trials separate from
Hardcore progress and the two goals whose requirements say no cheating.
Preserve the normal pre-marriage and pre-boss church files before any
optional experiment.
\end{achievementsetup}

\begin{reftable}{P{0.25\linewidth}P{0.63\linewidth}}
\toprule
\GuideTableHeading{Choice} & \GuideTableHeading{Compact procedure} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Ordinary play & Save at a church, reserve travel and recovery gold, purchase a bounded coin allowance, and stop at the chosen loss limit or prize target. \\
Optional save/load trial & Keep the church save. Place an arena wager, save a separate state before battle, watch the result, and manually load that state to retry a loss. Finish the trial after a chosen number of attempts. See 22.10. \\
Result to check & Confirm the actual coin balance and exchanged prize in the adventure being kept. Repeatedly loading an identical outcome can repeat that outcome. \\

\section{Banks, retained valuables, sale decisions, and late-game funding}

\begin{routecheck}[breakable=false]{Bank and purchase checks}
\begin{itemize}
\item \(\square\) Deposit spare gold in 1,000 G increments before dangerous travel. Banked gold survives defeat.
\item \(\square\) Retain Bianca's Ribbon, keys, rings, travel objects, reusable healing tools, and collection equipment.
\item \(\square\) Retrieve equipment deposited with stored companions before buying a duplicate.
\item \(\square\) Check eligible wearers and the shop's actual sale quote before selling a spare item.
\item \(\square\) Equip the full intended boss roster, fund recovery, then spend the remaining late-game budget.
\end{itemize}
\end{routecheck}
