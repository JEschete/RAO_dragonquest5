\subsection{Single-target weapons and character restrictions}
Ordinary swords, axes, fangs, claws, hammers, and staves attack one target unless an effect is specified. The Chain Sickle belongs in this single-target group. The Zenithian Sword belongs to the Son. The Dragon Staff belongs to the Hero. Beast weapons and human weapons have distinct eligibility, which the Equip menu checks per character. A high attack value alone does not establish eligibility.

\subsection{Whips, boomerangs, repeated attacks, and unusual damage effects}
Whips and the Morning Star strike an enemy group. Boomerangs and the Iron Ball of Decimation strike across the enemy formation. Falcon Sword and Killer Earring attack twice. Poison Needle and Demon Spear offer instant-death chances, which bosses and resistant targets can defeat. Sword of Miracles returns part of inflicted damage as HP. Multi-Edge Sword also injures its user. Dragon Killer and Zombie Killer specialize against their named target families. Compare practical damage over a full turn, especially against armored enemies.

\subsection{Weapon-cast spells and reusable support tools}
Carry Staff of Benediction for Healmore, Staff of Revival for Vivify, Staff of Stoross for paralysis recovery, Faerie Sword for Upper, and Zenithian Sword for dispelling enemy effects. Offensive tools include Magma Staff, Staff of Punishment, Staff of Thunder, Staff of Antimagic, Ice Blade, Flame Claw, Sword of Malice, and Spear of Zeus. Dragon Staff's Bedragon changes the user's battle role. Keep a healer available while it is used.

\section{Cursed equipment, drawbacks, removal, and sale restrictions}
Rogue Armor removes reliable control. Demon Armor is cursed, with its 85 defense distinct from ordinary 65-defense Devil's Armor. Multi-Edge Sword returns damage to the user. Sword of Decimation, also encountered under Doom Sword naming, is a cursed group weapon. Shield of Ruin reduces defense to zero. Satan Helm is cursed. Use a church's curse-removal service before trying to replace cursed equipment. Check the inventory afterward before budgeting a replacement or a sale. A missing buy price does not establish a sale restriction. Story and Zenithian pieces should be retained for their quests and assigned users. Exact sale prices and item-by-item destruction rules are unconfirmed.

\section{Elemental protection, status protection, and special properties}
For fire-heavy routes, compare Water Flying Clothes, Dragon Mail, Magic Shield, Dragon Shield, and Shield of Reflection. For cold-heavy routes, compare Angel's Leotard, Flame Armor, Flame Shield, and Dragon Shield. Royal Cloak, Zenithian Armor, and Princess Robe offer broader protection. Spell-focused defenses include Sage's Robe, Wizard's Robe, Magic Armor, Dress of Radiance, and Robe of Darkness. Mirror Armor reflects some spells, so keep item-based recovery available when assigning it. Golden Tiara, Elven Amulet, and King Metal Shield help against status ailments. Barrier remains useful against dangerous breaths. Mystic Armor's round recovery, Hat of Happiness's walking MP recovery, and Echoing Hat's repeated casting each address different resource demands.

\section{Casino prizes, Medal King rewards, unique finds, and monster drops}
Casino equipment includes Killer Earring at 10,000 C, King Metal Sword at 50,000 C, and Gringham Whip at 250,000 C. The Armband of Sacrifice costs 5,000 C. Recovery prizes include Elven Medicine and Leaf of the World Tree. Medal purchases are Combat Pajamas at 5 M, Mysterious Bolero at 10 M, Sword of Miracles at 16 M, Mystic Armor at 21 M, Falcon Sword at 30 M, and King Metal Shield at 43 M. Each exchange spends the medals. Golden Tiara is in the barred treasury.

Unique endgame finds include Royal Cloak in Emblem Cave, Dragon Staff in Boble, Princess Robe in Faerie Palace, and King Metal Helm and Orihalcon Fang in Evil Mountain. Bonus Dungeon adds King Metal Armor, Shield of Radiance, Hell Sabre, Iron Ball of Decimation, and the cursed Sword of Decimation. Random alternatives include Metal Dragon's King Metal Armor, Mechaban's Orihalcon Fang, Hellbattler's Hell Sabre, Lionex's Shield of Darkness, and Evil Clown's Echoing Hat. Chapter~\ref{written-bestiary} gives the complete drop reverse index.
