\section{Overworld map, region names, and location index}
The central continent links Santa Rosa, Alcapa, Reinhart, Oracleberry, and Vista Port. Port Selmi opens the western road through Kabochi and Ruraphin to Salabona. The southern desert leads to Telepador, while the eastern mountain crossing leads through Chizod to Granvania. El Heven occupies the northeastern landmass. The Demon World has its own field route between the ring portal, Shahanna, and Evil Mountain.

For travel commands and recovery, see Chapter~\ref{written-exploration}. The route descriptions in Chapters~\ref{written-childhood} and~\ref{written-escape} cover the early central-continent crossings.

\section{Town and settlement profiles}
\subsection{Vista Port, Santa Rosa, and Alcapa / Alkapa}
\textbf{Vista Port:} Central southern harbor, opening voyage and adult crossing to Port Selmi. The opening ship has one cabin Medical Herb. \textbf{Santa Rosa:} Childhood home and cave base, with Sancho's free rest and church. Household finds are Leather Hat, two Medical Herbs, and Strength Seed. The rescued herbalist later offers Handwoven Cape. Adult ruins add a village Intelligence Seed, the lower cave, and later weapon stock. \textbf{Alcapa:} West of Santa Rosa. Inn dressers hold Defense Seed and Wing of Wyvern in childhood, an adult pot holds Small Medal. The Hero and Bianca alone can revisit the inn's marriage scene. Stock and searches: 4.1--4.4, 4.6, 5.4--5.5, 8.8.

\subsection{Reinhart, Nameless Nunnery, and Oracleberry}
\textbf{Reinhart:} Northeast of Santa Rosa. Childhood chapel dresser has Acorn of Magic. Adult boat entry, prison, key, treasury, and mirror event restore the castle. Henry's later family visit gives Hat of the Wind and guarded treasure errands. \textbf{Nameless Nunnery:} Southeast coast, free recovery and saving after escape, Maria's tower-access stop, and 1,000 G gift. \textbf{Oracleberry:} Southern central-continent hub with casino, deposits, and monster storage. Search for Silk Robe, Silver Tiara, Monster Lure, Pot Cover, and Defense Seed. Buy wagon for 300 G at night, then Wayfarer's Map for 1,000 G on the merchant's later offer. Final Key opens the prison medal search. Sections 4.10, 5.2--5.3, 5.6--5.9, 8.4, 9.10.

\subsection{Port Selmi, Kabochi, and Ruraphin}
\textbf{Port Selmi:} Western harbor and roster hub. Church flowers hold Small Medal and 100 G, lighthouse dresser Small Medal, inn searches Silk Apron and 50 G. Kabochi request pays 1,500 G in advance. \textbf{Kabochi:} Farming village south of port, daytime underground shop and church. Elder's pot and shop corner each hold a medal. Hidden stairs near water lead to Armband of Sacrifice, other searches give Strength Seed, Leather Hat, and Magic Water. Ribbon identifies the panther in the western cave, completion pays another 1,500 G. \textbf{Ruraphin:} Northwest road town. Two medals, Wing of Wyvern, Acorn of Magic, Agility Seed, Medical Herb, and Magic Water accompany Benett's nighttime experiment. Lunar Herb lies southwest on the field. Later visits offer Chance and an optional 3,000 G book. Sections 6.1--6.4, 8.8, 9.12.

\subsection{Salabona and Mountain Village}
\textbf{Salabona:} Southwestern marriage base. Search inn Iron Shield, Andy's dresser medal, guesthouse Intelligence Seed, and Flora's Silver Barrette. Wedding gifts include Zenithian Shield and 2,000 G. The later jar investigation leads to Buorn and Final Key. \textbf{Mountain Village:} North of Salabona beside the river gate. Bianca and Duncan occupy the northern home. Search grave and inn-basement barrel medals, Strength Seed, and Medical Herb. The Demon Jar is a separate encounter. Bianca opens the gate, the village supplies the Silk Veil. Stock and event order: 6.5, 6.7, 6.9, 9.9.

\subsection{Telepador, desert stops, and Medal King Castle}
\textbf{Telepador:} Southern desert castle west of the Oasis. Wing of Wyvern, Defense Seed, and queen's well medal are placed finds. Kitchen Demon Jar is an encounter. Isis tests the Hero with Zenithian Helmet, the Son later receives it. \textbf{Oasis:} Coastal desert recovery stop and pot medal. \textbf{Medal King Castle:} Island east of Telepador, prize exchange and depository. Final Key opens two medal chests and Golden Tiara. Flora's courier supplies 2,000 G on the desert leg. Sections 7.1--7.3, 8.9, 9.10.

\subsection{Chizod / Chizot, mountain stops, and Granvania}
\textbf{Ned's Inn:} Eastern coastal landing, rest and save before the mountain. \textbf{Chizod:} Bridge village between ascent and descent. Mandatory overnight scene, shops and church. Finds are Monster Lure, Fighter's Hair, and Small Medal. \textbf{Granvania:} Royal home beyond descent, with shops, inn, church, depository, and later human and monster roster services. Sancho's house holds Acorn of Life and Boxer Shorts. Castle finds include Stone of Life, Lace Bisuche, Defense Seed, Intelligence Seed, Strength Seed, and three medals. Walk through the wall beside the stairs for the concealed Ring of Prayer, Naughty Underwear, and Hat of Happiness chests. Chancellor's dresser yields Flying Shoes after kidnapping. Magic Key later opens 3,000 G and Meteorite Armband. Sections 7.4--7.10, 8.1--8.2, 8.8.

\subsection{Northern Church, small shrines, and El Heven}
\textbf{Northern Church:} Flying Shoes arrival base with rest, saving, and stronger weapons. \textbf{Rumor Shrine:} Roadside recovery and saving south of Ruraphin, well medal. \textbf{Farthest Shrine:} Northern continent's eastern peninsula, Intelligence Seed pot, bucket-side ground medal, downstairs Stone of Life. \textbf{Gigo's house:} Southeastern island, mother's dresser medal. \textbf{El Heven:} Northern enclosed lake town entered through Water Shrine. Old couple's pots hold Defense Seed and medal, lower paths hold Magic Key and Magic Carpet. Speak to four elders. Sections 6.4, 8.5--8.7.

\subsection{Faerie Village, Faerie Palace, and Zenithian Castle}
\textbf{Faerie Village:} Childhood stair access, Holy Water pot, shops, inn, and church. Adult forest route requires a child, Powan supplies Horn, armor stock improves. \textbf{Faerie Palace:} Lake north of Sky Tower, small boat to lily pad and Horn reveal. Church, Staff of Thunder and Princess Robe basement chests, Queen's Glowing Orb, and painting exchange. \textbf{Zenithian Castle:} Mines arrival and Return point. Restored Orb opens flight, grandson's dresser medal, fireplace-room Grappling Hook, and western pool's replenishable Sap of the World Tree. Sections 4.7, 9.4--9.8.

\subsection{Shahanna and the Demon World}
\textbf{Arrival shrine:} Ring portal terminus, Sage's Stone gift and blue return portal. \textbf{Shahanna:} East across bridges from arrival, local rest, saving, depository, and Monster Gramps. Magic lock protects the weapon-shop entrance. Search town Defense Seed, inn Elven Medicine, outer barrel medal, and guarded Sands of Time chest. To reach Evil Mountain, leave south, follow coast north, cross bridges, and continue northeast past swamp. Sections 10.1--10.3.
