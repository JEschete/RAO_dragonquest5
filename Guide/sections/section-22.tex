rules. The six-item critical/recruitment configuration and early-patch trap
repetition belong on a separate trial file. Use normal combat and training
for That's Got To Hurt and Underworld Challenge, whose requirements
explicitly say no cheating.

\section{Version-specific Mimic encounters}

\begin{routecheck}[breakable=false]{Optional early-patch trap trial}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\square\) Keep a separate church save before a known trapped chest.
\item \(\square\) Win one encounter and record its actual reward.
\item \(\square\) Leave the room, re-enter, and examine the same chest once.
\item \(\square\) Record whether another battle occurs. A critical-hit finish can end early-patch repetition.
\item \(\square\) End the trial and retain the intended save. v2.01F repetition is unconfirmed.
\end{enumerate}
\end{routecheck}

Known locations include West Kabochi Cave, Volcano Cave, the eastern
mountain, Demon Tower, Boble Tower, Evil Mountain, and the Bonus Dungeon.
Use their trapped-container entries in 23.4. Bonus has one placed medal
and two distinct Mimic encounters, 11.2.

\section{Emulator save-state casino methods, clearly separated from normal play}

\begin{routecheck}[breakable=false]{Opt-in manual arena save/load}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\square\) Save the adventure at a church and retain that file.
\item \(\square\) Choose one arena board, contestant, and affordable coin stake.
\item \(\square\) Save a separate state after the wager and before the battle.
\item \(\square\) Watch the result. Manually load the trial state after a loss if continuing.
\item \(\square\) Stop after the chosen attempt limit, confirm the coin balance, exchange the intended prize, and save the adventure being kept.
\end{enumerate}
\end{routecheck}

An identical restored state can produce an identical result. Choose a
new wager or end the trial when repeats continue. The ordinary casino
rules and prize costs remain in 21.1--21.5.
