\section{The Hero: childhood, adulthood, and event-learned spells}
The Hero is available throughout the playable journey, with story intervals
separating the three generations. Strong weapons, substantial HP, single-target
healing, and wind magic make him a flexible front-line member. His natural
spell progression continues across generations. Return comes from Benett's
experiment in Ruraphin, and a later visit there adds Chance.

\section{Bianca: childhood carryover and the adult marriage route}
Bianca accompanies the Lenoire expedition during childhood and rejoins at the
Mountain Village for the Waterfall Cave journey. Her childhood development
carries into the reunion. Choosing her at the wedding makes her the permanent
wife. Bikill, group fire spells, and later Blazemost support a magical role.
Use whips for routine groups and protect her HP when assigning a casting turn.

\section{Flora: second-generation restrictions and later progression}
Flora joins at level 10 after her marriage route. During the second generation
she remains at level 10 and acts under automatic control. Her level progression
and ordinary party control become available in the third generation. Healmore
is available at joining, and her later list includes Bikill, Blazemost, and
Explodet. Ludman's gifts include 2,000 G and Water Flying Clothes on the sea
journey. Budget her second-generation equipment around that delivery.

\section{The Son: Zenithian equipment, support magic, and combat role}
The Son joins at level 5 after the Hero's restoration. He can equip the
Zenithian Sword, Shield, Helmet, and Armor as each becomes available. Physical
damage, Increase, Barrier at 23, Revive at 27, and Healus at 30 give him a
front-line support role. Carry the Zenithian Shield for its Bounce effect and
the Zenithian Sword for its battle dispelling effect.

\section{The Daughter: offensive magic, support, and equipment}
The Daughter joins at level 5 with Surround and Return. Her progression
combines ice and explosion spells with Bikill at 16, Decrease at 22, and
Bounce at 27. Keep a durable physical attacker beside her to exploit Bikill.
Prioritize MP, protective clothing, and reusable casting equipment.

\section{Sancho: recruitment, utility, and party role}
Recruit Sancho through Ruida's tavern in Granvania during the third generation.
His level-20 spells are Increase and Sleep. Later Robmagic, Beat, Sacrifice,
and Confuse provide situational utility. His substantial HP and heavy weapons
support physical combat. Equip agility and protection according to whether he
will attack, cast Increase, or use a recovery item.

\section{Pippin: recruitment, equipment, and physical combat}
Recruit Pippin through Ruida's tavern in Granvania during the third generation.
He learns no spells or special skills. His role depends on physical equipment,
HP, and carried items. A strong weapon and reusable healing item let him
contribute damage and emergency recovery without MP expenditure.

\section{Temporary and AI-controlled companions}
These companions have individual joining events and control rules. Their
equipment and spells matter within the period in which they travel with the Hero.

\subsection{Papas}
Papas is the Hero's father and accompanies selected childhood journeys,
including the initial road and the Reinhart rescue. He fights autonomously,
delivers powerful physical attacks, and heals the Hero after escorted battles.
His fixed level is 27, with Healmore. The Reinhart events end his party access.

\subsection{Bella / Vera}
Bella accompanies the childhood faerie-realm expedition and leaves when spring
is restored. Her fixed level is 7. Heal, Decrease, Firebal, and Surround give
the small expedition healing and group support. Her temporary availability
ends before the Hero resumes his journey with Papas.

\subsection{Henry}
Henry joins during the childhood Reinhart rescue and travels with the Hero
after their escape from captivity in the second generation. He combines
physical attacks with Blaze, Decrease, Bang, and control magic. He leaves after
the False Queen sequence to remain in Reinhart. His deposited belongings can
be collected before departure, and later castle visits continue his family story.
