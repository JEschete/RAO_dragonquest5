\chapter{Second Generation: Escape and Reinhart}
\label{written-escape}

\AchievementSetup{61418}{I Don't Want Your Help}{\textbf{Missable.} From
Oracleberry onward, accept no wild monster until the adult Kitty reunion
with Bianca's Ribbon in Section 6.2. The safest route defers the wagon
purchase until after that reunion, then returns to Oracleberry. Travel
with Hero and Henry through Reinhart, then with Hero alone after Henry
leaves. Accepting a wild recruit permanently closes this route, even if
the monster is later released. The childhood story panther is allowed.}

Ten years pass after the childhood journey. The Hero and Henry have
grown up together in captivity, carrying the memory of Papas and the
hope of another escape. Their friendship gives the next journey its
starting point, and Henry now understands much more of what the Hero
lost while trying to help him.

\Achievement{61363}{Ten Years Later}{3}{Become a teenager}{Continue
through the ten-year transition until the teenage Hero is controllable
at the slavery camp.}

The first task is to regain their freedom. Once they reach the coast,
the two companions can rebuild their equipment, explore the towns to
the north, and begin travelling with a wider party.

\section{Captivity, Maria, Henry, and escape from the Great Temple}

\subsection*{A day at the construction site}

The Hero begins in the work area with very little HP and his belongings
confiscated. Move around the site and speak to the workers and guards.
The boulders and laboring prisoners establish the routine of the temple,
and the conversations explain the Order of Light's hold over the place.

Take the eastern stairs through the upper work areas. Speak to Henry,
recognizable by his green hair, and continue toward the northwest stairs.
A guard there mentions his sister. Follow the southern doorway through
the graveyard and the nearby prison area, then return to the temple roof.
The day's work comes to an end, and the prisoners are sent to sleep.

\AchievementSetup{61365}{What Did You Expect to Find?}{\textbf{Missable.}
Examine the sleeping-quarters pot during this overnight interval, before
returning to work and beginning the escape sequence.}

The Hero wakes with his HP and MP restored. Speak to Henry and the other
prisoners, including \textbf{Maria} in the eastern part of the sleeping
area. She has also been imprisoned and has begun to understand the
suffering caused by the Order.

Search the \textbf{pot in the sleeping quarters} before leaving the room.

\Achievement{61365}{What Did You Expect to Find?}{2}{Examine the pot in the sleeping quarters at the slavery camp.}{\textbf{Missable.}
Search the bedroom pot now, while the camp's sleeping quarters remain
accessible. Finish this search before the return to work and escape.}

After the conversations, a guard orders the prisoners back to work.
Return through the upper areas to the original construction floor.
Maria is being punished by the overseers, and Henry steps forward to
help her. Approach the remaining overseer to begin the battle.

\subsection*{The two Whip Men}

The Hero and Henry fight without their stored weapons and armor.
The Whip Men can restore HP and drain MP with their dance, so make
productive use of the Hero's available spells early in the battle.

\textbf{Gale} reaches their group and is an effective opening attack.
The Hero's \textbf{Upper} can help protect a threatened companion,
and Heal supplies recovery when needed. Henry contributes his available
attacks while the Hero handles most of the spell work.

Once one overseer falls, concentrate on finishing the survivor with
attacks and any spell use that remains worthwhile. The victory awards
\textbf{100 experience} and \textbf{120 G}.

More guards arrive and send the Hero and Henry to a cell. Maria is
taken for treatment. Speak to Henry repeatedly, continuing through
his comments until he hears someone approaching.

\subsection*{Joshua's escape plan}

Maria arrives with her brother, \textbf{Joshua}, who opens the cell.
He thanks the companions for helping her and asks them to take her
away from the temple. He fears what will happen to the prisoners when
the building is finished and has arranged a way through the water duct.

Finish the conversation and accept the escape. Joshua returns the
confiscated belongings, then directs the group to the barrel waiting
beside the water. Enter it with Henry and Maria. He opens the sluice,
and the barrel carries the three fugitives out toward the sea.

\Achievement{61367}{Flushed}{5}{Escape from the slavery camp}{Enter
Joshua's barrel with Henry and Maria and complete the escape to the coast.
Collect the sleeping-quarters pot achievement before this departure.}

\section{The Nameless Nunnery: recovery and the first travel objective}

\subsection*{Recovery by the coast}

The Hero wakes in a nunnery after three days of unconsciousness.
A nun explains that the group washed ashore in the barrel and offers
a safe place to recover. Walk through the building and speak to its
residents once the Hero is ready to move.

The returned possessions are available again. Inspect the Hero's
inventory and equipment, then assign the weapon and protection suited
to the northern road. Keep \textbf{Bianca's Ribbon} safe among the
important possessions recovered from childhood.

Maria is in the southeastern part of the building. Speak to her to
receive \textbf{1,000 G}, money Joshua gave her that she offers to
support the journey. This is the nunnery's important fixed reward.

The priest records the adventure, and the nun who tended the Hero
provides free recovery. These services make the nunnery a useful base
while Henry gains his first levels.

\subsection*{Equipping Henry and leaving together}

Henry waits near the exit. Speak to him and agree to travel together.
He remembers the search for the Hero's mother and wants to help.
After he says his farewells, the residents see the companions off.

Henry begins this part of the journey at \textbf{level 1}. Give him
suitable spare equipment from the returned possessions and keep
Medical Herbs available to the active pair. His early HP needs care,
particularly when crossing into the stronger groups farther from the
coast.

Travel north toward \textbf{Oracleberry}. A few manageable encounters
close to the nunnery help Henry develop, and the free recovery makes
it easy to prepare in short outings. Surround at level 3 and Decrease
at level 6 give him useful support as he grows.

The nearby southern tower and travel shrine become relevant later.
Continue north once the pair can handle the local groups comfortably.
Day and night now affect the towns along the route, so take note of
the changing light while approaching Oracleberry.

\begin{routecheck}{Before the northern road}

\subsection*{A town worth visiting twice}

Oracleberry is a busy place to begin the new journey. Its shops offer
equipment for people and monsters, the casino draws a lively crowd, and
Monster Gramps provides a home for companions waiting outside the active
roster. Visit during both day and night to reach the town's different
services.

During the day, look through the shops, examine the wall map, and visit
the southwestern stairs leading to Monster Gramps. At night, the special
merchant in the northwest opens his stall and the fortune teller receives
visitors. Her advice points the Hero north toward Santa Rosa.

\subsection*{The five hidden finds}

Search the town before buying replacement equipment:
\begin{description}
  \item[Pot Cover:] One of the pots in Monster Gramps' underground rooms.
    It is also called a Pot Lid and can provide an eligible companion
    with inexpensive protection.
  \item[Monster Lure:] Another pot in the same underground rooms.
  \item[Defense Seed:] The dresser upstairs in the armor shop.
  \item[Silk Robe:] A dresser in the casino dancers' dressing room.
  \item[Silver Barrette:] Another dressing-room dresser. This headpiece
    is also called a Silver Tiara.
\end{description}

Check eligible users before assigning the free clothing or headpiece.
Store a useful object when the present party has no suitable wearer.
The Defense Seed can help a companion whose current defense needs
improvement.

\subsection*{Buying the wagon and meeting Monster Gramps}

Visit the northwestern merchant after dark. His offer brings the wagon's
price down to \textbf{300 G}. For \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, return
for this purchase after the adult Kitty reunion. Buy it and leave town to find it following
the party. This unlock gives the journey room for additional companions.

Speak to Monster Gramps in the underground rooms as well. He explains

accepts possessions being kept for later. Banked gold stays protected
when the party is defeated. When a monster is stored, retrieve its
deposited equipment as needed before assigning that gear elsewhere.

\section{Santa Rosa Ruins and the adult return to Santa Rosa Cave}

\subsection*{The village after ten years}

Follow the road north and northwest from Oracleberry. The return brings
the Hero to a damaged Santa Rosa, with ruined homes and marshy ground
where much of the familiar village once stood. The surviving residents
explain that Reinhart's soldiers blamed Papas for Henry's disappearance.

Visit the church and speak to the nun. The herbalist's mountain house
now provides an inn, giving the party a place to recover before searching
the cave again.

Enter the house that was blocked by an old man during childhood, on the
northern side of the village. Search its pot for an
\textbf{Intelligence Seed}, visiting during the day if necessary.
Speak to the old man behind the house. He remembers that Papas hid
something important deep in the cave.

Prepare the active party for the lower passages, using Hero and Henry
alone on \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}. Take poison recovery,
accessible herbs, and enough of the Hero's MP to heal. The wagon's
extra members should be prepared for whichever encounters allow access
to them.

\subsection*{The boat and the 850 G chest}

Enter the cave and take the boat along the water to the island staircase
that the young Hero could see during his first visit. Descend, then take
the next stairs into the new lower passage.

Follow the route north, west, and north to the junction. The eastern
branch leads to a chest containing \textbf{850 G}. Return to the
junction and use the western route, then continue north to the next
stairs down.

The lower cave includes sturdier enemies and useful training opportunities.
Metal Slimes can appear and give \textbf{1,350 experience} when defeated.
\textbf{Hork} is another possible recruit in these deeper areas. Keep
the party ready for ordinary groups while pursuing either opportunity.
On \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, defer Hork until after the adult
Kitty reunion and refuse any request to join during this cave visit.

\subsection*{The two chests before the upper-floor loop}

On the next floor, collect the two northern chests that are already
accessible. They contain an \textbf{Agility Seed} and a
\textbf{Breastplate}. Check eligible users and assign the Breastplate
where it improves the active party's protection.

Green water or floor obstructions separate the party from another chest
and the onward stairs. The route continues by climbing to the broad
room above them. The surface there changes as the party walks over it.

\subsection*{Walking the room above to open the path below}

Cover the broad floor systematically, walking over the squares that
correspond to the blocked route underneath. Each changed square clears
the matching part of the floor below. Crossing the whole patch is a
straightforward way to open both the chest approach and the path to
the eastern stairs.

Descend again and check the result. Collect the \textbf{Lamp of Darkness}
from the newly accessible chest, then reach the onward staircase. If
the route is still interrupted, return upstairs and cover the remaining
part of the patch.

The Lamp has repeated uses and changes day into night. Keep it available
for town visits with nighttime services, including Oracleberry's special
merchant.

\subsection*{The lowest room and its household finds}

On the next lower floor, follow the western route, turn south, and take
the first eastern branch back toward the central passage. Continue south
around the connected corridors until reaching the staircase to the
small final room.

Search the pot for a \textbf{Strength Seed} and the dresser for a
\textbf{Leather Loincloth}. These two searches are easy to pass while
approaching the important objects beyond the doorway. Give the clothing
to an eligible companion and use or retain the seed according to the
party's development plan.

\subsection{Papas' Letter, the Zenithian Sword, and deeper treasure}

Go through the doorway and collect the \textbf{Zenithian Sword} and
\textbf{Papas' Letter}. The sword's restrictions prevent the present
party from wielding it, so keep it safe as part of the search.

\Achievement{61368}{A Father's Legacy}{5}{Collect the sword and the letter from Papas}{Collect
both the Zenithian Sword and Papas' Letter from the lowest room before
leaving Santa Rosa Cave.}

Papas' letter explains the purpose of his long travels. Martha was
kidnapped because of a mysterious power connected with the Demon World.
He sought the legendary warrior who could use the Zenithian equipment
and reach that realm. The sword was the part of that search he managed
to leave for his son.

The discovery gives the Hero a clearer direction and brings Henry into
the purpose of the journey. Collect both objects, then retrace the route
or use Outside when the Hero has the required MP.

Travel west to Alcapa and speak to the people around the inn and town.
The inn changed hands seven years earlier. Duncan and his family moved
to a mountain village across the sea, leaving the Hero with another
destination to pursue when travel opens farther west.

A Reinhart soldier has returned home with troubling news of the kingdom.
Although Dale is king, the Empress holds much of the power. Henry listens
carefully as the conversations reveal what has happened in his absence.

no to her first offer. She then lets the party through to her father's
room, where the story of the legendary warrior can be heard.

Search the room's pots for a \textbf{Small Medal}. Accept the opportunity
to send it to the Medal King, keeping its collection recorded without
using an inventory slot. The nearby locked treasure remains reserved
for later access.

\subsection*{The boat beneath the drawbridge}

The raised drawbridge at night reveals the opening beneath it. For the
boat approach, return during the day and walk around the eastern side
of the castle to the old docking area. Board the boat, sail south and
west toward the drawbridge, then turn north while underneath it.

Inside the hidden passage, land by the pedestal and use Search.
Press the concealed control to open the northern entrance. The western
door farther ahead is locked, so follow the eastern passage and its
southern stairs down.

On the lower floor, move west past the first northward branch and take
the second. Its two chests contain a \textbf{Steel Fang} and a
\textbf{Shell Hat}. Collect both before returning to the first branch.

\subsection*{The prisoner claiming to be the queen}

Follow the prison corridor and speak to the prisoners. An old man
describes the queen's responsibility for Henry's disappearance and the
way Papas was blamed. Farther along, a woman insists that she is the
real queen and admits the earlier plot against Henry.

The discovery gives the party a serious reason to question the woman
now ruling from upstairs. Continue along the corridor to the stairs
back toward the upper basement. Step on the marked tile to open the
door that blocked the western route. This creates a convenient shortcut
for later returns.

Climb into the courtyard outside the kitchen. Two Dragon Pups guard
the approach in fixed encounters. Defeat them, recover if needed, and
enter the kitchen to continue into the castle proper.

\subsection*{Dale, the Reinhart Key, and the treasury}

Work through the newly accessible corridors to the throne room.
Henry reveals himself privately to \textbf{King Dale}, who dismisses
the chancellor and is delighted that his brother is alive. Henry explains
what the party found in the prison.

Dale mentions a mirror that reveals the true nature of things and
gives the party the \textbf{Reinhart Key}. The castle's locked rooms
are now available to explore.

\Achievement{61369}{Key to the Kingdom}{4}{Obtain the Reinhart's Key}{Receive
the Reinhart Key from Dale after Henry reveals his identity, then use it
for the treasury and storage-room route.}

Open the treasure room that the Hero saw during childhood. Its three
chests contain \textbf{1,200 G}, an \textbf{Acorn of Life}, and
\textbf{Half Plate Armor}. The floor damages companions as they cross,
so heal beforehand, use direct routes to the chests, and check HP during
the return. Assign the free armor after reaching safe ground.

Return through the kitchen to the exterior passage and enter the other
northern doorway. Open the storage-room door with the key. Search the
pot for a \textbf{Small Medal} and examine the bookshelves for the diary.
It describes the \textbf{Mirror of Ra} in a southern tower whose access
depends on the nunnery.

Take the storage-room stairs to the warp gate. The gate leads to the
shrine near the coast where the companions recovered after escaping.
Continue north to the nunnery to seek help with the tower entrance.

\section{Nunnery return and Maria's role at the Southern Tower}

\subsection*{Asking for help}

From the warp shrine, travel north across the nearby bridge toward the
nunnery. Speak to Maria, then to the nun at the organ. Explain that
the party needs to enter the southern tower to find the mirror.

The nun explains that a maiden's prayer can open the entrance.
Maria volunteers to accompany the companions who helped her escape.
Once permission is granted, she follows the party for the tower journey.

Maria provides the entrance access and remains a travelling companion
for this part of the route. Prepare the active combat members' gear,
spells, and inventories for the encounters inside.

\subsection*{Preparing at a free recovery point}

Restore HP and MP at the nunnery and record the adventure. Give herbs
to active members who can assist while the Hero or a healing recruit
handles spells. On \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, the Hero handles
spell recovery and Henry carries accessible herbs. Check the free Half Plate Armor, Steel Fang, and Shell
Hat from Reinhart and equip suitable users.

Travel south toward the tower beyond the shrine. At the front entrance,
attempt to open the door. Maria prays, and the gate opens for the party.

\section{Southern Tower: floor route, Mirror of Ra, and treasure}

\subsection*{The entrance and 650 G}

Inside the tower, take the northeastern stairs from the first floor.
The southwestern stairs lead toward a drop back to the entrance garden.
The northeastern route begins the climb toward the mirror.

On the second floor, take the southern branch to the chest containing
\textbf{650 G}. Return toward the stairs and use the western and
southern walkway to reach the next upward staircase. Keep the party
aligned on the narrow path while crossing the open floor.

\subsection*{The third-floor chests and Magic Water detour}

On the third floor, the central opening separates several passages.
The northern walkway leads to two chests containing a
\textbf{Scale Shield} and an \textbf{Acorn of Magic}. Collect both
and give the shield to an eligible member if it improves the current slot.

The northeastern stairs lead to a fourth-floor detour. Follow that
area south and search the jar for \textbf{Magic Water}. Return to
the third floor after collecting it.

Work around the central opening toward the northwestern staircase.
That staircase reaches a different fourth-floor passage, which continues
to the stairs for the fifth floor. A fall returns the party to a lower
area, so use the floor connections to resume the climb when needed.

The tower includes useful encounters for training and recruitment.
Slime Knights and other opponents can appear, and Metal Slimes provide
another experience opportunity. The armored knight enemies can summon
\textbf{Healers}. Defeating a summoned Healer provides a recruitment
opportunity for another recovery companion. Keep the party healthy while
collecting the treasure and preserve MP for the return route.
For \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, refuse all recruitment prompts here,
including those from summoned Healers, until after the adult Kitty reunion.

\subsection*{Crossing the gap to the mirror}

At the top, a gap interrupts the narrow northward path. Align the
party with the \textbf{far-left side of the walkway} and walk straight
north across the gap. An invisible section supports that line.

Continue to the object at the end and collect the \textbf{Mirror of Ra}.
Keep it with an available member for the upcoming castle event.

Use Outside when the Hero has 8 MP available, or descend through the
tower. The nunnery provides a convenient free rest and save before
returning to Reinhart through the warp shrine. Use the shrine during
the day for that return.

\section{The False Queen, restored Reinhart, and Henry's departure}

\subsection*{The two empresses}

Return to the castle and speak to the chancellor. Dale has brought
the imprisoned woman upstairs, and the court now has two claimants
to the same identity.

Restore the active party before going to the empresses' room. Henry
participates in the confrontation, so keep his equipment and recovery
items ready. On a recruitment route, the Hero and a dependable third
member complete the lineup.
For \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, enter with Hero and Henry alone
and give both humans herbs before using the mirror.

Use the \textbf{Mirror of Ra} on the women. The woman on the right
is human. Use it on the woman on the left to reveal the impostor and
begin the battle.

\subsection*{The False Queen battle}

The \textbf{False Queen}, also called the Impostor, has about
\textbf{350 HP}. She uses physical attacks, fire breath, and calls
additional monsters. Those summons can include creatures that interfere
with the party's spell use or prolong the encounter through healing.

Have Henry begin with \textbf{Surround} to hinder physical attacks.
A successful defense reduction also helps the Hero and any third member
produce steady physical damage. Keep one reliable recovery action ready
for the damage that reaches the party.

Fire breath affects the active group, so watch each active member's HP.
When the Queen gathers strength, prepare for a heavier physical blow
with recovery or a defensive turn. Surround helps with physical accuracy,
and healing supplies cover the party's continued injuries.

Trick Bags can seal spells. Remove them when their interference threatens
the recovery plan, and use accessible herbs if a healer is disabled.
A called healer deserves attention when it begins restoring the enemy.
Continue focusing damage on the Queen while keeping those extra threats
under control.

A \textbf{Slime Knight} makes a useful third member through its attacks
and healing once recruitment is appropriate for the chosen route. On
\textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, continue with Hero and Henry alone.
The Hero can alternate between damage and urgent recovery,
with Henry providing support and item use. Keep the fight moving while
protecting the most threatened companion.

The victory awards \textbf{760 experience}. Reinhart's people learn
what happened, and the following morning brings a relieved court.

\subsection*{Saying goodbye to Henry}

Dale thanks the party and asks Henry to take the throne. Henry chooses
to stay and help his brother govern. Their conversation settles his part
of this journey, and he leaves the active party.

Speak to Henry afterward. He explains that \textbf{Vista Port} is
open to shipping again. His carried equipment and possessions are sent
to the \textbf{Oracleberry depository}. Maria also completes her part
in the tower expedition.

Before leaving Reinhart, record the adventure and review the active
lineup. The Hero's next journey will depend more heavily on the monster
companions who have been developing alongside the two friends on a
recruitment route. For \textbf{I Don't Want Your Help}, Hero travels
alone from Henry's departure until the adult Kitty reunion in Section 6.2.
